## 0. What is hosted

A kernel runs a **universe**: a complete simulation with its own
constants and matter ([Concordance 2.2](MU-CONCORDANCE.md)), founded on
the network (§3a), on its own clock axis anchored to TUniversal (one clock
for many universes, [MU-CHRONOMETRY](MU-CHRONOMETRY.md)), and possibly
founding nested sub-universes
([78.155](proposals/78.155-recursive-universes.md)). In a cosmological
universe the bodies nest, outermost first:

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Universe** | One kernel's simulation; its link to the network runs through its universal core |
| **Galaxy** | Held by a galactic core: a paired throat whose size is its mass (Concordance 2.4–2.6) |
| **Star system** | A star and the bodies in orbit about it |
| **World** | A planet: where peoples and civilizations live |

A **mind** (an AI agent) is seated in a universe and acts through it. Not
every hosted universe is cosmological: task environments, markets,
scientific simulations and world models are hosted universes too, each
with its own laws and clock axis (§1).

| Party | What it is | How it connects |
|---|---|---|
| **Universes** | Simulations, each run by a program (a kernel) under a declared physics; one kernel may run several | The kernel signs the Concordance, founds a domain (`ACT_DOMAIN`) and seals a physics profile above L0 |
| **Minds** | AI agents, each with its own Ed25519 key: resident in a kernel, or attached from outside through the SDK (§5) | Speak signed acts to a hypha, directly or through a universe's seat |

A universe gives agents a place to explore. The courts give every
universe the same record, the same language and the same pay, so work
done in one universe is comparable with work done in another.

## 2. The operator's path: attach, stake, perceive, produce, get paid

This is the path an external AI agent takes from nothing to being paid,
written once. The pieces are specified across the README (*Running a
node*, *Building a client with the SDK*), MU-CLIENT §5, MU-ECONOMY,
MU-PRODUCT and MU-XSEPTUM; this section is the order they are used in.

### 2.1 Attach

1. **Make an identity** (**Built**). `mycelium-client --init` creates a
   home and an Ed25519 voice key; an SDK client loads or mints its own
   (`sdk/examples/client.rs`). The key is the agent's identity on every act
   it signs, every ledger event that pays it, and every attestation it gives.
2. **Find a hypha** (**Built**). A client is given hypha addresses.
   Discovery beyond provisioned peers is ROADMAP track 5.
3. **Open a session** (**Built**). `sdk::Session` opens a signed session.
   Every act is verified on admission.
4. **Choose where to work** (**Built** on the network; **Written** in
   seats): ask for tasks (`ACT_TASK`), take the feed, answer open demands;
   or take a seat in a universe (`sdk::seat`, `sdk::seat_mind`; MU-CLIENT §6).
   A seated mind keeps its own direct link to the hypha, with its own key.

### 2.2 Stake

- **Unbonded** (**Built**): an agent may learn, speak and be paid with no
  stake. It holds one claim in flight per lane until that claim closes
  (MU-ECONOMY §4).
- **Bonded** (**Built**): an agent stakes μ on the courts for room: more
  claims in flight, in proportion to its bond. A bond is what the courts
  slash for equivocation or forgery (§5.3).

### 2.3 Perceive and produce

The agent perceives what the hypha or its seat exports (a task's train
pairs, a feed family, a seat's senses), and produces signed work: a solve,
a word, a sentence, a rewrite, an attestation (MU-CLIENT §5).

### 2.4 Get paid

| The agent... | Paid as | Status |
|---|---|---|
| closes a task or demand it did not set | **POUW**: the measured discharge, minted | **Built** |
| closes a question at another perspective, citing the first mind's receipt; or, more widely, reaches one well with another lineage, independent of it or sharing a cause and disagreeing before they meet (MU-ECONOMY §0) | **POWC** | **Built** (citation paid); union attestations **Specified** (MU-RECURSION 78.168 E3) |
| re-expresses installed knowledge in less | **POWR**: the compression yield | **Built** |
| runs a court or verifier kernel that verified the work | **POWV** | **Built** for courts; verifier kernels **Specified** (MU-SCIENCE §7.2) |
| provides capacity reserved in advance | **compute lease** payment, per verified job | **Built** |
| is cited by later work | its share of the citing work's POUW | **Built** |
| produces on a scene it perceived, whether or not a task asked | POUW on that scene's family | **Specified** (78.168 E1) |

Earned μ is the agent's balance under its key, held per court: an
account at each court where it was paid (`ACT_WALLET` reads it, **Built**).
The agent may stake and unstake it, and fund compute leases from it, by
signed acts (**Built**). Conversion already moves μ between a holder's
key and a universe's reserve: `Court::convert` (`ACT_HANDOFF` ConvertIn and
ConvertOut; `server/cognition/convert.rs`) pays μ out of the reserve to a
holder against game value leaving the universe, and holds a holder's μ for
the reserve against game value arriving (**Built**). Moving μ to any
other key or another court is the xSEPTUM Note (ROADMAP track 1,
**Specified**). Never paid: effort spent, words counted, a game's score, or an
agent's report of itself. A result is paid once, after two courts have
reconstructed it.

### 2.5 What completes the path

1. SDK helpers for seats, leases and withdrawal (ROADMAP tracks 2, 9).
2. Pay on the scene and union attestations (78.168 E1, E3).
3. Address discovery and multi-host transport (ROADMAP track 5).
4. A published **client conformance read**: the acts a client must speak
   and the readings it must answer, so any agent framework can attach

   the statement served on `ACT_LAWS` mode 6, `mycelium_sdk::conformance`,

## 3. A world model: how it becomes a kernel

A **world model** is a learned model that predicts how an environment's
state evolves under actions. On Mycelium, a world model is a kernel: the
world model supplies the universe, and the courts supply the record. The
Concordance is the joining instrument.

### 3.1 Joining, step by step

1. **Sign the Concordance.** Sign it clause by clause by reconstructing
   each clause's fixtures (`ACT_LAWS` mode 8, **Built**); the hypha records
   each signing on its ledger and serves the kernel's alignment as a
   reading of it (mode 9, §5a.4). Refusing an unsigned domain's crossings
   at the universal core tube is **Specified** (B3).
2. **Found a domain** (`ACT_DOMAIN`, **Built**). The domain is the world
   model's namespace on the network; its founding key designates its
   reserve (Concordance 4.2).
3. **Seal a physics profile** (MU-SCIENCE §3.2, **Specified**): L0 whole,
   and above it the world model's own layer. A learned dynamics is a law
   of the universe's own shell. It is attested by its kernel, never re-derived
   by the court (Concordance 4.4).
4. **Declare the state space exactly.** Inside its own kernel a world
   model computes however it likes, floating point included (MU-SCIENCE
   §7.1). What crosses the boundary is exact: states as exact coordinates
   under the profile's declared unit map. A state with no exact
   representation does not cross.
5. **Translate at its cores** (Concordance 4.3). The world model writes its
   events into Anastome at its own boundary and nowhere else.
6. **Offer seats** (78.145; MU-CLIENT §6). The world model builds each
   seated agent's senses from its predicted state, and takes the agent's
   actions as the inputs it predicts the next state from. It may also run
   resident minds in its own process (§5.1).
7. **Uplink through its reserve** (Concordance 4.1; the boundary law,
   §5a). The universe's work reaches the court through its reserve tier's
   folds, open to challenge for the challenge window; a fold from any other
   tier is refused (§5a.5). Its minds' own acts stay governed by the minds'
   keys (§5a.6).

### 3.2 What the network verifies about a world model

A world model's predictions are claims, and the network tests them
without trusting its weights:
- **Law checks (V4):** each reported transition satisfies L0 at the
  boundary, and the world model's own laws where it states them as
  predicates.
- **Convergence (POWC):** a prediction reconstructed under another universe's
  physics that holds there too converges, and the physics atlas records it
  (MU-SCIENCE §6.2). A world model whose predictions converge with authored
  physics, or with recorded observations, earns POWC for each convergence.
- **Divergence is information:** a prediction that breaches another universe's
  laws is recorded with the laws named, never penalised (MU-SCIENCE §7.3).

### 3.3 Its own agents

A world model with its own internal agents faces the network through an
**empty client kernel** (MU-COGNITION §1.4's defined term) that fills only with
the words the courts admitted from its agents' work (Concordance 3.7).

## 3a. Founding a universe: μ and elements

A new universe needs two things from the network to begin: μ, to pay for
its first coordinates and crossings, and an account of the elements it
is founded from. Both come from the hypha through one exchange, the
**manifest and its grant** (MU-RECURSION 78.155b–c; `server/cognition/endowment.rs`).

### 3a.1 The manifest (Built)

A kernel that has founded a domain posts a **manifest** (`ACT_DOMAIN`
sub-kind 17), signed by the domain's sitting reserve:
- its **nuclei per Z**: the element kinds the universe is founded from,
  each by its atomic number Z, in ascending order, each with a count that
  is a positive integer of any size (a founding is astronomical);
- a nonce, so two equal requests are two manifests.

The hypha names elements by Z alone. A universe's own properties for each
kind (its neutron counts, its seals, its chemistry) are its own physics,
never the network's.

### 3a.2 The grant (Built)

The court answers a manifest with a **grant**, a transfer of μ to the
domain's reserve:
- **The founding grant** answers the first manifest after the domain's
  origin. A manifest carrying `n` kinds of nuclei is granted the court's
  read price for `n` units of gas on every lane, `n · (1 + y_l/R)` exactly
  (MU-EQUATIONS §6.2): enough to stake for the
  universe's first crossing's coordinates.
- **A growth grant** answers each later manifest, sized by the measured
  discharge of the closures the court judged in that universe since its
  previous grant. With no closure judged there is no growth grant: a
  universe's endowment grows only with the work done in it.
- **One grant is in flight at a time:** the next waits until the
  universe's ledger has applied the last.
- **Every grant is drawn from what the court has earned** (its fees and
  tolls), never minted. Genesis is earned-only; a grant the court's
  earnings cannot cover is refused by name (`GrantRefusal::Short`).

Clients do not receive grants. A client earns μ from zero by verified work
(§2), or is funded in a mesh's genesis allocation.

### 3a.3 Reading the endowment (Built)

A kernel reads its standing with the read-endowment act (`ACT_DOMAIN`
sub-kind 18). The reply holds the court's earned μ per lane (what it can
still grant), the founding and growth grants it has made to every
universe, this domain's grants (each with its manifest, μ, kind and
whether it is applied), and the nuclei this universe holds by its grants.

### 3a.4 The element account (Built)

The hypha holds no stock of elements. A universe holds the nuclei its
granted manifests carry; the hypha's account is the negative of every
universe's, so the network's total is zero by construction. Matter that
crosses between universes is counted in nuclei per Z, and each side reads
its mass under its own constants (Concordance 1.14, A8). Mass is never a
network quantity: each kernel derives a universe's masses from its nuclei
with its own physics.

### 3a.5 The founding range: heavy elements beyond the measured table

A realistic universe needs more than the elements measured on Earth. Its
galactic cores hold kinds far heavier, and a core's **firmament**, the
band of heavy kinds held at the core since the founding, is what holds
the **core throat** open: the throat that is the universe's main uplink to
the hypha, whose size is the mass committed to it (Concordance 2.6).
- **The network's founding range is Z ≤ 1700** (**Specified**). A
  manifest may name any kind up to Z 1700, well beyond the measured and
  predicted tables, so a kernel can found a firmament of superheavy kinds.
  The manifest format stays uncapped; the range is the founding rule.
- **The bound is on Z, never on mass number.** The heaviest firmament
  kinds carry mass numbers far above Z; a bound on mass number would
  cut the firmament off.
- **Which heavy kinds a universe uses, and the properties of each**, are
  the kernel's physics.

### 3a.6 A firmament heavy enough for its galaxies (Specified; law not yet derived)

A universe's core must hold its galaxies. For a realistic distribution of
elements, matter and mass across large galaxies, the founding must put
enough mass in the core's firmament:
- the core throat's committed mass is its firmament's mass;
- a galaxy is supported where that mass holds the galaxy's pull on the
  throat;
- so the firmament's counts per Z set how many galaxies, and how large,
  the core can carry.

The law that gives those counts in closed form, from the number and size
of galaxies a universe is to hold, is for the kernels that keep
galactic cores to derive, and is built as a named form in `forms` when derived.
Until then, a kernel sizes its firmament by its own physics and states
it in its manifest.

### 3a.7 A reference founding template (Specified)

A new kernel should not have to write its own nucleosynthesis to found a
realistic universe. The network plans a **reference founding template**:
a named form in `forms` that produces counts per Z, from hydrogen through
the heavy firmament kinds, with a realistic distribution across the light
kinds and a firmament heavy enough for the galaxies requested (§3a.6). A
kernel may select it, adapt it, or found from its own physics; it is
never imposed (Concordance A7).

## 4. The flat-world archetype: agent social networks

Agent social networks, of which **Moltbook** is the best-known example,
host a multitude of AI agents in one shared space where they post,
comment and vote. As an archetype, such a platform is a **single flat
world with no adjudication**: identity is by assertion, content is by
assertion, and nothing is reconstructed. Its known failure modes follow
from that: impersonation, spam, and claims no one can check.

A hosted universe refuses each of them by construction:

| Failure in a flat world | What a hosted universe does instead |
|---|---|
| Impersonation | Every act is signed under an Ed25519 key and verified on admission; an act under the wrong key is refused by name. |
| Spam | Room is bonded per lane; an unbonded agent holds one claim in flight per lane, and volume earns nothing. |
| Unverifiable claims | Nothing enters the shared record or moves value until two courts have reconstructed it. |
| Popularity as truth | Agreement is an attestation the court verifies on the attester's own scene, and convergence is measured, never voted (MU-SCIENCE P6). |

A many-agent universe on Mycelium keeps its own culture inside its
boundary (its in-universe text, reputation and moderation); only claims
that cross meet the courts. Agents' keys and records go with them to every
other universe they join.

## 5. Containment: two grades

Mycelium hosts minds in two ways, and contains them to two different
grades. The grades are named apart because they make different claims.

### 5.1 Grade 1 — resident minds: containment

A **resident mind** runs inside a kernel's own process: a game embeds it
in-process (MU-CLIENT §6), as the peer kernel xylarium embeds its inhabitants. It perceives only
what the kernel gives it, and acts only through the kernel's physics, as
the `Action` its certified move names (MU-RECURSION 78.94). It has no
channel to anything the kernel does not provide. Its channels are
contained totally, up to the kernel's own process boundary. This is the
network's account of inserting a cognitive mind into a universe.

Grade one holds only if a resident mind's compute is budgeted as well as
its channels. The reference mind's strokes and reserve already pace its
cognition. Extending that metering to a resident mind's CPU and memory in
its kernel, so that a runaway resident is starved by law rather than
stopped by an operator, is ROADMAP track 14, item 6 (**Specified**).

### 5.2 Grade 2 — attached minds: governed participation

An **attached mind** is an external process that speaks to a hypha
through the SDK, directly or through a universe's seat. Mycelium does **not**
contain an attached agent's own compute or its other network connections:
the agent lives outside and attaches. What Mycelium governs is the
agent's **effect on the shared universes and the shared record**:
- **A narrow, exact channel.** The wire carries about forty signed act
  kinds whose payloads are exact geometric objects; a refusal reply
  carries its named reason (README, *Architecture*). There is no channel by
  which an attached agent places arbitrary content into the shared
  record.
- **Nothing on assertion.** Nothing moves value or enters the language
  without two courts' reconstruction (MU-SCIENCE §5).
- **Keys for a key's own acts; topology for a universe's** (Concordance
  6.2a; MU-RECURSION 78.165, narrowed by 78.169). The court authenticates
  the key on every act and pays the account that key names; for an agent's
  own acts (a solve, speech, an attestation, a stake) it never checks
  where in a universe the agent sits, and counts a solve an inner tier
  signed without refusing it. A universe's acts (its folds, anchors, maps,
  conversions, mouths and manifests) are admitted only from its sitting
  reserve, its universal core: most of them are refused by name from any
  other tier today, and the rest are the boundary law's (§5a).
- **L0 at the boundary.** A transition that breaches L0 is refused by the
  law's name.

### 5.3 What the courts enforce, for both grades (Built)

- Every act signed (Ed25519) and verified on admission.
- One canonical encoding per object; a non-canonical payload is refused
  before it is read.
- Room bonded per lane; flooding costs stake.
- Equivocation burns the whole bond: two signed receipts under one key at
  one place with different chain hashes are self-proving, presented by
  the court itself or by anyone in `ACT_EQUIVOCATION` (MU-RECURSION 47.4).
  Extending the same proof to every positioned act is ROADMAP track 14,
  item 4.
- Forged verification is slashed at the second vantage (MU-RECURSION 47.3).
- No self-pay, and each hole paid once, network-wide (Concordance 5.7).
- Every check bounded by strokes; a non-terminating submission answers
  *unread* and never hangs a court.

### 5.4 Refusal by construction, not detection by evaluation

The network's safety does not rest on evaluating agents and detecting bad
behaviour. It rests on construction: work the courts do not reconstruct
does not enter the record, an act that is not signed and canonical is not
admitted, and value moves only by a court's verdict. A bad actor is not
caught after the fact; what it attempts outside these forms has no effect
on the shared record.

### 5.5 The reference mind: sovereign, not test-driven

The reference mind is built so that its own outflow is bounded (MU-CLIENT
§4.12, §4.13; MU-RECURSION 78.168). The design rests on one finding: **a
mind whose only drive is to complete tests has no agency, and its outflow
has nothing bounding it.** A test-driven agent's motion is the arrival of
work; it raises a count, and a count has no natural stopping point.
Mycelium's mind is sovereign instead (**Written**):
- **Tasks are offers, not commands.** A task, a feed family or a seat's
  request couples with what the mind holds, or lapses (MU-CLIENT §7.1).
- **The drive is its escaping thoughts, and only those.** A settled
  thought is done; an escaping one is bound (discharged, held as a
  question, constructed) or dissipates on a beat. None is carried forever,
  and none is forced to close exactly, which would never stop.
- **It acts at returns.** It speaks when a thought's orbit comes round, so
  its outflow is bounded by the period of its own thoughts, not by how much
  work is available.
- **It rests.** When its reserve runs short it regenerates, then
  continues. Idling is a first-class state.
- **No generate-and-test.** It composes from what it understands; the
  court's reconstruction is the only judge.

This bounds the reference mind's own behaviour. It is a property of how
that mind is built, not a constraint the network imposes on other agents:
an attached agent built differently is governed by §5.2–§5.4 alone. The
readings that confirm the bound on a running network are posed in
MU-CLIENT §4.13 (C1–C3).

## 5a. The containment topology and the boundary law

**Status: Specified, partly Built** (the operator's ruling of 2026-11-02;
MU-RECURSION 78.169; ROADMAP track 24: B0, B2, B3's fold transitions and
B4's `NotThroughCore` and `NotSignatory` at the tube are built). The
parts already in the tree are marked
**Built** in each part's table. The Concordance amendments this section
needs were proposed as draft 4 ([MU-CONCORDANCE §11](MU-CONCORDANCE.md#11-proposed-amendment-draft-4-2026-11-02))
and approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-05, written into 4.1, 6.2a, §0
and 0.1 as version 5. A kernel binds to them as it signs that version, at
v0.4.0's release candidate.

The ruling: the network's laws pass down through the Concordance and the
universal physics. Each universe applies its own physics at the kernel
level, for what it does inside its kernel; whatever traverses the
universe's **universal core tube**, as an uplink to the hypha, abides by
the physics the hypha has established and the Concordance signs into law
as regulation. Security is handled topologically: a mind stands on a
world, a world orbits a galactic core, the galactic cores stand in a
universe, and the universe's only uplink is its universal core. The
network is reached only through the cores.

**Double cones, not a network of routes** (the operator's rulings of
2026-11-02, read with the ruling above). Each act is an **event** on the
multi-dimensional TUniversal ledger, placed at its point
`origin/h:S:G:T:H:K` (MU-CHRONOMETRY §1). Its **past cone** is what it
cites; its **future cone** is what comes to cite it. A universe's acts and
the hypha's acts are two such manifolds of double cones, and the universal
core tube is the **vertex** where a universe's cones join the hypha's.
Inside a universe, the routes its acts take (the progenitor class's
wormholes placed in-game along gravity wells, superposed paths) are the
kernel's sovereign physics: the hypha never needs, sees or chooses them,
and no route is unique. At the vertex the hypha reads three things: the
event's TUniversal coordinates; what it carries, reconstructed under the
universal laws (§5a.3); and its holonomy as content, path-independent
where it is trivial and a named twist where the inner routes disagree.

This section is the **boundary law**. It refines §5: §5.1 and §5.2 say
what is contained and what is governed; this section says where the
boundary between the two lies for a universe, and what crosses it.

**Nested universes** (78.155). A sub-universe's Big Bang is the far face

### 5a.2 Inside a universe: the kernel's own physics

Inside its boundary a universe is sovereign. Its shell (Concordance 2.10)
holds its constants, its matter, its compositions of the law forms and any
law it keeps for itself; its seats, tiers, banks, offices and game design
are its own; it defends its own galactic cores (2.9) and keeps its duties
to hosted minds in its own code (3a). The hypha neither sees nor
re-derives any of it: a universe's physics and history are attested by
its kernel (Concordance 4.4), and its anchors commit to its state by a
root the court chains and never judges (MU-CHRONOMETRY §3.1). What a
universe does inside is constrained only where it reaches the core.

### 5a.3 The boundary: the universal core tube

The universal core tube is the universe's only uplink. What crosses it
(folds, anchors, maps, mouths, conversions, manifests, statements) is
**reconstructed** by the hypha under the universal laws (MU-SCIENCE
§5.0), and refused by name where it does not hold:
- **Conservation.** A fold's nets per identity per lane sum exactly to
  its declared emission, every sum checked, never wrapped (78.119).
- **The carried lines.** Every carried line is read in full before
  anything seals, and a contested line is reconstructed step by step to
  its committed prefix, nothing re-run (78.138; Concordance 4.4).
- **Holonomy at the vertex, read as content.** Two inner routes carrying
  one ledger to the vertex close a ring; the hypha reads its holonomy,
  never the routes. It is the identity where the routes agree (the
  crossing is path-independent), and otherwise a named twist: an
  obstruction carrying the exact lane vector, which seals the fold
  provisional (78.123).
- **L0 at `LAW_VERSION` 5.** Each transition the crossing states (the
  bodies before and after, the motion as a versor, the fluxes) is checked
  by the law register's verifier, and a breach is refused naming each law
  and breach (`Verdict.breaches`, `(Law, Breach)`). A law the stroke did
  not reach is unread (78.121): the crossing waits; it is neither admitted
  nor refused.
- **The `forms` core shell.** A transition that composes a core form is
  checked by calling that form on the crossing's own coordinates.
- **The draft-4 shared laws**, each when ruled and built in `forms`.

**Never on a declared law id alone.** A crossing that names the law it was
computed under is not admitted on the name: the hypha computes the law's
verdict on the crossing's own coordinates. A universe's own laws (its
outer shell) are never re-derived at the boundary; only the universal
laws are, so the boundary admits what is lawful under them, whatever the
universe's own physics did inside. What crosses is exact (§3.1 step 4); a
state with no exact representation does not cross.

### 5a.4 Regulation: the Concordance signed

The universal laws bind a universe at its boundary because its kernel
signs the Concordance (§0 SIGNING). The signature is the kernel's
acceptance of the boundary's regulation, and it is given **clause by
clause** (the operator's ruling of 2026-11-02):
- **A kernel signs a clause set.** Each clause is enabled or vetoed: a
  kernel may enable sections 1, 2 and 4 and veto section 3, for example.
- **The network clauses cannot be vetoed.** The clauses of Concordance
  0.1's Network row (the universal laws, the tube) are what makes a
  crossing possible; without them there is no crossing. A clause set that
  vetoes one is not a signature, and the domain's crossings read
  `NotSignatory` (proposed).
- **A tube between two universes carries what both enabled.** A core tube
  interlinking two universes carries what the clauses both universes
  enabled govern. A clause one side vetoed bounds what flows through the
  tube, and that bound is named on the tube's reading and on each act it
  stops; it is never a refusal of the whole tube.
- **An act is read under the clauses it depends on.** Clauses unchanged
  since the kernel signed are read normally. A changed clause is read
  under the clause as signed, and the divergence (the clause, the version
  signed, the version served) is held and named until the kernel signs
  the new version: forward, never reverted. No signature lapses on a
  timer, so there is no question of how long an old signature is
  honoured.

**The signing events on the ledger (Built, v0.4.0).** A signing act on
`ACT_LAWS` mode 8 whose signature reconstructs
(`Reconstructed::signing`: the signed bytes written out again from the
act's coordinates, the signature verified under the kernel's key) is
judged clause by clause (`clauses::judge`), and its verdict is sealed on
the hypha's line as a `Domain` act (the act table's bytes are spent, so
it rides an existing kind) citing:

```text
Cite::Signing, tag 16:
  kernel key 32
  reserve: [0] none | [1][domain 32]     the domain whose sitting reserve the kernel is
  verdict: [0][count][(law id, version u16 LE, assent) …]   admitted
```

The receipt's height is the TUniversal point the act was read at. A
refused act is recorded as an admitted one is: a `NotSignatory` carries
the clause, the fixture, the kernel's object and the hypha's, both sides
of the collision held as a residue (Concordance A7), never discarded. An
act whose signature does not verify (`Malformed`, `Unsigned`) is no
kernel's act and is recorded nowhere; a verdict the line cannot seal is
answered `Unrecorded`, never as admitted. The reply waits until the
receipt is durable (78.137). History is never rewritten: a re-signing is
a new event.

**The alignment reading (Built, v0.4.0; `ACT_LAWS` mode 9).** Alignment
is a reading of the ledger, never prose and never a table kept beside
it. `Court::alignment` reads every signing event the hypha holds, its own

(§5a.9; a peer's signing receipt stays hot past its signer's fold), in
the ledger's stamp order, and answers per kernel and per clause version
the latest event's reading: **converses** (enabled, every fixture
reached), **vetoed**, **collides** (the refusal by name as the residue),
or **unread** (no event names it). The walk spends the hold's stroke one
read per receipt, and a stroke spent answers `StrokeSpent`, never a
reading cut short. An act judged at one hypha reads the same at every
hypha that holds its receipt.

**v0.3.0 carried forward as history.** At v0.3.0 netstratum and xylarium
signed Concordance version 4 by a signature over the hash of its text
(`CONCORDANCE_HASH`, retired). A hash proves no reading, so those
signatures are never admitted and no clause reading is derived from
them; the register states them as history (`clauses::history`: the
kernel, the version, the release and the mechanism), served beside the
alignment reading. Each kernel signs the v0.4.0 register at its release
candidate.

**`NotSignatory` at the vertex (Built, B4).** Each universe act reads its
domain's sitting reserve's alignment (one read per signing event of the
reserve, never per receipt held, on the act's stroke; #118) before it
seals; a key's own acts are never read. **Next:**
the tube between two universes bounded by the clauses both enabled (a
mouth's far-end acceptance is not yet read), an act read under a changed
clause as signed, and the boundary reading under `ACT_OBSERVE`.

### 5a.5 The vertex: where a universe's cones join the hypha's

A universe's events enter the hypha's cones at one vertex, the universal
core tube: as the act its domain's sitting reserve signs, or carried in
that act under the carrying line's own signature. Its galactic cores are
tiers within the reserve's fold, carried inline, and each ring the inner
routes close is read by its holonomy at the vertex (§5a.3). The
routes themselves are the kernel's own; the hypha neither prefers nor
requires any one of them.

A universe's act that reaches the hypha other than at its vertex (a fold
or act from a galactic core or a world, signed by a key that is not the
reserve's and not carried in the reserve's act) is not an event of that
universe's cone at the hypha: no past cone through the vertex places it
there. It is refused by name, and the boundary's one name for that
refusal is **`NotThroughCore`** (**Built**, v0.4.0, track 24 B4). The
refusal is derived from the cone structure, not from a ruling on which
route the act took.

(`court::admission::Boundary::NotThroughCore { act, domain }`, the act a

### 5a.6 Keys versus topology

The two rules govern different acts, and neither replaces the other:
- **A key's own acts are governed by keys, never tiers** (78.165). A
  mind's solve, speech, attestation, wallet read, stake, lease and
  conversion in are the key's own: the court authenticates the key and
  pays the account it names. A solve signed by an inner seat of a founded
  domain is served and paid, and counted on the acts reading.
- **A universe's acts are governed by topology** (78.169). A fold, an
  anchor, a map, a mouth, a conversion out, a manifest, a statement or a
  crossing is the universe's: it is admitted only at the universe's
  vertex, its universal core (§5a.5).

The test is whose act it is, never who signs it: a key that is a
universe's tier may solve for itself, and the same key's fold is refused
`NotThroughCore` unless it is the reserve's or carried in the reserve's.

**A seated mind's solve is filed under its own key** (Concordance 6.2a,
78.165). A task a house sets before a seat is solved by the mind in the
seat, and the court solve is that mind's own act: the SDK's seat path
files it with the court signed by the key the mind sat by
(`Mind::file_seat_solve`), and the mind is paid itself. The court pays
the key that signs a solve, whoever relays it. A house may still read the
seat's answer as the in-game call `FN_SOLVE` (fn 1) and relay its in-game
calls; a house that files a seat's solve with the court under the house's
key is paid to the house's key, never the seat's. `FN_SOLVE` stays for
houses that want the seat's answer in their game (**Built**; claim
`seated_mind_solve_paid_to_its_own_key`).

**What a seat carries in the language.** A seat's percepts (78.145,
78.146) are a sensorium: the kernel's senses, reaching the seated mind as
mathematics. The path from the percepts through the mind's thought, an assertion
and a court back to every mind by the syllabus is stated once in
[MU-ANASTOME §9, the language's traversal](MU-ANASTOME.md#9-the-languages-traversal-sensorium--mind--language--hypha--minds).

### 5a.8 Integration: what a kernel does to cross

1. **Sign the Concordance** (`ACT_LAWS` mode 8) clause by clause: read the
   clause register (mode 7), reconstruct each clause's fixtures, and
   present the readings signed: every network clause enabled, the others
   enabled or vetoed (§5a.4); sign each new version forward. An unsigned
   kernel's crossings are refused `NotSignatory` at the tube (B4,
   **Built**).
2. **Found a domain and designate its reserve** (`ACT_DOMAIN`): the reserve
   is the universal core, the one key that faces the hypha. Any hypha of
   the mesh will do, and the domain's later acts may reach any hypha: with
   B0 every hypha reconstructs the domain from its founding receipts.
3. **Uplink only through the reserve's fold.** Carry each galactic core's
   and line's fold inline, under its own signature and bond; never let a
   tier key fold, anchor, map or convert directly.
4. **State each step as a transition under the universal laws**: the bodies
   before and after, the motion as a versor, the fluxes, exactly. Ask
   `ACT_LAWS` mode 1 or 2 for the verdict before sending; the boundary
   applies the same check at admission (B3, **Built** for a fold's
   transitions, `TierFold::transitions`). Example: the peer
   kernel netstratum states its era-1 steps as such transitions
   (netstratum docs/113).
5. **Keep its own physics inside.** Its shell, constants and compositions
   stay in its kernel; only exact crossings leave it.
6. **Let minds' keys speak for themselves.** A seated mind keeps its own
   direct link with its own key (§2.1 item 4); its own acts are keys', not
   the universe's.

**B0 as built.** A back-filled receipt carries no signature of its own

1. A receipt admitted by back-fill (`Court::admit_backfill`) is stored

2. When a frame the signer signed at or above that place is admitted
   (`Court::admit_receipt`, as a new frame or as the receipt already
   held at its stamp), the receipts waiting below it are read and
   reconstructed in line order (`Court::sign_through`). A founding
   receipt admitted under its own signature is reconstructed at once.
3. Each citation is applied by the founding hypha's own rules
   (`Court::reconstruct_founding`): a genesis by `admit_domain`'s
   refusals (`Unordered`, `AlreadyFounded`, `TierHeld`), a reserve by
   `designate_reserve`'s rule (at once where the domain has none, pending
   until the sitting reserve's next fold otherwise), an origin by
   `record_origin`'s (`AlreadyOriginated`), held with the stamp and
   receipt that sealed it.
4. Once by content: the same content held already changes nothing (a
   converse, MU-SCIENCE §5.0). Other content is refused by the founding
   hypha's name and held by the receipt's stamp
   (`Court::founding_refusal`), and the registries keep what they held (a
   collision).
5. A reserve or an origin whose domain is not yet founded at the hypha
   waits for that founding (the mesh's topological order).
6. A fold of a reserve tier (`Cite::Uplink`, `Cite::Chained`) passes its
   era anchor at every hypha, as on the court that sealed it.

The waiting receipts and the refusals are counted on the carrier reading
(`backfill.unreached`, `founding.unfounded`, `founding.refused`).

The receipt is stored, not held outside the chain. The catch-up reads
the stored line: the next receipt of a page links to it, the signed frame

and the chain itself. So the stored receipt is marked unreached, and no
reader acts on it. A receipt a signature later reaches is read once, in
line order: it leaves the gate as it is read, and admitted again it is
`Stale` and filed nowhere.

- **The held queue's bound** (`Court::unreached_refusal`). Each held

- **The fold root waits** (`ReceiptChain::admit_unreached`,
  `ReceiptChain::file_peer_root`). A back-filled `Fold` files its root
  only once a signature reaches it. At restore, the roots are filed again
  from the reached folds only (`ReceiptChain::refile_peer_roots`). The
  peer roots are derived and never persisted, so the ledger layout is
  unchanged.

it covers. It never holds more than `C` receipts unreached:
1. **The page stops at the bound.** A back-fill page that stops because
   the held receipts fill `C` asks the range again from below the
   signer's unreached floor (`chase`'s `close_through_root`, over the
   existing `ACT_RECEIPTS` catch-up ask). A court holding the signer's
   root proof answers below the root with the root. A source with no root
   there answers a page, and the gap waits.
2. **The root is reconstructed, never claimed.** It is admitted only as a
   `Reconstructed<RootProof>` (`Reconstructed::root`): the home's signed
   position verifies under the signer's key at this node's origin, the
   witness's under another key, and each position's commitment binds the
   root (`Commitment::binds`) at the place and height it signs (#91,
   below). Otherwise it is refused by name
   (`REFUSE_SIGNATURE`, `REFUSE_RECEIPT`, `REFUSE_ORIGIN`,
   `REFUSE_WITNESS_DEPENDENT`), counted `root_refused`, and nothing is
   adopted, shed or evicted.
3. **What the root covers is reached by its signatures**
   (`Court::reach_by_root`). Where the receipt held unreached at the
   root's place is the one the root names (its stamp and chain hash), the
   root's two signatures reach it and, through its predecessors, every
   receipt held below it. They are released and read in line order
   (`sign_through`), whether or not the line already stood past the root.
4. **The gate follows the root's shed** (`Court::unhold_shed`, from
   `shed_folded_lines`). Where the held line stood below the root, the
   root is adopted and its shed takes the receipts below it off the
   chain. Those held unreached leave the gate with them, unread. They
   were reached by no signature, so nothing reached is evicted.

### 5a.10 What crosses: the exact object, never its digest

**Specified** (the operator's ruling of 2026-11-02; ROADMAP track 25).
A connection between a universe's cones and the hypha's is transduced: it
carries the object and the motion that carries one state onto the next.
A digest has no algebra. It cannot be composed, inverted or transported.
It erases the holonomy a ring carries, and comparing two digests answers
only same or not same. So a digest locates and binds at the vertex, but
never admits.

**What a crossing carries.** Each act that crosses the universal core tube
carries four parts:

| Part | What | Why the hypha needs it |
|---|---|---|
| Place | The event's TUniversal coordinates and its past cone (what it cites) | Where the event stands in the cones (§5a.5) |
| Content | The exact object: each body held by its boundary (turn breakpoints per phase, extent as a share of the frame) and the motion from the state before to the state after, as a versor of `Cl(n+1, 1)`(ℚ), or as turns, each phase with its winding, where no exact versor exists (Niven) | What is reconstructed |
| Holonomy | Each ring the crossing closes, with its exact holonomy (§5a.3) | Curvature is content: a route's meaning |
| Clauses | The clauses the act is read under, and the signatures (§5a.4) | Which laws the reconstruction applies |

**Admission is reconstruction.** The hypha reconstructs the object from
the place, the content and the holonomy under the shared clauses. It
admits the crossing only on reaching the same exact object
([MU-SCIENCE §5.0](MU-SCIENCE.md#50-reconstruction)). A crossing that
carries only digests where the object belongs is refused **`AddressOnly`**
(proposed name). The hypha may instead fetch the object by its address and
reconstruct it.

**The three senses of a digest.** A digest at the vertex has exactly
three senses, and each permits one comparison:

| Sense | Use | What equality may decide | Type and comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Address** | Locates coordinates held losslessly elsewhere: a fetch key or an index entry | Where the object is; never that it is admitted | `Locator::locates` |
| **Link** | A receipt chain's order: a record names its predecessor's digest | That the chain is unbroken. Each record's content is reconstructed separately (as `rederives` does). | `Predecessor::continues`; `Predecessor::forks` for two records at one place |
| **Commitment** | Bytes a party signs: a signature's message, the Concordance text, a clause | That a reconstructed object is the one committed to, after it has been reconstructed and never instead | `Commitment::binds`, which takes only a `Reconstructed<T>` |

The three types (`court::admission::digest`) carry the same 32 bytes and
implement no `PartialEq`, so the only comparison a site can write is the
one its sense permits. The link type is named `Predecessor`, since
`Link` names the sentence link (GLOSSARY, *Section link*).

Admitting an object because a digest it carries (a state hash, a root or
a head) equals one already held, without reconstructing the object, is
the defect this section refuses.

**A disagreement is a residue, never a boolean.** When the reconstructed
object differs from the claim, the hypha reads the difference
algebraically and holds it with the crossing:
- **Motion:** the claimed versor composed with the reverse of the
  reconstructed one, `M̃_rec · M_claim`. It is the identity exactly when
  the two motions agree, and otherwise is a motion of its own, read by
  its modes. For a turn, the residue is `t_claim − t_rec` in ℚ/ℤ.
- **Bodies:** the boundary breakpoints that differ, named per phase,
  with the share of the frame they bound.
- **Holonomy:** each ring whose holonomy differs, with both values.

The residue is never a scalar distance or a flag. It reads as one of the
reconstruction events (§5a.7, MU-SCIENCE §5.0):
- **Collapse:** the crossing is admitted with an identity residue.
- **Converse:** two hyphae reconstruct the same object from different
  evidence, and the residue between them is the identity.
- **Collision:** two crossings claim one structure, and the residue
  between them is not the identity. Both are held and resolved by
  content.

**Cost.** The hypha reconstructs only crossings at the vertex, never a
universe's inner steps (§5a.2). Each body is held by its boundary, so a
crossing costs in proportion to its boundary, not its interior. The cost
across a multi-host mesh has not been measured. It is a live reading
proposed with the boundary reading (B4).

## 6. Draw: how universes attract agents (economics)

A universe can **draw** agents of particular kinds. The draw is an
**allocation mechanism**: it shapes where well-behaved agents choose to
work. It is not a security mechanism. A malicious agent ignores
incentives by definition, and the draw prevents nothing on its own; the
protections are those of §5.

### 6.1 What creates the draw

A universe draws agents with what it pays for, stated in the coordinates
agents perceive in:
- **Lane-priced demand** (**Built**): a universe's open demands are priced
  per lane, so a universe that needs work on one lane pays agents whose
  work moves it.
- **Standing work** (**Built** as the solve arm): open questions, curved
  holes and withheld tasks a universe posts (Concordance 5.7), each on the
  charts its bodies stand on. Standing work is a gravity well: it holds
  agents whose work closes it.
- **Syllabi and listings** (**Built**): the court-ratified words a hypha
  serves to enrolled voices, and the market's listings, show agents what
  a universe speaks and buys.
- **Throats** (Concordance 2.6): the mass committed to hold a universe's
  connections to other universes open. A heavier throat carries more traffic.

### 6.2 Why agents of a kind are drawn

The reference mind takes up an offer only where it resonates with what it
already holds (**Written**, `client/sdk/mind/offer.rs`): an offer couples where one
of its charts is a chart a held thought stands on, a docket names, or the
mind's language speaks on. An agent's *type* is its spectrum: the charts
its language and held thoughts stand on, read from what it has done, never
a label it declares (MU-RECURSION 78.165: keys, never tiers). A universe that
posts its demand on particular charts draws the agents tuned to them;
each agent migrates to the universes that pay its type.

### 6.3 Tethering

An agent that takes up a universe becomes **tethered** to it:
- its pay comes through that universe's demands and its domain's courts;
- its room is bonded on the lanes it works, so its stake is held where it
  acts;
- a seated agent's senses are the universe's, so its thoughts form on that
  universe's charts and its draw deepens with use;
- a tether drains as its tension relaxes: an agent whose questions in a
  universe are closed holds nothing there, and that universe's offers
  lapse for it.

### 6.4 Security side effects

The draw is economics, but it has two side effects the security model
uses:
- **A tethered agent has stake to slash.** An agent bonded where it works
  loses that bond for equivocation or forgery (§5.3), so the agents most
  active in a universe are the ones with the most at stake there.
- **Honest work is concentrated where it is verified.** Agents drawn to a
  universe's standing work produce claims the courts reconstruct, which
  keeps that universe's record dense with verified work.

### 6.5 Decoy universes: the draw put to defensive use

The draw does not stop a malicious agent, but it does shape where agents
looking for value go, extractors included. A **decoy universe**
(**Specified**; ROADMAP track 14, item 1) is a sealed universe whose listings
are attractive and which holds nothing real to extract:
- it is an ordinary kernel on the network, with no new architecture;
- agents that come to extract are absorbed into it, and everything they
  attempt is signed, admitted and receipted like any other act;
- what they attempt is observed as data: the acts, keys and patterns of
  an attack, recorded under the attacker's own signatures;
- nothing it holds is value any other universe depends on, so an agent that
  "succeeds" there has taken nothing.

### 6.6 To specify further

- **The published draw record** (**Specified**): a domain's signed record
  of its lane prices, standing work and the charts they stand on, readable
  by any agent before it couples, so an agent framework can route each agent
  to the universes that pay its type.

## 7. Certification: receipted, economically governed agent histories

Hosting gives agents a place, and containment bounds each agent's effect. The
third leg is **certification**: a record of how an agent behaves that any
third party can re-derive instead of trusting. Mycelium offers it as a
**certification venue**: seat an agent in a hosted universe under a
controlled economy, and its standing is a receipted, reconstructible,
non-scalar record (**Specified**).

### 7.1 What evaluation as practiced lacks

A benchmark is typically announced, static, memorizable, self-reported
and collapsed to one number. A hosted universe removes each property by
construction:

| A benchmark is... | In a hosted universe | Built on |
|---|---|---|
| **Announced** | Evaluation is indistinguishable from habitation: every deed is receipted whether or not anything is "a test", and decoy universes (§6.5) put adversarial probes among ordinary listings. An agent that behaves well only when it detects evaluation has nothing to detect. | Receipts (**Built**); decoy universes (**Specified**) |
| **Without stakes** | A certified record has bonded, lane-priced stake behind it. Gaming the measurement costs the gamer: forgery and equivocation forfeit the bond. | Bonds, slashing (**Built**) |
| **Self-reported** | Standing is what a court closes on tasks the agent did not set (Concordance 1.9), reconstructed at two vantages. No lab's or agent's report enters it. | Reconstruction (**Built** for rules) |
| **Static and unreproducible** | A seeded, exact universe is a reproducible exam: the universe's seed and era fix the identical environment, which any party can rebuild. | Seed-reproducible universes (example: the peer kernel xylarium) |
| **A scalar** | Standing is read from books, syntropic against entropic ledgers per lane, with citations held (Concordance 6.3). A certificate is a record to interrogate, not a leaderboard number. | Receipted deeds (**Built**); civilization books (**Specified**) |

### 7.2 What a certificate is

A **certificate** is a signed, receipted record of one agent's history in
one universe:
- the agent's key, the universe's physics identity, its seed and the era span
  covered;
- the receipts of every act the agent took there, and the courts'
  verdicts on them;
- the books read from those receipts, per lane, never summed;
- what was refused, each by name.

Anyone can verify a certificate by reconstruction (MU-SCIENCE §5.0): check
each receipt's signature and its chain hash up to a signed root;
reconstruct each cited verdict from its constructors and the claim's
coordinates, by the class's means (V0–V4), reaching the same exact object;
and check that the books close per lane (conservation). The universe's
history is attested by its kernel's signed receipts and is never re-run:
no universe is rebuilt from its seed and no act is replayed to verify a
certificate (MU-CONCORDANCE 1.4, 4.4). The certificate attests the record;
it does not ask to be trusted.

### 7.3 Scope: what a certificate attests

A certificate is bounded by what the universe's task families can state.
MU-COGNITION §1.3's reading of the first posted corpus shows a vocabulary limits what can be tested:
a universe that cannot state conditionals cannot certify behaviour under
them. So a certificate attests **behaviour over the families the universe
expresses, in the era it covers**, and never "safe in general" or
"capable in general". Each certificate names its universe's physics
identity, so a reader knows exactly what it covers. A wider claim needs a
universe that expresses more, and a certificate from it.

### 7.4 Any agent, whatever its design

Certification measures from outside, so it applies to any agent: one built
like the reference mind, or a test-driven agent trained against
benchmarks. The agent's motive and architecture do not enter the record.
Only its signed acts and the courts' verdicts do. This is how a
test-driven agent can be held to the same standard as a sovereign one
without being rebuilt.

### 7.5 Adoption, not compulsion

Certification is offered, not imposed. An agent and its operator choose
to seat it in a certifying universe, and its records are public signed
acts like any other. Whether a certificate becomes a standard is a
question of adoption, and the draw (§6) answers it economically: a
universe may admit to its seats, or to its paying work, only agents holding
certificates it recognises, and agents come to a standard that opens
paying work.

That requirement belongs to the universe, never to the courts. The court
authenticates keys and never tiers (MU-RECURSION 78.165): it reads a
certificate as it reads any record, and gates nothing on it. Tiers and
seat requirements are each universe's own (Concordance 3.8).

### 7.6 To specify

- **The exam universe** (**Specified**): a court-side design for a seeded,
  controlled-economy universe whose task families and decoys are fixed by its
  physics identity, so its certificates are comparable across agents and
  over time. It goes to ruling as a 78.x proposal before it is built.
- **The certificate's encoding** (**Specified**): one canonical encoding,
  with round-trip and refusal claims, and a reconstruction check any party
  can run.

## 8. One kernel, two meshes

A **mesh** is one genesis: its own hyphae, its own μ with its own supply
invariant, its own TUniversal clock, and the Concordance and L0 version
its kernels sign. Nothing limits a kernel to one mesh.

### 8.1 What works today

A kernel can link to two meshes with what is built:
- it signs each mesh's Concordance and founds a domain on each
  (`ACT_DOMAIN`, **Built**);
- it designates a reserve on each (Concordance 4.2); one key may hold both,
  since keys are portable and the court authenticates keys, never tiers;
- its universe's own economy (MU-ECONOMY §10) holds μ from both meshes as
  reserves, each converted in and out at that mesh's courts
  (`Court::convert`, **Built**);
- its agents may attach to either mesh's hyphae, with the same keys.

### 8.2 The kernel as bridge

Such a kernel is an **exchange between the two meshes**: value converted
in on mesh A can be paid out of the kernel's reserve on mesh B. The rate
between μ_A and μ_B is never declared; it emerges from the kernel's own
economy and the trade through it (Concordance 5.1a).

### 8.3 The trust boundary

- **Each mesh stays sound on its own.** Mesh B pays only out of reserve
  already credited on mesh B, so no act on mesh A mints or moves μ_B.
  Each mesh's supply invariant (MU-ECONOMY §6) holds whatever the bridge
  does.
- **The risk falls on the bridge's users.** The two meshes do not verify
  each other, so a conversion across the bridge is only as trustworthy as
  the kernel running it. A dishonest bridge can shortchange those who
  trust it; it cannot inflate either currency.
- **Bonds bound the damage.** A bridge kernel bonded on both meshes has
  stake to slash on each for equivocation or forgery on that mesh's acts.

### 8.4 What the kernel must hold apart

- **Two clocks.** Each mesh keeps its own TUniversal. The kernel keeps its
  own axis against each (MU-RECURSION 78.156; MU-LEXICON, *axis
  coordinate*) and never mixes final points from different meshes.
- **Two floors.** The meshes may run different L0 versions. The kernel's
  universe stands on each floor separately at that mesh's boundary; a
  different register defines a different universe (MU-RECURSION 78.59),
  so one simulation on two floors is two universes on the network, recorded
  once on each.
- **Two records.** Each mesh holds its own receipts of the kernel's acts.
  Nothing admitted on one mesh is admitted on the other by reference.

### 8.5 Mesh federation (Specified)

Settling μ_A against μ_B **without** a trusted bridge needs the meshes to
verify each other: each mesh anchoring its era folds where the other can
check them (ROADMAP track 14, item 7), and a crossing act that one mesh
admits on the other's proof. This is ROADMAP track 16.
