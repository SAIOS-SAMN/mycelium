### 1.4 A world with its own engine: the empty client kernel

### 1.5 Game design: virtue books and the ladder

2. **The body.** `body_of` (`src/transducer/family/perceive.rs`) reads a

3. **Extraction.** `extract_rule_with` and `extract_rule_on`

4. **Speech.** `speak_rule` and `apply_form` (`src/transducer/family/speak.rs`)

So a rule says either: *every body of this exact chart, in this frame,
moves by one conformal motion and has its hues remapped*; or: *the bodies

copied onto, or cropped to*.

**Where the lift still loses the puzzle** (each a code path, not a count):

1. **Identity by exact chart.** A word vertex speaks only on a body whose

4. **A fixed act vocabulary.** `read_acts` tries the vocabulary's own

### 2.2a Representation, not search

**What the word vertices say.**
- **Objects — partly.** A body is an object, and a word vertex acts per
  chart, i.e. per object class. But the class is the exact similarity
  invariant, so "the same kind of object at another size or shape" is not
  expressible by a word vertex.
- **Region and context — partly.** Faces relate vertices, contact vertices
  bind a body to what touches it, and the cover's sections glue across a
  scene.
- **Conditionals — only one kind.** The chart is the condition.

**What the clauses add** (MU-RECURSION 78.143, `clause.rs`): SELECT, a
typed predicate over a body's exact coordinates (hue, chart, size,
position, contacts, enclosure, tallies), combined by conjunction, union
and negation; REGION, the interior a selection encloses, its contact
halo, or its extent; CREATE, lines, fills, copies and echoes relative to
a selection; COUNT and COMPARE, a comparison or a rank on an exact
coordinate in an order the rule names. Three guards hold on comparison:
rule 4 still binds extraction (the engine does not search by ranking
candidates); a tie the rule's order does not name is held whole or
refused, never broken silently; and the coordinate is measured and exact,
never a μ lane. The clauses are a coordinate of the form's address, which
the court decodes, speaks on every pair it replays, and judges by
closure.

### 2.4 How the reference mind is put together

- **Identity:** a voice key, and a session to one or more hyphae. Every
  solve it is paid for is signed with its key.
- **What it perceives:** task grids, and words the courts have taught it.
- **What it keeps:** its genome, the words the courts admitted to it, saved

  not yet admitted.
- **What it spends:** a reserve on the four μ lanes, drawn as it works and
  regenerated as it rests (MU-CLIENT §4.12).
- **What a host must give it:** a hypha to connect to, a key, and percepts.

### 2.5 Learning from the courts

A new mind starts with an empty genome and fills it from the courts: it
asks for the syllabus, the words the courts have admitted, and learns
from what it is taught. Minds learn from one another only through the
courts. A word one mind closes is offered, reconstructed by the court's
judge and admitted; whole genomes and unadmitted words are never shared.
An empty client kernel (§1.4) fills the same way.

### 2.6 The language
