### 1.2a Representation, not search (netstratum's finding, tested against the form language)

**What the form language can already say.**
- **Objects — partly.** A body (a maximal hue-blind connected region) IS an
  object, and a rule acts per CHART, i.e. per object class: "move every object
  of class X" is expressible. But the class is the EXACT similarity invariant,
  so "the same kind of object at another size or shape" is not.
- **Region / context — partly.** Faces relate vertices, contact vertices bind a
  body to what touches it, and the cover's sections glue across a scene. So
  adjacency exists, but there is no "the region bounded by …" and no "inside".
- **Conditionals — only one kind.** The chart IS the condition: a member acts
  exactly on bodies of its chart. There is no predicate on any other coordinate
  (hue, size, position, contact), and no else.
- **Counts — refused, until 1.10.** A body carries its cell count (`shell`) as
  a coordinate, but comparing counts, majority and rank were refused (rule 2;
  MU-RECURSION.md:4256, 4993). Concordance 1.10 rules that a coding standard,
  restored for minds.
- **Arithmetic and creation — none.** There are no new cells, no lines or fills
  beyond `Unfolded` flood-fill, and no counted output size.

**So the failure is mostly what a rule can SAY**, not what is perceived. The
perception choices compound it: hue-blind merging, the background dropped, and
the pair index used as an axis (§1.2, items 2 and 7). So does one defect (§1.3,
frame-axis motors). But even a perfect percept could not state most ARC-AGI-2
rules in this vocabulary. More search over it finds nothing, and the doctrine
said so (55.8).

**A minimal extension, kept inside the laws.** Grow the COURT'S form language,
so that the court and every kernel speak one rule language:
1. **SELECT**: a typed predicate over a body's own coordinates. The chart up to
   moduli (a class, not an exact shape), the gauge (its hues), the shell, contact
   (what it touches) and frame position. A Motion or Hue step then applies to
   the selection, and selection is the conditional. Equality, membership and —
   under Concordance 1.10 — order (rank, extremum) in an order the rule names.
2. **REGION**: a body's contact neighbourhood and the cells its boundary
   encloses, read from the blade (dual sphere and support), and made addressable
   as a selection target.
3. **CREATE**: the existing `Unfolded` and `Demand::Source`, keyed relative to a
   SELECTED body instead of to training cell sites, so that they cross to unseen
   pairs.
4. **COUNT and COMPARE** (ruled 2026-10-08, Concordance 1.10). Exact counts,
   and rank, majority and "largest" as predicates over a measured exact
   coordinate, in an order the rule names, ties included, judged by closure on
   the withheld pairs. Three guards hold:
   - rule 4 still binds EXTRACTION (the engine does not search by ranking
     candidates);
   - a tie the rule's order does not name holds both or is refused, never broken
     silently;
   - the coordinate is measured and exact, never a μ lane.

**Where it sits.** It is NOT additive. It changes the form language and the
judge's replay of it, so it is a court change with LAYOUT and WIRE impact. It
follows 78.142 as 78.143 (whose `Step` gains Select, Region, Create, Count and
Compare as the court learns them), before curved holes (the ruled order).
