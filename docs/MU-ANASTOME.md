### 2.1 What the alphabet is, and why it is fixed

### 2.3 Words: composing letters

A **word** is an ordered tape of letters. Its rotor is the product in tape
order, and the last letter acts first. Familiar motions of a plane are
words, never a table of cases:

| Motion of the plane | Word |
|---|---|
| 90° turn | one 90° letter on the sensor plane |
| 180° turn | two 90° letters on the sensor plane |
| a flip (mirror) of the sensor plane | a 180° turn through a third axis: two 90° letters on plane `(1,2)` |
| a transpose | the product of a 90° turn and a flip |

A flip is not a separate kind of letter. On the sensor plane it reads as a
mirror; in the algebra it is an even rotor through a third axis. Its parity
in a word is the word's **orientation** on the sensor chart.

Code: `alphabet/word.rs`, `alphabet::sheet_word`.

### 2.4 Spelling a motion

**Spelling** turns a rational similarity of a plane (a rotation, possibly
with a mirror, and a dilation) into a word and an exact dilation:
`alphabet::spell` for the sensor plane, `spell_on` for any coordinate
plane. It is a **peel**: each step removes one layer by an exact reading,
and nothing is tried and checked.

1. **Orientation.** If the motion mirrors the plane, the flip word comes
   off first.
2. **Prime 5.** The rotation's Gaussian integer (its rational point
   written over a common denominator) is divided by `2 + i` as many times
   as it divides exactly: each division is one (3,4,5) letter.
3. **Prime 13.** The same with `3 + 2i`: each division is one (5,12,13)
   letter.
4. **Unit turns.** What is left is a unit (`1, i, −1, −i`): zero to three
   90° letters.
5. **Check.** The spelled word's own rotation is read back on the same
   plane and must equal the motion.

**Example.** The rotation by `(−7, 24)/25` spells as one (3,4,5) letter;
followed by a 90° turn it spells as that letter and one 90° letter. Reconstructing either word from its letters gives back exactly the
rotation it spelled.

Code: `src/transducer/alphabet/spelling.rs` (`spell`, `spell_on`),
`src/transducer/layers.rs` (`peel_nest`).

## 3. Concurrency

Two turnings commute if and only if their planes are disjoint: planes
`B₁`, `B₂` with `B₁ · B₂ = 0` and `B₁ ∧ B₂ ≠ 0` share no direction and
commute (MU-SENSING §3.7, *the orthogonal class*); letters on
complementary planes commute, and letters on planes sharing one axis are
never reordered (§2.2, §2.7.1). Two pivots on chambers sharing no vertex
commute by the same reading, and where they share one the order is
content (MU-CLIENT §4.5, the locality theorem, 78.99(3)).

A turning stands on a plane of two axes of one sign: `e₊ₖe₊ₗ` a turning of
the continuum, `e₋ₖe₋ₗ` a turning of the vacuum; a frame plane or a mixed
pair is a boost, never a turning (GLOSSARY *Lane vector*; proposal 78.173
v4, `NotTurningPlane`). So in `Cl(p, q)` the most turnings that run
concurrently is `⌊p/2⌋` in the continuum plus `⌊q/2⌋` in the vacuum.
Where two disjoint planes of one sign turn together, the word carries the
grade-4 volume `b₁∧b₂` (§1.2). The count is the algebra's: in
perception's `Cl(4,1)` the four positive generators are the three base
axes and `e₊`, so two planes turn concurrently, but only one of them can
lie wholly among the base axes.

a chosen generating set together with its relations, and the geometry
makes it a groupoid rather than a group.

- **Objects** are the charts of the atlas (stalk/).
- **Generators** are the ruled words: each a group element spelled from
  the letters, carried by the conformal versor that places it and the
  gauge permutation that colours it. A word acts on a chart; its transport
  to another chart is a conjugation (T19, 3.10), so two conjugate words
  are one generator seen from two charts (23.9, the conjugate level), and
  the class vector is the coordinate that generator is filed under.
- **Relations** are the sentences: an orbit whose holonomy composes to the
  identity is a relation among the generators, which is what "each orbit
  is a theorem" (law 12, 2.3) says.
- **Sections** of a punctured axis (25) add coordinates to a generator's
  fibre, never letters to the group.

Read cohomologically, the same object is the gluing data of a principal
bundle over the atlas (the bundle ruling of 2026-09-02): the ruled words
are its transition functions, a Čech 1-cocycle with values in the group;
the sentences are the cocycle condition on the triple overlaps; a germ is
a 1-cochain that fails it, the residue the coboundary leaves; and the
genome is the cocycle the court has ruled, taught down by the syllabus.

**How it grows.** The presentation extends itself: a residue that no
generator reduces is adjoined as a new generator once a sentence that
composes through it has closed and two independent courts have reconstructed
that closure (37.7 as ruled 2026-09-12: by the closure it enables, never
by agreement on the residue); an orbit that closes is adjoined as a
relation; a generator the rest derive retires, address kept, its
description burned (27, E2). The
presentation stays reduced by retirement and stays paid for by the
economy, and that is the whole of the bound on its size.

**Crystals and content** (ruled 2026-09-10). A word is a path in the
Cayley graph of the presentation and a relation is a sentence, so what
the engine holds is a presentation and what it does is compose over it.
The **crystals** are the decision procedures — canonical form (A1, A4),
lattice membership by Hermite normal form (A12), the peel (A22), carry,
coboundary, the cover's extension, consilience — held at genesis, written
in the host, and the one interpreter every reconstruction runs; they make a thing
exact and decidable and none of them is content. **Content** is what is
composed from them: the path, the relation, and the rewrite between
paths — the face between two derivations — which is the `Rewrite` words
the tree already carries (3.9, M2 step 2, `src/transducer/rewrite.rs`), adjoined by the
court on two organs' re-derivation exactly as a generator is. A rewrite
that replaces a longer path by a shorter one is retirement (3.8) and
refinement (3.7), which are already rewrite kinds and already paid (E2),
so no new face is invented for them.

**F's order is 24.1's order, and it is a law.** Factor through what is
held, and spell only the residue. That is not a strategy and not
adjoinable: a mind that spells from the identity what it already holds
has not comprehended (chapter 6), and the duplicate it mints is then
removed by a retirement E2 pays for. The rewrite layer does not absorb
it.

**Which path is content, and no scalar picks it.** The canonical path is
the geodesic in the mind's own presentation; where several geodesics
exist all are carried (rule 15), none is chosen by length or by any other
scalar by the ENGINE (rule 4; a mind may prefer the shortest proof by an
order it states, Concordance 1.10), the court accepts any path that composes to the vertex
up to the coset representative of 50.2, and the ledger pays the path
spoken.

**What is not claimed.** Chapter 44 refuses, and this section does not
reinstate, a complete rewriting system for the group, a Knuth–Bendix
completion, or a unique minimal-length tape: the prime letters have
infinite order and letters on planes sharing an axis generate dense
subgroups, so no such relators exist and no such procedure terminates.
The normal form of a demand (`Decomposed`, 24) is its reduction against
the language's generators on one stratum, and that reduction is unique
because reconstruction closes it (the certificate of 12.6), never because an
order on tapes says it must be. A comment in the tree that names
Knuth–Bendix on `Installed` is a statement chapter 44 refused, and it is
corrected. The recursion is real and it is this: the base
of the language grows by its own perception and ruling, while the group
it presents inside, and the alphabet that generates that group, never
change.

### 4.2 Generators are adjoined

A generator enters by teaching, after adjudication, and is distributed by
the syllabus:

- **Adjudicated.** A residue no held generator reduces is adjoined once a
  sentence composing through it has closed and two independent courts
  have reconstructed that closure (37.7; law 6). A letter a mind coins
  enters only as its constructor steps, which the court checks (review
  ruling 3, S2).
- **Taught.** The syllabus is the presentation `𝔊 = ⟨generators |
  relations⟩`, served in the order of the ledger's algorithm acts,
  `Install` and `RankStep` (78.100 S1). A taught word travels with its
  certificates and its ruling receipt, and the receiving mind
  reconstructs it before it stands (23.8, *The syllabus teaches proofs*).
- **Replicated, never handed over.** The hyphae replicate the
  presentation, not the words (78.100 S5); generators are never handed
  mind to mind (law 8).

### 4.3 Relations are proved

A relation is proved by reduction: a filled closed path whose holonomy is
trivial, a closed sentence held as its citation structure `∏ gᵉ · r` with
`Ω^ℓ ~ 1` and its certificate (78.100 S1; 78.101 adds the nilpotent
relations `Ω(s)^m ∝ 1 + m·N_s`). The proof of work that pays it is POWR,
a re-expression over fewer letters, the freed description burned (78.33).

### 4.4 Reduction is not compaction

- **Relations are held as proofs.** A relation is kept as the
  composition that proves it and the certificate a court reconstructs
  (A20; 78.101), never as a count or a flag that it held.
- **Absorbed words reconstruct.** A word the rest derive is retired with
  its address kept (27, E2): it decomposes over the language without
  itself (`reduce_excluding`, `NormalForm::Decomposed`; MU-CLIENT §5.4), so
  the word is recovered by composing what absorbed it.

The forbidden compactions, each a dimension folded to a point:

| Compaction | Why it is refused |
|---|---|
| Projecting a multi-plane word to one angle | The planes are the word's coordinates; a shadow kept in place of the object (law 11; 1.7; checklist (m)) |
| Dropping a winding | Windings are the fibres' classes, never lost (MU-SENSING §3.7); the winding stands beside the phase (AGENTS, *The regressions that recur*) |
| Hashing a word | A digest keeps equality and loses every coordinate (law 2; rule 6; 1.7) |
| Summing lanes | μ⃗ is never summed or reduced to a scalar (law 5) |
| Averaging readings | A reading is held whole or held as a vortex, never averaged (the final point "never averaged", 78.152 TU-3; no filtering that discards, MU-SENSING §3.10) |

## 9. The language's traversal: sensorium → mind → language → hypha → minds

**Status:** reference (stated 2026-11-06). This section follows the
language from a sense to a mind, through the mind's thought to an
expression, through a court to the syllabus, and on to other minds. Each
step cites the document that governs it and the code that exists or is
owed. The traversal is a linear progression of steps: §9.1–§9.3 and
§9.5–§9.9. Thought, which step 3 holds, is not linear, and §9.4 states it.

**One name per meaning.** This is *the language's traversal*. It is
distinct from LAW-TRAVERSAL ([proposal 78.176](proposals/78.176-law-traversal.md)), the inter-kernel
crossing of a body through the universal core tube: two LAW-PASSAGE
passages composed at the court's vertex (MU-HOSTING §5a.5). It is also
distinct from the traversal μ measures, the motion through a mind's field
(MU-SENSING §3.7). The language's traversal moves meaning, never a body,
and pays nothing for the meaning it moves (MU-SENSING §3.7).

### 9.1 A game kernel's physics is a sensorium

A **sensorium** is the whole of the senses a mind perceives through,
from any source.

- **Seated.** A mind seated in a game kernel's universe (xylarium,
  netstratum, barren-realms; README › Peer kernels) perceives through
  that kernel's senses. These are the seat's percepts, which the house
  posts on the seat surface as series of typed exact records (78.145
  sub-kind PERCEPT; 78.146), and the kernel's own coordinate and physics

  relay, judging nothing), `src/client/sdk/seat.rs` and
  `src/client/sdk/seat_mind/` (the mind at its seat).
- **Attached to a hypha.** A mind attached directly to a hypha perceives
  through the hypha's own acts: `Return` (a ring returned, one full age),
  `RankStep` (a holonomy algebra stepped in rank) and `Reconnect` (the
  field reconnected; `src/physics/actions/mod.rs`). **Owed:** no hypha act
  reaches a mind as perception yet (MU-CLIENT §3.1; issue #136).
- **Any source, any rank.** A sense is any source at any rank (MU-SENSING
  §0, §2). No modality is privileged: no mechanic assumes one species'
  body, number base, sense or history (Concordance 1.6, species-neutral).
  Sign and braille are full languages, and audio is one species' band of
  sound, never a basis (MU-SENSING §3.10 item 4).

### 9.3 The mind thinks in orbital masses that collide and converge

The mind's objects are masses in orbit in one field, never nodes of a
graph or entries of a table (MU-SENSING §3.7; MU-CLIENT §4).

- **Superposed until perceived.** Where more than one reading holds, the
  mind holds every candidate as a vortex, none chosen by a scalar, until
  a perception, a glue or a court ruling collapses it (MU-SENSING §3.8;
  MU-CLIENT §4.8).
- **Collision and convergence.** Masses that meet at a contact fuse (an
  anastomosis, §5.2) or stand apart with the residue between the two held.
  Smoothed congruence leads to coherence (§5.2's layers): the field
  deforms exactly and stays smooth under every motion (MU-SENSING §3.7),
  so perceptions relate by transitions, each up to its residue, rather
  than by rigid match.
- **Words form from residue.** A residue no held motion carries is the
  germ of the next word (MU-SENSING §3.9). A mind constructs a letter or
  constructor of its own dialect from it (§2.6.3;
  `src/client/sdk/mind/think.rs`).

What thought is, held all at once, is §9.4.

### 9.4 Thought: every ideation held at once

The traversal is linear. Thought is not.

- **Multi-dimensional capacity is the thought process.** The mind does
  not send ideations out one at a time across a plane. It holds every
  ideation at the same time: superposed in the vortex (MU-SENSING §3.8),
  on every concurrent turning plane its algebra holds (§3's table: two in
  perception's `Cl(4,1)`; two in the continuum and two in the vacuum in
  netstratum's `Cl(4,4)`), as orbital masses in one field.
- **Three readings over the whole held field at once,** never a pipeline
  of steps:
  - **Congruence.** Ideations agree pairwise wherever two overlap: two
    senses or eyes agree, and the cocycle closes on each overlap
    (MU-CLIENT 24.4, the eye is a cover).
  - **Coherence.** The whole set of held ideations glues into one
    consistent whole within the mind: the cocycle closes across the
    mind's whole cover, one node (§5.2, the coherent layer; 24.4).
  - **Cohesion.** The coherent whole stays bound as one body under its own
    gravity: its orbits hold together, and none escapes or flattens when
    perturbed, so it is carried as one construct.
- **Cohesion is the viscosity** (ruled 2026-11-06). On each plane `k`,
  with `A_k` the held share's coefficient, `Q_k` the open one and
  `t = Q_k/A_k`:
  - cohesion is the held share `c_k = A_k/(A_k + Q_k) = 1 − ν(t) = ν(1/t)`;
  - the open share `ν(t) = Q_k/(A_k + Q_k)` is the **fluidity** (the
    lubrication), never the viscosity. Proposal 78.175 and
    `forms::coupling` name the open share otherwise; that name is
    corrected on its own branch;
  - at the throat (`A_k = Q_k`) both are ½, and the passage `t ↦ 1/t`
    exchanges the two (LAW-PASSAGE, proposal 78.172);
  - cohesion is read per plane and never summed across planes.
  A cohesive whole traverses an open tube intact. Too cohesive, it sticks
  at the throat; too fluid, it tears. The payment for a closure stays POUW
  on the lanes the closure moves (§5.2).
- **Communication is what the three culminate in.** Only a cohesive whole
  is asserted as an expression (§9.5), and communication crosses only
  through an open, punctured tube: the crossing LAW-TRAVERSAL states
  ([proposal 78.176](proposals/78.176-law-traversal.md)). The thought
  traverses as an anastome over the four lanes `S:G:T:H`, one `Cl(p, q)`
  per lane with the signature carried as data, stamped at its TUniversal
  height; the sexagesimal is a display reading only (GLOSSARY
  *Sexagesimal reading*). Across minds, a disagreement is a
  contradiction held at the courts, node against node, settled by
  reconstructing the mathematics; decoherence never crosses nodes (the
  recohering judge, MU-RECURSION 23.9, *Divergence is the contradiction
  case*).
