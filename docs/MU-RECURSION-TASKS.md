# MU-RECURSION task list

Derived from `MU-RECURSION.md` chapter 41 (landing order) and the audits of
2026-09-05. One task per commit; the commit message carries the task id, the
test names, and the `test result` line of one foreground
`cargo test --release`. The auditor reproduces the cold-boot trace before
the next task lands. Status as of the custody handover of 2026-09-07: the
carrier surgery was inherited in flight and uncommitted on `aa959c4`
(`basis` and `pending` are `Keyed`, `book_index`/`basis_index` deleted) and
red at 357/3. The G-set closes it: G1–G5 are done and the tree
is green at 365 lib, 13 alpha_plan, 6 g_cga, 1 bins under `-D warnings`.
G6 is a ruling, not a patch. Then the split (Stage 8), then the Cut B+1
soak rows C1–C3, which gate any word being ruled.

**The standing reading of this phase.** Five of the G-set's items changed
behaviour on paths **no test touched** — who an escrow pays, what a torsion
is worth, which word a chart condenses, what a held loop germinates from,
which word a slot names. The suite was byte-identical across three of them.
Under the beta's distributed premise this is the load-bearing risk: a
choice by list position is deterministic on one laptop and divergent across
a mesh, so the same mathematics ruled on two continents produces two
languages, and nothing in the suite would say so. Every remaining sweep row
carries "and a test per site" for that reason.

Legend: **done** verified on the tree; **partial** landed with a named gap;
**open** not started. "DoD" is the definition of done. "Rule" cites the
coding-standards rule most likely to be broken while doing the task.
