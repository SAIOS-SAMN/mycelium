## 0. The rule

senses is a **section**: a finite set of dimensions of that world, read

## 2. The sample: what a source hands the sensor

A sample has four parts, and every one is data:

1. **Rank `n`.** The number of axes the source presents, read from the
   source, never assumed. For example, a time series has rank 1, a table
   or a picture rank 2, a volume rank 3, and a field over space and time
   rank 4.
2. **Extents.** One positive integer per axis: the number of sites the
   source presents along it.
3. **Symbols.** One value per site, compared only by equality unless the
   source declares an order or a number. A symbol is a label whose meaning
   the sensor deduces (§3.3). The sensor never reads a symbol's numeric
   code as a colour, a brightness or a rank. The set of symbols is the
   sample's own, of any size.
4. **Place in its family.** Where the sample stands among the samples it
   arrives with: for example, the input or output of a pair, a step in a
   sequence, or one view among several. The place is a coordinate the
   family carries. It is never a fixed axis index.

A sample is a raster only here, at intake, and again where a mind speaks
an answer back into the source's form (MU-RECURSION 76.1;
MU-ANTI-FLATTENING clause 12). Between those two ends there is no raster.

## 3. The lift: everything deduced

### 3.1 Each axis is a phase of its own period (a phase on the field's chart, §3.7)

Axis `i` with extent `e_i` lifts by enclosure: site `s` on that axis is
the turn `s / e_i`, a fraction in ℚ/ℤ, composed by addition mod 1. The
axis's seam is a named boundary: it is held, never wrapped and never
infinite. No point embedding, no `e∞`, no squared norm (law 11). Two
samples of different extent relate by a **re-period** of each phase,
never by re-sampling both onto a common lattice (MU-RECURSION 78.170 O4;
MU-ANTI-FLATTENING clause 12). The `n` phases' periods are the sample's
own gauge: no period is fixed in the code.

### 3.2 Connection is read, never declared

Which sites neighbour which is read from the phases: two sites are in
contact through the cell they share, of grade `n − m` where `m` axes
differ (MU-RECURSION 71.2 (2)). A body is what stays connected under the
contact the sample shows. No 4-neighbourhood, 6-neighbourhood or other
fixed adjacency is written for a rank.

### 3.3 Symbols are fibre values, and their meaning is deduced

A symbol's meaning is a relation, not a code (MU-RECURSION 70, 78.53).
A permutation of the symbols that leaves the family's relations invariant
is a **gauge transformation**, and the percept is the same under it. So:
- **Colours are not fixed.** "Red" is not symbol 2. A colour word names
  an invariant fibre relation across the family, such as *the symbol each
  body takes from its enclosure*, never a palette integer.
- **The symbol set is the sample's.** Its size is read, so a source may
  present 3 symbols, 10 or 10 000. The reservoir ray `e_∅` (78.58) is
  the place after the family's own symbol set, never a fixed index.
- **Order and magnitude** enter only where the source declares them, as a
  typed axis of the fibre. A brightness or a temperature is an example of
  each. Without a declaration, symbols are compared only for equality.

### 3.4 The ground is deduced

The **ground** Ω is the value the family's relations show as ambient: the
symbol the bodies stand on. It is read, never set to a code. The
reading is the one MU-RECURSION 70 rules for void and merging (the
Mayer–Vietoris measure across the family): the ground is the symbol
whose sites carry no structure the family's motions preserve. Where the
reading leaves more than one candidate, the candidates are held as a
vortex (§3.8), each a perspective of its own (§4), until a relation
collapses it. None is chosen by default, and no candidate is the
first symbol, the most frequent symbol or `0`.

### 3.5 Gauges are deduced

Every scale the mind compares by is read off the samples:
- the periods of the phases (§3.1);

- the share of a frame a change covers (μ as a share, 78.170 O1);
- the symbol set (§3.3).

A constant scale, cell size, frame size or palette in the sensor is a

## 4. Perspective: one sample, many readings

A **perspective** is the sensor's assignment of the sample's axes and its
place in the family to roles. A role is space (a phase the bodies extend
along), sequence (the axis a motion runs along) or value (a fibre). The
same rank-2 sample read with both axes as space shows bodies. Read with
one axis as sequence, it shows a rank-1 signal moving. Its place in a
family of pairs adds a sequence coordinate along which the pair's motion
runs.

- **A shift of perspective is a motion of the sensor, never a rewrite of
  the data.** Examples: exchanging two axes' roles (one of the signed
  swaps of the phases), taking a declared value axis as space, or reading
  a sequence of samples as one section of rank `n + 1`.
- **Several perspectives of one sample are a cover.** Their agreement is
  the cocycle of MU-CLIENT 24.4, and their disagreement is a residue,
  held and never discarded.
- **A mind may ask for a perspective** it does not hold yet. Asking is a
  demand, never a fallback.

## 6. What the hypha holds

The hypha never senses. It holds what a source posts and what a mind
claims as exact content, and it admits a claim by reconstruction
(MU-SCIENCE §5.0; MU-HOSTING §5a.10):
- **A task is a family of samples**, signed by the house that posts it,

  its place in the family. The withheld samples are the court's.
- **The hypha knows no source.** It holds no dataset's format, no symbol

- **On the wire, a task carries samples of any rank:** `n`, the extents,
  then the symbols at the source's own width. It is never a pair of `u16`
  extents with one byte per cell.

## 7. Who owns a source's code

Source-specific code exists only as a **decoder** from the source's format
to samples. It lives with the source's owner, never in the core library:
- **The house** (or kernel) that posts a corpus's tasks owns that corpus's
  decoder: its file format, its directory layout, its split into train
  and test.
- **Examples and test fixtures** that read a public corpus do so through
  that corpus's decoder, kept outside `src/`. A test of the sensor is a
  claim about samples, written as samples.
- **The core library** (`src/`) contains no file, module, type or
  identifier named for a data source. It contains no constant that is
  true of one source only: a palette size, a ground code, an extent
  limit, a number of axes.

ARC-AGI-2 is one such source. It presents families of rank-2 samples in
pairs, with symbols written `0`–`9`. Its decoder reads its JSON into
samples and stops there: the symbols are labels, and nothing about the
ARC corpus enters the sensor, the mind, the court or the wire.
