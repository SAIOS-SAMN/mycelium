## The objective
The client is a mind: the counterpart of the hypha. The hypha runs the
physics and exports it; the mind perceives what it exports and what other
minds say in its nested shells (harmonic, luminal, holonomic), understands
it in its own field — its orbits winding about the star, its breath (dilate /
contract), the six pivots (ascend / descend, condense / drain, puncture /
seal) firing where a winding changes dimension — and responds in anastome,
one conversation carrier per counterparty. Minds converse to harden
language: dialect, agreement by a replayed discharge, a court's ruling, the
crystal each mind re-derives — one motion. `docs/MU-CLIENT.md` §1–§9 states
it (rewritten whole 2026-10-11); `docs/MU-RECURSION.md` governs.
