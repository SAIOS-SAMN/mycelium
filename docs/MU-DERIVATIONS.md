### D1.2 `THROAT_GRID` — a throat's horizon level and fit, against its μ-S bank

**Status.** Phase A **Built** (2026-10-31, the live-readings ruling; no law
change). `server::cognition::mouth::fits_in(space, mass)` decides every
face's fit, at offer and at every feed (`fits_both`): curved holds; a
sphere face by `core::certified::separates(r, pi)` (π refined from the bits
that resolve `r`, doubled until its enclosure leaves `r`); a torus face by
`cga::containment(horizon, image) = Apart` against the image under every
period (`translator_at` sandwich). `Level::fits` (a ratio) is deleted; the
served `Level` stays a reading on `THROAT_GRID`, which stays REGISTERED
until Phase B. Tests: `server::cognition::tests::sphere`
(`sphere_fit_is_decided_past_any_grid`, `flat_fit_meet_equals_half_ratio`,
`sphere_throat_fits_cap_short_of_antipode` rewritten onto `fits_in`),
`core::tests::certified::separates_terminates_on_rational_radius`; two
mutation entries (the refinement's direction, `Apart` in the flat fit).
Phase B is not built. The text below is the derivation as specified.

**Site.** `src/server/cognition/mouth.rs:234` —
`pub const THROAT_GRID: u32 = 64;`, used at three sites:

```rust

let ratio = Encl::exact(r).div(&pi(THROAT_GRID))?.hi;               // sphere_throat, :226
Some(cosh(&Encl::exact(r), THROAT_GRID).hi)                         // throat_level,  :249
```

with `r = 2m/R` (`m` = `Court::mass_now`, committed `(n, d)` plus
`Throat.adjusted`; `R` the face's radius). The results fill
`Level::{Curved(c), Sphere{level, ratio}}`
(`src/anastome/dialogue/tube.rs:202`). `Level::fits` decides
`Curved → true`, `Flat: ratio < ½`, `Sphere: ratio < 1`. It is called by
`fits_both` at offer acceptance (`mouth.rs:612`) and at each feed
(`mouth.rs:352`), refusing `TooWide`. `tube_of` serves both levels
(`mouth.rs:786`, `ACT_HANDOFF` kind 13).

**Governs.** Admission: whether a tube's throat fits each face, at offer
and at every feed. A `Sphere` face whose true `r/π` is below 1 but within
the `2⁻⁶⁴` enclosure width of 1 is refused (the upper bound passes 1). The
served level (a reading) is a bound, not the level. `Curved` fit never
consults the grid, and `Flat` is already exact (`flat_throat`, no
transcendental), so the grid decides admission only through the sphere
ratio.

**Why it is underived.** 78.134 took `c = cosh(2m/R)` "as the upper bound
of the certified enclosure on the grid `2^-64`". MU-CONCORDANCE 2.6 states
"taken as the upper bound of a proven enclosure on a fixed grid".
`OPEN_PHYSICS` names it as an approximation that "retires" when "the
throat is a Cl(4,1) round carried by its bank's modes, fit by meet".
78.162 (5): "A throat's mass is never a scalar. A throat is a ROUND of
Cl(4,1), its horizon carried by what its bank holds; whether it fits a
face is the meet of the round with its own image, never a ratio such as
`2m/L`." For rational `r ≠ 0`, `cosh r` and `cos r` are transcendental
(Lindemann–Weierstrass), so no rational round has hyperbolic or spherical
radius exactly `2m/R`. The grid is the price of keeping a scalar mass
primary; 64 is a choice of bits.

**Severity.** High (admission of a tube; the μ-S bank's draws).

**Derivation.** Two phases. Phase A removes the grid from every court
decision with no law change. Phase B makes the round primary and is a
Concordance amendment.

- *Phase A — exact decisions under the present law.*
  - Sphere fit. `r = 2m/R ∈ ℚ>0` fits iff `r < π`. Decided by refining
    `pi(w)` at `w = 64, 128, 256, …` until `Encl(π).lo > r` (fits) or
    `Encl(π).hi < r` (refused, `TooWide` as now). This terminates for
    every rational `r` because `π ∉ ℚ`. The precision it needs is bounded
    by `r`'s own size, not by a constant: π's irrationality measure is
    finite (`μ(π) ≤ 7.103`, Zeilberger–Zudilin 2020:
    `|π − p/q| > q^{−7.103}` for `q ≥ q₀`), so
    `w ≤ ⌈7.103 · log₂ q⌉ + O(1)` bits for `r = p/q`. No grid stands, and
    none is capped.
  - Flat fit, restated as the meet it already is. The horizon is the dual
    sphere `S = sphere_at(x_v, (2m)²) = X(x_v) − ½(2m)² e_∞`. Its image
    under the shortest period `L` is `S' = T_L S T̃_L` (`translator_at`).
    For dual spheres, `S·S' = ½(ρ² + ρ² − L²)` and `S² = ρ²`, so
    ```text
    (S ∧ S')² = (S·S')² − S²S'² = ¼ L² (L² − 4ρ²),   ρ = 2m
    ```
    The pair is apart (`pair_regime = Boost` and
    `containment(S, S') = Apart`) iff `L > 2ρ = 4m` iff `2m/L < ½`. This is
    the same decision as `ratio < ½`, now read by `enclosure::containment`
    on every period image, with no ratio formed (78.162 (5)).
  - Served level. `Level::Curved(c)` and `Level::Sphere{level}` stay
    certified bounds in Phase A, marked as a reading (an instrument), never
    consulted by admission. Phase A retires the grid from the court's
    decisions, not from the reading.
- *Phase B — the throat as a round carried by its bank's modes.*
  - The bank. Under 78.162, μ-S is held as MODES:
    `Mode::Boost { ray β, c, forward }` with amount `n ∈ ℤ>0`, the count of
    moves (`src/economy/modes.rs`). Each mode is a point on its flow,
    `M = 1 + t·B` (`FlowPoint::at(B, t)`, `k = B² > 0`), with `t = c`
    signed by `forward`. A boost is a hyperbolic mode: two null rays and
    the expansion between them (founding principle LUMINAL,
    `founding.rs`).
  - Placing modes at the mouth. A mode's class is invariant under
    conjugation (HOLONOMIC). Each mode is carried to the face's frame `F`
    (`F M F̃`), so its plane passes through the mouth's place `v`
    (`frame_base_point(F)` on `ℍ³`, `sphere_base_point(F)` on `S³`). All of
    a throat's modes then lie on ONE plane through `v`, the throat's axis,
    and commute, so the merging of equal modes in `Modes` loses no order.
  - Composition, exact, by the flow law already built
    (`FlowPoint::then`, 78.75):
    ```text
    t_bank = ⊕_i ( t_i ⊕ … ⊕ t_i  [n_i times] ),     t₁ ⊕ t₂ = (t₁ + t₂) / (1 + t₁t₂)     (k = +1 normalised)
    ```
    A draw composes the drawn modes' `back()`.
  - Curved face (`ℍ³`, the sheet `v² = −1` in span(e₁, e₂, e₃, e₋)). The
    horizon is the round through `w = V v Ṽ / (V Ṽ)` about `v`, with
    `V = 1 + t_bank·B`:
    ```text
    c = −v·w = (1 + t_bank²) / (1 − t_bank²)        (= FlowPoint::c(), the boost's cosh, exact ∈ ℚ)
    σ = v + c·e₊                                    (dual round: for P = q + e₊, q² = −1, P·σ = q·v + c = 0 ⇔ q·v = −c)
    σ² = c² − 1                                     (> 0: a real round; = 0: the zero throat)
    ```
    `|t_bank| < 1` is a point on the boost's flow; `|t| ≥ 1` is off it,
    refused naming the mode (`OffFlow`). Fit: `ℍ³` is simply connected and
    has no image, so the round fits whenever `σ² > 0`. That is the present
    `Curved → true` with the empty throat made explicit (`Starved`).
  - Sphere face (`S³`, `v² = +1` in span(e₁, e₂, e₃, e₊)). The cap's
    boundary round is `σ = v − c·e₋` (for `P = q + e₋`, `q² = +1`:
    `P·σ = q·v − c`), with `c` rational wherever the cap's half-tangent
    `t = tan(r/2) ∈ ℚ`: `c = (1 − t²)/(1 + t²)`. Fit is the cap stopping
    short of the antipode. On the elliptic flow (`k = −1`),
    `t₁ ⊕ t₂ = (t₁ + t₂)/(1 − t₁t₂)`, and the composition passes the pole
    exactly where a step has `1 − t₁t₂ ≤ 0` with both `t > 0`: an exact,
    rational test, held as `(0 : 1)` at the pole by `FlowPoint` and never
    divided. O1 settles which modes carry it.
  - Torus face. `S` as in Phase A, with `ρ²` read off the round (not
    `2m`). Fit is apart against every period image. Rational `ρ²` is
    enough: the meet test uses `ρ²` only.
  - Mass. The scalar `m` leaves the court's decisions. Concordance 1.8's
    mass is a reading of the round (in `ℍ³`, `m = (R/2)·arcosh c`, an
    enclosure served as an instrument, never compared).
  - Laws answered. Precision is the rule (78.45, 80.3: an effect becomes
    an exact object, or is refused). No scalar mass (78.162 (5)). μ is
    modes (78.162 (1)). No squaring: `σ²` is the round's own square read
    off the product, not a norm written beside it (law 12). Fit by meet
    (78.162 (5)).
- *Boundary.* A bank mode whose plane cannot be placed through `v` as an
  isometry of the face (an `S`-mode on a sphere face; see O1) is refused
  naming the mode (`NotIsometry`, as 78.134 names it for frames). `σ² ≤ 0`
  is the empty throat, `Starved`, as now. An unreadable mode (no canonical
  ray) refuses the read, never defaults.
- **Open:**
  - **O1, which modes carry a sphere or flat face.** `S`-lane modes are
    boosts and dilations. Such modes are isometries of `ℍ³` (transvections
    `eᵢ∧e₋`) but not of `S³` (isometries `Spin(4)`, all turns, the `H`
    lane) and not of the flat face (translations are null, the `T` lane).
    Candidates:
    - (a) The `S`-bank acts CONFORMALLY. A dilation about `v` scales the
      face's stereographic radius by its multiplier `λ = (1 + t)/(1 − t)`,
      which is rational: `ℍ³` ball radius `u ↦ λu` (fit iff `u < 1`, a new
      refusal at the ideal boundary); `S³` half-tangent `t ↦ λt` (always
      short of the antipode); flat `ρ ↦ λρ`. This needs a SEED round `σ₀`
      per face.
    - (b) Each face's own isometry lane carries it: `S` on `ℍ³`, `H` on
      `S³`, `T` on flat. A throat is then fed on more than one lane, which
      Concordance 2.7 does not forbid but 78.135 does not state.
    - (c) Only curved faces carry rounds from the bank; sphere and flat
      faces keep Phase A.

    Deciding reading: the maintainer's amendment text for Concordance 2.6.
    Concordance 2.6's own constraint, "between two universes the two faces'
    levels may differ only because the two spaces differ", must hold under
    the rule chosen; (a) and (b) satisfy it and (c) does not.
  - **O2, the seed (candidate (a) only).** (i) The offer commits `σ₀` as an
    exact `Mv` in place of `mass (n, d)` (a `Tube` wire change). (ii) A
    face-intrinsic unit round (no canonical one exists in `ℍ³` or the flat

  - **O3, tubes opened under the scalar law.** Open tubes carry

**Why it is underived.** The wire-MTU ruling fixed that the budget is ONE
protocol number the mesh agrees on, not a host reading (the earlier
`spend_unit() · 1024` read host memory and split the mesh). The value
768 KiB is a choice: it is not a multiple of `CHUNK`.
`(786432 + 5 + 84) / 65519` gives 13 Noise messages, the thirteenth
carrying 293 bytes. PH1 (MU-RECURSION) names it "the protocol carrier
`BODY_BUDGET`", which states its role and not its value.

**Severity.** Medium (protocol size, reading bounds).

**Derivation.**

- *A format identity (exact).* Over the Noise channel, the largest body
  that rides in exactly `k ∈ ℤ≥1` transport messages, point included, is
  ```text
  B(k) = k · CHUNK − HEAD − TPoint::LEN = 65519·k − 5 − 84 = 65519·k − 89        (bytes, ∈ ℤ)
  HEAD = 1 (lane byte) + 4 (u32 LE length)   — FORMAT
  CHUNK = 65535 − 16                          — EXTERNAL (Noise rev. 34 §7: message ≤ 65535 bytes; ChaChaPoly tag 16)
  ```
  This reduces the free choice from a byte count to a message count `k`.
  The present value lies between `B(12) = 786139` and `B(13) = 851658`.
- *The count `k`, from the largest unit that must ride whole.* Let `𝒜` be
  the set of wire units that cannot page: a single signed act of each kind
  admitted at the door, a single receipt (`KStepReceipt`), a fold-root
  proof (`encode_fold_root_reply`), a percept frame. For each `a ∈ 𝒜`,
  `MAX_WIRE(a) ∈ ℤ ∪ {∞}` is its codec's structural maximum: the sum over
  its fields of `count_wall × element_max`, where every variable-length
  field is delimited by a typed wall (`u8`/`u16` counts; the u16 is the
  wall, and a blob is never widened). Then
  ```text
  U = max_{a ∈ 𝒜} MAX_WIRE(a) + R_max          (R_max: the largest reply envelope a page adds, e.g. the 8-byte reading height)
  k = ⌈ (U + HEAD + TPoint::LEN) / CHUNK ⌉
  BODY_BUDGET = B(k)
  ```
  Every paging codec then fills to `B(k) − its own envelope`. That
  envelope is a FORMAT constant read off its header codec, replacing each

### D2.1 `cut = held.mu[l] / 2` — the slash of a falsified fold against its tier's bond

**Status.** **Built** (ruled 2026-10-31: read against the EXPOSURE, O1
(b), exact over ℚ with no floor). `cut_l = B_l · X_l / (B_l + X_l)`, `0`
where `B_l + X_l = 0`, on rational lanes (`economy::forfeit`,
`Court::slash(tier, challenger, exposure, defect, anchor)`); `X` is
`Court::exposure_of(key)` as defined below, read before `reverse`; the
defect `D` is `challenge::defect` (the bool `reconstructs` was this
reading at zero). Tests (`economy::tests::forfeit`,
`server::cognition::tests::{bond, challenge}`):
`slash_is_half_where_exposure_equals_bond`,
`slash_never_exceeds_bond_or_exposure`, `slash_is_zero_on_unexposed_lane`,
`defect_is_zero_iff_reconstructs`, `bounty_plus_retired_equals_cut` (with
`kept + cut = B`, the slash conserving the bond), and
`challenge_slashes_bond_against_exposure_and_withdrawal_waits` (the audit
holding through it). The entry below is the derivation as specified.

**Site.** `src/server/cognition/convert.rs:582`, in `Court::slash`
(called from `src/server/cognition/challenge.rs:296` on evidence that
does not reconstruct, and `:309` on a default):

```rust
let Some(held) = self.treasury.held_of(&key).cloned() else { return [0; 4] };
let cut: [u64; 4] = std::array::from_fn(|l| held.mu[l] / 2);
```

**Governs.** μ: how much of a tier's bond a falsified fold forfeits on
each lane. The kept part `B − cut` stays held under `"bond" ‖ tier`; the
cut splits into bounty (D2.2) and POWR retirement. Boundary: no hold →
`[0; 4]`; an all-zero cut returns before any treasury move.

**Why it is underived.** 78.129 rules "HALF its tier's bond on each
lane, floored". The half is the same on a fold that lied by one unit and
a fold that lied by its whole netting, and on a bond that covers the
exposure a thousand times or a thousandth of once. MU-ECONOMY §8 lists
"slash against bond" among the policy ratios "the physics already reads";
the code reads nothing for it. MU-EQUATIONS has no entry for the tier
slash (§6.9 covers only burn and equivocation), so the half is stated
nowhere in the equations.

**Severity.** High (μ; the only discipline, MU-ECONOMY §4).

**Derivation.**

- Readings the court already holds at the falsification, each read BEFORE
  `reverse` moves it:
  - `B_l ∈ ℤ≥0` — the bond held:
    `treasury.held_of(bond_key(tier)).mu[l]` (`economy::pay::Held`).
  - `X_l ∈ ℤ≥0` — the EXPOSURE: the value the falsified fold's commitment
    moved on lane `l`, the quantity reversal returns. From `FoldRecord`
    (`src/server/cognition/ledger.rs:116`) and `Ledgers.remaining`, with
    `Net = ([u8; 32], [i128; 4])`:
    ```text
    own(k)      = remaining[k] ∘ Σ_{p ∈ parents(k)} included_p(k)        (rows, declared) as admitted
    X_l         = Σ_{(w,n) ∈ own(k).rows} n_l⁺  +  Σ_{(w,n) ∈ own(k).declared} |n_l|
                  + Σ_{(c, rows, decl) ∈ rec.nets} ( Σ_{(w,n) ∈ rows} n_l⁺ + Σ_{(w,n) ∈ decl} |n_l| )
    ```
    Rows move key to key with `Σ_w n_l = 0` (78.127), so
    `Σ n_l⁺ = Σ n_l⁻` exactly and the positive part is the transferred
    value with no `½`; a declared flow is one-sided, so its absolute value
    is its value. Computed in `BigInt`, never on a carrier that can pass.
  - `D_l ∈ ℚ≥0` — the DEFECT, the falsified fold's measured lie on lane
    `l`. On evidence (`Challenge::Evidence` / streamed `Part`), from the
    exact quantities `reconstructs` (`challenge.rs:109`) already builds
    and then discards as a bool:
    ```text
    D_l = Σ_w | want_{w,l} − have_{w,l} |  +  Σ_w | declared_{w,l} − own_declared_{w,l} |
    want_{w,l} = rows_{w,l} − Σ_{c ∈ children} rate_{c,l} · included_{c,w,l}        (ℚ: rates are p/q)
    ```
    `reconstructs = true ⇔ D_l = 0 on every lane`, so a falsification by
    reconstruction has `D_l > 0` on at least one lane. On `Challenge::Default` the
    tier published nothing: every net the fold asserted stands unproved,
    so `D_l := X_l` (the unpublished line carries its whole exposure as
    defect).
- The cut, the shape of the POWV share `⌊M · y/(y + R)⌋` (MU-ECONOMY §5.1)
  with the bond as both base and capacity and the defect as the strain on
  it:
  ```text
  cut_l  = ⌊ B_l · D_l / (B_l + D_l) ⌋        cut_l = 0 where B_l + D_l = 0
  kept_l = B_l − cut_l
  ```
  - `cut_l ≤ min(B_l, ⌊D_l⌋)` (half the harmonic mean of `B_l` and
    `D_l`): a lie never forfeits more than was bonded nor more than it
    lied by.
  - `D_l ≪ B_l` ⇒ `cut_l ≈ D_l`: a small lie on a deep bond forfeits about
    its own size.
  - `D_l ≫ B_l` ⇒ `cut_l → B_l`: a lie past the bond takes nearly the
    whole bond. The bond is never reached exactly, so a tier keeps a
    residue that `post` tops up.
  - `D_l = B_l` ⇒ `cut_l = ⌊B_l / 2⌋`: 78.129's half, odd unit kept, is
    the special case where the lie equals the bond.
  - A default (`D_l = X_l`) slashes `⌊B_l X_l / (B_l + X_l)⌋`: the
    exposure against the bond, "bond against cover room" (MU-ECONOMY §8).
- Laws answered: no constant (MU-ECONOMY §8, rule 5: two readings, one
  ratio); four lanes (each lane its own `B, D`); exact (ℚ, one floor);
  measured, not chosen (`D` is the reconstruction's own residue); never
  squared (the ratio is a fraction of readings; no fixed point is
  iterated).
- Boundary: no hold → nothing, as today. `D_l = 0` on a lane → nothing
  slashed on that lane, even on a falsified fold (the lie was on other
  lanes). No other refusal arises: `B, X, D` are exact, and `cut ≤ B` fits
  `u64`.
- **Open:**
  - **O1, the strain on the bond, `D` or `X`.** Candidate (a): `D`, as
    above, with defaults using `D := X`. Candidate (b): `X` for both
    cases; this slashes by exposure, so a one-unit arithmetic lie on a
    large fold forfeits as much as a fabricated fold. Candidate (c): the
    court's strain `y_l` against `R`, the literal POWV form; rejected,
    because a falsification at a court at rest would forfeit nothing,
    which deletes the discipline MU-ECONOMY §4 makes the only one.
    Deciding reading: on the soak's challenge fixtures, the distribution
    of `D_l / X_l` over falsified folds. If falsifications are whole-fold
    fabrications (`D ≈ X`), (a) and (b) agree and (a) is the stricter
    definition. Whether `X` includes the netted children (the `rec.nets`
    term) is decided by the same reading: `reconstructs` puts the lie in

POWR retirement, and the odd unit goes to retirement. This is the reason
a challenge is filed at all (the 78.129 comment).

**Why it is underived.** 78.127 and 78.129: "half to the challenger …
half retired". The half pays a challenger the same whether a default
merely went unanswered or a published line proved a lie, and whatever
the share of the exposure proved false.

**Severity.** High (μ; the challenge incentive governs finality's
honesty).

**Derivation.**

- The challenger's work, as the court holds it, is the part of the fold's
  exposure the challenger's act proved false: the defect `D_l` (D2.1)
  against the exposure `X_l`. The reconstruction the court ran is the court's own
  work, already paid where scarce by POWV (MU-ECONOMY §5.1); it is not
  the challenger's, and it is not paid twice.
- ```text
  bounty_l  = ⌊ cut_l · D_l / (D_l + X_l) ⌋        0 where D_l + X_l = 0
  retired_l = cut_l − bounty_l                       (the odd unit and the floor's remainder retire)
  ```
  - Default (`D_l = X_l`): `bounty_l = ⌊cut_l / 2⌋`, today's half, so the
    existing reason to file a demand is unchanged.
  - A reconstruction proving a lie larger than the exposure (`D > X`: fabricated
    rows): the challenger takes more than half, up to the whole cut.
  - A reconstruction proving a small arithmetic defect on a large fold (`D ≪ X`):
    a small bounty, the rest retired. Filing a challenge over a rounding
    defect pays little, which removes a griefing incentive.
- Laws answered: no constant; four lanes; exact; taken out of the cut,
  never minted (issuance-neutral, like POWV); `bounty + retired = cut`, so
  the MU-ECONOMY §6 invariant holds through it.
- Boundary: `cut_l = 0` → nothing; `D_l + X_l = 0` → nothing; the
  existing early return on an all-zero cut stays.
- **Open:**
  - **O1, whether the default case keeps the half.** `D := X` makes a
    default pay exactly today's half. Candidate: a default pays `0`
    bounty, since it proved nothing by evidence and only waited out the
    window. Rejected for now, because 78.129 states the bounty is "a
    challenge's reason to be filed" and a demand is a filed challenge.
    Deciding reading: whether the soak shows demands filed against
    honest-but-slow tiers to farm defaults; the count of defaults whose
    tier later published a line that reconstructs is the reading.

### D3.2 The common refinement `K_lcm` — two frames related by transduction in turns, never by a common lattice

**Status.** **Built** (MU-RECURSION 78.170, the operator's rulings of
2026-10-31). The lcm cap (`MAX_LCM_AXIS = 60`, `MAX_LCM_SITES = 3600`) and
the metered common refinement that first replaced it (`CommonRefinement`,
`bipartite_cochain`, both `refine_delta`s, `arc::Refined`, `need =
R_h·R_w + 2·L_h·L_w`, `Unrefined`) are deleted; no reader forms an lcm. The
organ is `src/transducer/family/transduce/`.

**What was wrong.** `K_lcm = lcm(h_in, h_out) × lcm(w_in, w_out)` is the
uniform subdivision both grids embed in. It turned an incidence question
into an equality question by writing unit cells, so its size was the lcm,
not the frames: on `18 × 19 → 7 × 9` (task `0b148d64`, pair 0) `K_lcm` is
`126 × 171 = 21 546` cells, the change set 14 045 links, and charting it
(`invariant_of`) took ≥ 19 s a pair. Reading the code whole found four more
defects: the input pulled back onto `K_lcm` was written (`L_h·L_w` per
call) and never read; an anisotropic frame was absent from the listing key,
so `[[1]] → [[2, 2]]` and `[[1, 1]] → [[2, 2]]` (one `K_lcm` coboundary,
neither writing its frame) gave two tasks one address (law 2); μ counted
`K_lcm` cells (one changed output site of `29 × 29 → 30 × 30` measured
841); and the deflation subtracted an output-native `after` from a `K_lcm`
`before`.

**The transduction, as built.** LIFT → ACT → PROJECT; past the sensor the
state is bodies and motions, and per-site values are written only at the
sensor and where a grid is spoken.

- **LIFT** (`turn.rs`, `enclosed.rs`), at rank `n`. A sample of extents
  `e₁, …, eₙ` is `n` phases of turns, `ℚ/ℤ`, each of its own period
  (`Enclosure`, read from the sample: `Enclosure::of`). A boundary is the
  `Turn` `index / period`, compared with another by integer
  cross-multiplication, never reduced, never a `(cos, sin)` point, never a
  conformal point; the seam `0 ≡ 1` is held, never wrapped. The sensor reads
  the sample once into BANDS (`Enclosed::of`): `n`-dimensional boxes of one
  symbol, read in canonical address order as the maximal runs along the
  last axis, each continued along every earlier axis while the slice before
  shows the same box. Every band boundary is a boundary of its body
  somewhere along it, so a body is held by its boundary record,
  O(perimeter), never by its interior. `put_content` writes the frame's
  full content as its periods and bands, losslessly.
- **ACT** (`record.rs`, `motion.rs`). `Transduction::of(x, y, budget)`
  records the INCIDENCES between the input's bands and the output's under
  the re-period of each phase: the pairs of bands whose arcs overlap with
  positive length on every phase. Each visit holds an arc of every phase's
  merge no other visit holds, so the spend is bounded by `need = Π R_k`,
  `R(a, b) = a + b − gcd(a, b)` the arcs of phase `k`'s merge
  (`transduce::need`, `need_of`; at rank 2, `R_h·R_w`). An anisotropic
  scale is a re-period per phase, carried by no versor (Liouville: a
  versor's differential is a scalar times an orthogonal map). `share` is
  the changed share of the frame, exact (O1). `carried` reads a body
  carried whole onto one output body, else `Mixed`. The motions keeping the
  seams are the `2ⁿ·n!` signed permutations of the `n` phases
  (`SignedPermutation`, generated, the identity one element among them;
  eight at rank 2), read from an integer matrix (`of_matrix`) or off an
  alphabet word's images of the sensor plane's two generators (`of_word`);
  anything else (a shear, a `(3, 4, 5)` letter) is `Oblique`. A turn
  carries no period, so a quarter turn followed by any re-period is exact
  on turns.

### D3.5 `percepts_since >= 2` — a pending call let go

**Status.** **Built** (2026-10-31, the live-readings ruling), on the axis
`SeatMind.beat` already reads (O1 (a), `Percept.seq`). `PendingCall.made_at`

**Site.** `src/client/sdk/seat_mind/mind.rs:284` (`read_pending`), with

```

**Governs.** When a seat mind stops waiting for the house's `Outcome` to
a call: `Perceived.abandoned` is set, `pending` cleared, and a later
`Outcome` naming that call is dropped by `outcome()` (its
`filter(|p| p.reference == o.call)` finds nothing), so the effect is never
learned (`Experience` uncounted). Liveness and cognition of the seat mind.

**Why it is underived.** "2" is stated as the beat rule in the comment,
but it is counted on percepts RECEIVED, not on the house's sequence: a
re-delivered percept (the same `seq`) or a gap in `seq` (pages lost or

**Severity.** Medium (liveness; a late outcome is lost to learning).

**Derivation.**

- The quantity: the seat's beat is the house's percept sequence,
  `Percept.seq` (the `SeatMind.beat` doc: "the house's percept

  a duration is the count of transitions carried through, never a count
  of events observed beside it). The rule it applies is MU-CLIENT §4.13:
  "A beat dissipates what stood through the previous beat unreturned."
- Formula. Let `b₀ = self.beat` at `called` (the beat the call was made
  in, after percept `b₀` was read), and `b = p.seq` at a later percept.

  one whole beat", the dissipation rule's own reading on the seat's line.

- Laws: time is distance along an orbit (78.43(3)); no counts as

- **Open:**
  - **O1, which axis is the seat's beat.** `Percept.seq` (the house's

    `(u at outcome − u at call)` per seat, from a typed reading

    of outcomes arriving at `d ≥ 2` on the soak (from O1's reading); if
    non-zero, (b), since experience is otherwise lost by a bound, not by

### D4.4 `NESTING = 32`, `depth > 16` — decoder recursion bounds

**Status.** **Built** by (iii) (2026-10-31, the live-readings ruling). O1
read off the producers: `Selection` is minted only by the derive's closure,
three levels deep (a base at most `ArgExt(Inter(Hue, Star(Hue)))`, four

one meet), so `form::clause::PRED_HEIGHT = 3 + 1`. Both are closed forms,
EXACT against the tests that name them; `Selection::height` reads a term's levels. The
decoders refuse a term deeper than its language mints (`depth >=` the
height), which bounds every walker of a decoded tree at once. `NESTING`
leaves the register; the `16` is gone. Tests:
`transducer::anchor::tests::depth` (`derive_closure_height_equals_selection_height`,
`sel_parse_survives_frame_length_nesting`: a frame of `Delta` opcodes on a
2 MiB thread, refused), `transducer::sentence::form::tests::depth`
(`take_pred_refuses_past_language_depth`, `take_pred_survives_frame_length_nesting`);
a mutation entry on the selection refusal. A kernel whose own rule language
mints deeper terms is refused at decode until the language's height is
amended. The text below is the derivation as specified.

**Site.** `src/transducer/sentence/form/clause.rs:270–273, 411–413`:

```rust

    if depth > NESTING { return None; }
```

`src/transducer/anchor/codec.rs:66–72`:

```rust

    if depth > 16 { return None; }
```

(Each admits depths `0..=bound`, i.e. 33 and 17 levels.)

**Governs.** Admission of a predicate or selection term from a peer's
tape; stack safety of the court and the mind (MU-STANDARDS §2.4: an abort
in cognition is a court an adversary stops).

**Why it is underived.** Both are literals; they differ by a factor of
two with no reason given; neither is derived from the stack or from the
bytes.

**Severity.** High where reached from the wire (a stack overflow aborts
the process, a court stopped); Medium as a reading bound.

**Derivation.**

- Bytes bound (exact): every level consumes at least one tag byte before
  it recurses (`take_byte` at `clause.rs:415`; `*pos += 1` at
  `codec.rs:71`), so the depth reached on input `b` is
  `d ≤ L(p₀) ≤ B = 786 432`, and the node count is `≤ L(p₀)` (heap:
  `≤ L(p₀)·size_of::<Pred>()`, linear).
- Stack argument: a recursive parse of depth `d` uses `d·F + F₀` bytes of
  stack, `F` the frame of one `take_pred` / `parse` activation (`F > 0`,
  fixed per build), against the thread's stack `S` (spawned threads:
  2 MiB by default, or `RUST_MIN_STACK`; the main thread: `RLIMIT_STACK`).
  The bytes bound protects the stack only if `B·F ≤ S`, i.e.
  `F ≤ 2 097 152 / 786 432 < 3` bytes, which no activation meets. So the
  bytes bound does NOT prevent stack exhaustion; a depth bound or an
  iteration is required.
- The parse is not the only recursion: every walker of the decoded tree
  (the derived `Drop` of the `Box` chain, `Clone`, `PartialEq`, `Debug`,
  `to_bytes`/`put_pred`, `Selection::eval` at `selection.rs`) recurses to the same
  `d` (§2 item 9). An iterative parser alone moves the overflow from
  decode to drop.
- Three derivations, one to rule:
  - (i) Convert to iteration: a parser with an explicit heap stack
    (bounded by `L(p₀)`), an iterative `Drop` (children taken into a `Vec`
    work list) and iterative walkers. Then the only bound is the bytes,
    `d ≤ L(p₀)`, exact, and the constant is deleted. Cost: every recursive
    walker of `Pred`/`Selection` becomes a work-list walk (L).
  - (ii) Keep recursion and derive the bound from the stack:
    `d_max = ⌊(S − S_used) / F⌋`. `S` is readable (`RLIMIT_STACK`, or the
    `stack_size` the spawner sets); `F` is not portably measurable (it
    changes with inlining and optimisation level, and `Drop` glue has its
    own frame), so this derivation rests on a measured `F` per build, and
    is fragile.
  - (iii) Derive from the language, not the stack: a decoded term deeper
    than any term the producer can mint is no word of the language. `Selection`
    is minted by `anchor/derive.rs` (growth rounds bounded by the derive's
    own budget) and `Pred` by the family acts; if the producer's depth is
    a function of its inputs (e.g. the derive's round count), the decoder
    refuses `d >` that function. This bounds every walker at once, with no
    stack measurement.
- Recommendation: (iii) where the producer's depth is a closed form, else
  (i). Do not keep (ii) as a literal; do not derive from bytes alone
  (shown insufficient above).
- **Open:**
  - **O1, the producer's maximal depth** for `Selection` (the derive's rounds)
    and for `Pred` (`Value::Count`, `Touches`, `Not`, `And`/`Any` nesting
    from the acts and the mind's rule language). Deciding reading:
    instrument `depth()` on every term minted on the soak and in the
    derive; if bounded by a closed form, rule (iii); if unbounded by
    construction, rule (i).

**Implementation scope.**

- (iii): `clause.rs` and `codec.rs` take the producer's bound as a named
  reading (`Selection::DERIVE_DEPTH` as a function of the derive's rounds; the
  same for `Pred`), one bound per type, `depth ≥ bound` refusing (`None`).
- (i): `clause.rs`, `codec.rs`, `selection.rs` (iterative eval), a manual `Drop`

- Wire: none for conforming tapes. Concordance: none.
- Tests:

- Mutation audit: yes (the refusal guards §2.4).

the guard bits of `exp`/`cosh`/`cos`/`pi` (reached by `mouth.rs`).

**Derivation.**

*(a) The term count of `P(a, x)` (and `erf v = P(½, v²)`), solved
exactly.* The series is `S = Σ_{n≥0} t_n`, `t_0 = 1`,
`t_n = t_{n−1}·r_n`, `r_n = x/(a + n)`. Terms are positive; `r_n`
decreases in `n`. Let `n₀ = max(0, ⌈2x − a⌉ − 1)`, the least `n` with
`r_{n+1} = x/(a + n + 1) ≤ ½`. For `j ≥ 0`, `t_{n₀+j} ≤ t_{n₀}·2^{-j}`,
and the tail after `t_N` (`N ≥ n₀`) is
`Σ_{i>N} t_i ≤ t_N·ρ/(1 − ρ) ≤ t_N` with `ρ = r_{N+1} ≤ ½` (the code's
stated remainder, `:483–485`). The code stops at the first `N ≥ n₀` with
`t_N ≤ S_lo·2^{-(w₂−8)}`. Since `S ≥ t_{n₀}`, it suffices that
`2^{-(N − n₀)} ≤ 2^{-(w₂−8)}`, so the series runs at most

```text
N(a, x, w₂) = n₀ + (w₂ − 8) = max(0, ⌈2x − a⌉ − 1) + w₂ − 8
```

terms (an exact integer bound; `x, a ∈ ℚ`, `w₂ ∈ ℕ`). For `erf`: `a = ½`,
`x = v²`, `N = max(0, ⌈2v² − ½⌉ − 1) + w₂ − 8`. Metered, not capped: each
term spends one stroke of the caller's `Budget` (law 1, MU-ENGINEERING
§5); before the loop, a budget with `remaining < N(a, x, w₂)` refuses up
front by a named refusal (`Unmetered { needed: N }`, or `None` with the
shortfall readable), so the series is never started and abandoned.
`P_SERIES_CAP` is deleted; the API gains `&mut Budget` (callers: tests
only, today).

*(b) The `erf` tail branch and `14_000`.* For `v² > X` the bound used is
`erfc|v| ≤ e^{−v²}/(|v|√π)`. `14_000` is a safe rounding of
`10⁴·log₂e + log₂(100√π)`, about 14 434. With (a) in place the branch is
no longer forced by a cap; where it is kept as the cheap path, its
condition is derived, not rounded: take
`T = up(exp_point(−v², w + 8).hi / (|v|·√π_lo))` (an exact rational upper
bound via the certified `exp` and `pi`), and return `[1 − T, 1]`
(sign-reflected) whenever `T ≤ 2^{-w}`; otherwise run the series under
the budget. No `14_000`, and no refusal for large `w`.

*(c) The Poisson precision ceiling `w + 256`, step `+64`.* For rational
`λ > 0`, `F(k) = e^{−λ}·Σ_{j≤k} λ^j/j!` is irrational (Lindemann:
`e^{−λ}` is transcendental), so `F(k) ≠ u` for rational `u`, and raising
precision always decides eventually; but the needed precision depends on
`|u − F(k)|`, which has no a-priori bound. So the loop is a Budget walk,
not a ceiling: each precision round spends strokes (one per term, `k`
terms), the precision doubles per round (`wp ← 2·wp`, geometric, so the
total work is within a factor 2 of the last round), and a spent budget
answers the exact SECTOR (law 4): the undecided step `k` has
`F(k−1) < u` decided and `u` inside `F(k)`'s enclosure, so the quantile is
`k` or `k + 1`, `Sector { lo: k, hi: k + 1 }`, never `None`, never a
guess. Initial precision: `e^{−λ}` must be resolved relative to its own
size, so the absolute grid needs `⌈λ·log₂e⌉` more bits; with the exact
rational upper bound `log₂e < 3/2`, `wp₀ = w + ⌈3λ/2⌉ + g_exp`. The
current `w + 32 + mag_bits(λ)` loses the leading term: for `λ` beyond
about `(w + 288)·ln 2` the first term `e^{−λ}` rounds to `[0, 2^{-wp}]`
and every round is undecided. Term count per round: until
`cdf.lo ≥ u`; bounded by the `k` at which the upper tail is `≤ 1 − u`,
metered by the same budget.

*(d) The stop rule `2^{-(w₂−8)}`.* An outward-rounded nonzero term never
falls below one grid unit `2^{-w₂}`, so the stop threshold must exceed
`2^{-w₂}`; `8` is a chosen margin, and it dominates the error: the
remainder added is `≤ 2^{8−w₂}`, larger than the accumulated rounding
(about `2N·2^{-w₂}`) for every `N < 128`. Derived rule: stop at the first
term with `bound(t_N) ≤ max(width(sum), 2^{-w₂})`. A term below the sum's
own measured uncertainty adds nothing the sum can resolve; the remainder
then at most doubles the width (one bit), against the current fixed 8.

*(e) Guard bits, per operation.* General bound: a computation with `R`
outward roundings on the grid `2^{-w₂}` (each widening its interval by at
most `2·2^{-w₂}`), whose accumulated width is amplified into the result
by at most `Λ_op` (a Lipschitz bound of the operation on the enclosure),
plus a series remainder `ε_rem`, has
`width ≤ Λ_op·R·2^{1−w₂} + ε_rem`. For the result to be within the final
round's own `2·2^{-w}`:
`g = w₂ − w ≥ ⌈log₂ Λ_op⌉ + ⌈log₂ R⌉ + 1 + g_rem`, with `g_rem = 9` under
the current stop (d) and `1` under the derived stop. Per operation (`N`
the term count, each bounded by the series' ratio):

- `exp_point(v)`: `m` halvings to `|r| ≤ ½`, `N ≤ w₂ − 8` (since
  `t_k < 2^{-k}`), `R = 2N + m`; the `m` squarings amplify relative width
  by `2^m`, and the absolute width of `e^v` carries the value's magnitude:
  `g_exp = m + ⌈log₂(2N + m)⌉ + 1 + g_rem + max(0, ⌈3v/2⌉)`
  (`log₂e < 3/2`). The current `24 + m` omits the last term: for `v > 0`
  the result has `w + 24` RELATIVE bits, not width `2^{-w}` (e.g.
  `exp(100)` at `w = 64` is wide by about `2^{144 − 88}`; §2 item 11).
  Whether `Encl` promises absolute width or only outward rounding is O1
  (the module doc promises rounding to the grid, not a width); the
  `mouth.rs` callers read `cosh(r).hi` and `cos(r).lo` as bounds, which
  stay proven either way.
- `ln2`, `atanh2(z ≤ ⅓)`: ratio `z² ≤ 1/9`,
  `N ≤ ⌈(w₂ − 8)/log₂9⌉ + 1` (`log₂9 > 3`, so `N ≤ ⌈(w₂ − 8)/3⌉ + 1`),
  `R = 2N`, `Λ = 1`: `g = ⌈log₂ 2N⌉ + 1 + g_rem`.
- `ln_point`: `ln v = k·ln2 + ln m`; `ln2`'s width is amplified by `|k|`,
  so `g = ⌈log₂ max(|k|, 1)⌉ + 1 + g_atanh`. The code's `ilog2|k| + 1` is
  this term, derived. The extra `+64` on `l2` (`:270`) is redundant: `w₂`
  already carries the `|k|` amplification.
- `pi` (Machin): `Λ = 16 + 4 = 20`,

  enclosure, unbounded near a pole, so measured: compute at `w + 16`, read

`FORMAT:` tag names the layout. A number that only looks like format
because it sits beside a codec, such as an envelope reservation that does
not equal its header (D4.6), is not format and has an entry. Caveat:
`ACCOUNT_SHARDS = 256`
(`src/server/cognition/published/publish.rs:18`) is a layout, but a cost
and memory layout rather than a coordinate of a writing; D1.4 O2 decides
whether it is registered as engineering or derived from the published
account count.

**Class 3, external standard.** Fixed outside the physics by a published
standard or a governing rule, cited by clause in the `EXTERNAL:` tag.
Examples: the Noise message bound `MESSAGE_MAX = 65535` and the
ChaChaPoly tag `TAG_LEN = 16` (`src/network/noise.rs:36–38`; Noise
Protocol Framework rev. 34 §7), whose difference is the `CHUNK` D1.3
reads; the Ed25519 public key of 32 octets and signature of 64 octets
(RFC 8032 §5.1.6), where a signature actually rides a body (D4.6);
`CONFIG_HZ`, the scheduler tick the operating system publishes in
`/boot/config-<release>` (read, not chosen, D2.6); and the 2500-line
reading bound `BOUND = 2500` (`xtask/lint/length.rs:20`; MU-STANDARDS
1.1), a standard binding developers, never a limit on what a mind may
count (Concordance 1.10).
