# 78.189 — The atlas on Sⁿ: charts as caps, transitions on overlaps, gluing on triangles

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** `transducer::stalk::sphere` (new); `transducer::cga` (`containment`, EN1); the body's nucleus (`blade::Blade::nucleus`); the physics' motion between bodies (`blade::versor_between`); the atlas (`transducer::stalk`).

**Status:** ruled 2026-11-20, derived in §2. Built in part (§4); the rest is tracked on #401.

## 1. Why

The atlas today is a prefix trie of chart keys (`Invariant`, A21). It locates candidates and holds no geometry: no open sets, no overlaps, no transitions. A key prefix is a coarser stratum, nothing more.

The GLOSSARY defines more than that. An atlas's "transitions between its charts are the field's connection, and the holonomy of an orbit's transitions per revolution is the field's curvature". Those transitions need charts that overlap, and on a lattice or a key trie there is nothing to overlap.

The rules this rests on:
- Concordance 1.1: "Space is spheres, manifolds and tubes."
- Concordance 2.10: nested shells, each an Sⁿ.
- The no-lattice rule (#401): no lattice past the sensor.

## 2. Derivation

1. **The base is `Sⁿ = ℝⁿ ∪ {∞}`.** `Cl(n+1, 1)`(ℚ) is its conformal model, and its versors are the Möbius maps of Sⁿ (MU-RECURSION 38.5, 51.2).
2. **A chart is an open cap of Sⁿ.** The cap is bounded by the body's nucleus, `c + ½(c² − r²)e∞ + e₀`, with `r²` the exact second moment and no root taken (#134). On Sⁿ a ball, its complement and a half-space are all caps, and one Möbius map carries any cap onto any other.
3. **Two charts overlap** by the sign of their rounds' inversive product, read through GR1's pair regime and EN1's radical-plane side (`cga::containment`):
   - on each other (cut or tangent);
   - one inside the other, either way;
   - apart.

   A Möbius map keeps the inversive product up to a positive weight, so the reading is the same on every image of the atlas. Claim 2 tests this under a dilation and a translation. No grid, size or place enters.
4. **The transition on an overlap** is the motion carrying one body's readings onto the other's. The physics states it as the versor between the bodies' shapes; the atlas never chooses it. An overlap with no stated motion is held unread.
5. **Gluing is the cocycle.** On a triangle of pairwise overlapping charts, the circuit `a → b → c → a` composes to `g̃_ac · g_bc · g_ab`.
   - Where that is a scalar, the triangle glues: coordinates agree wherever the caps overlap.
   - Otherwise the composite is the triangle's holonomy. It is kept whole, as the field's curvature (MU-TOPOLOGY §4).

   This is the gluing the sheaf statements already name (MU-ANASTOME §4.1; MU-RECURSION 13.5, 54.5), now read on charts that overlap.
6. **Coordinating by coordinates.**
   - Coordinates move between charts only along transitions on overlaps.
   - Where they agree around every triangle, the atlas holds together as one coordinate system.
   - Where they do not, the holonomy says exactly how they differ.

   The pivots (ascend/descend, condense/drain, puncture/seal; MU-TOPOLOGY §2) are the moves across these transitions. A path between two charts is read by composing the transitions along overlapping charts.

## 3. Names

- **Gluing**, not "cohesion": GLOSSARY's *Cohesion* is the held share per plane, `A/(A + Q)`.
- **The atlas's overlaps** (`Overlaps`), not "cover": GLOSSARY reserves *Cover* for `cech::Cover`.
- **Chart**: the GLOSSARY entry is amended. A chart is now an open cap of Sⁿ bounded by a body's round. It is no longer "never a round".

## 4. What is built, and what is not

**Built:** `transducer::stalk::sphere`.
- `meeting(a, b)`: the overlap reading.
- `overlaps(rounds, motion, budget)`: an edge per overlapping pair, with the motion asked only there, one read per pair (78.153).
- `gluing(overlaps, budget)`: per triangle, `Glued`, `Holonomy(composite)` or `Unread`, one read per triangle.

Claims (`stalk/tests/sphere.rs`):
1. Charts meet by the inversive product: cut, nested either way, apart.
2. Every meeting survives a conformal map (a dilation by 7/3 and a translation).
3. Overlaps hold an edge per meeting pair, and the motion is asked only on an overlap.
4. A triangle glues where its circuit is a scalar. Off by `e`, its holonomy is the translation by `−e`. An edge with no stated motion leaves it unread.

**Not yet built** (tracked on #401):
- **Grouping by overlap and transition** in place of grouping by chart key (`family/percept.rs`, `family/speak.rs`, `stalk::congruent`), #408.
- **The stratum key.** The trie's key read from the cap (#402/#407) instead of lattice moduli. Once grouping no longer depends on it, the key only locates.
- **The pivots as moves across transitions** (#406), with the transport kept as holonomy.
- **The body's Sⁿ inside the larger manifold** (#404). The cap is read in the sample's manifold (78.179).
- **The wire, routing and ledger** (`ACT_DIGESTS`, `HomeTrie`, `Cite::Word`). These are a network contract change and a recut, raised separately.
