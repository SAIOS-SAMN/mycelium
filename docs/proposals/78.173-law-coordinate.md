# 78.173 — LAW-COORDINATE v4: the coordinate a kernel addresses its bodies by

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

## 1. The law

- **The coordinate is the state.** A body stands at
  ```
  origin / h : S:G:T:H : K
  ```
  - The **origin** is its enclosure: the universe's root (the genesis
    hash and the epoch TUniversal already carries, Concordance 1.11) and
    the enclosures the body stands in, outermost first, each written out
    as the coordinate its branch was cut at (78.152 TU-6 BRANCH). It is
    never a digest beyond the genesis hash.
  - `h` is the **bell**.
  - The **lanes** are the body's cumulative change by motion type, with
    the network's meanings: **S** spatial (boost and dilation, the
    reach), **G** gauge (change of kind), **T** temporal (null flux: the
    beat and the passages), **H** holonomic (turnings between planes).
  - `K` counts the orbits the body closed (45.7: a kernel's own count,
    never compared with another kernel's), one count per component of H.
- **Lanes are vectors.** A lane holds exact signed components, each
  keyed by its axis, never summed into one number.
  - An **axis** is named by one ASCII capital and a positive index
    (`S1`, `G1`, `T1`), or is a **frame unit**: plane `k`'s positive unit
    `e₊ₖ` (squaring to +1), written `+k`, or its negative unit `e₋ₖ`
    (squaring to −1), written `-k` (78.172 §1). A **key** is one axis,
    written `e_S1`, or a **generator**, the plane named by its two axes in
    the orientation written: `e_+2+3` is `e₊₂e₊₃`. One plane named in both
    orientations in one lane is refused.
  - **H stands only on turning planes** (v4; netstratum docs/117 §10.3).
    Each component is keyed by a generator of two frame units of one
    square, on two planes: the continuum's `e₊ₖe₊ₗ` (`e_+k+l`) or the
    vacuum's `e₋ₖe₋ₗ` (`e_-k-l`), the inverted face. A frame plane's own
    `e₊ₖe₋ₖ`, a mixed pair `e₊ₖe₋ₗ`, and a generator of axes named by
    letters (no square named) hold no turning, and on H each is refused
    `NotTurningPlane` by name, with the plane it names. The phase turned
    "in plane k" is the turning about axis k, the plane of the other two
    frame axes, written in the cyclic orientation: `e₊₂e₊₃` about axis 1,
    `e₊₃e₊₁` about axis 2, `e₊₁e₊₂` about axis 3. τ₁₂, the turning between
    planes 1 and 2, stands on their positive units, `e₊₁e₊₂`: in three
    frame planes it is the plane of the turning about axis 3, one
    component, stored.
  - **S's keys may name boost planes:** a frame plane's own motion
    `e₊ₖe₋ₖ` (`e_+k-k`) and a mixed pair `e₊ₖe₋ₗ` are boosts, on S.
  - **Degrees.** H's components are stored in degrees, exact. A
    component is a motion: its state at a bell, plus a rate in degrees per
    bell, advanced in closed form, `θ(t) = θ(h) + ω·(t − h)`, on the
    universe's own bell. Angles are stated in degrees with the plane named,
    never by a name for a fraction of a turn.
  - **S, G and T** hold their components in their own units.
  - **K per component:** `K_c = ⌊|θ_c| / 360⌋`, 360 the degree's
    definition.
- **Open lanes.** S, G, T and H are the base lanes, each held in every
  lane set. A lane set is a list of named lanes, never a fixed four. A
  further lane is admitted only through a ruled opening, never by
  convention: opening one states its name and its law version, and a lane
  no ruled opening admits is refused by name. No opening is ruled yet;
  the ruled act itself, agreement between kernels, is the next step (§7).
- **Signed lanes inside a universe.** Each component is an exact rational
  of either sign.
- **TUniversal's point is the non-negative case.** `TPoint` at the vertex
  is the non-negative issuance reading: a coordinate with no enclosure
  and one non-negative component per lane, with the same order and
  writing. **Not yet law:** the map from a body's signed lanes inside a
  universe to the issuance reading at the vertex (§7).
- **The ordered key.** The canonical order is the origin, then `h`, then
  the lanes in order (S, G, T, H, then any opened lane by name and
  version), each lane's components by their keys written out, each key
  then its exact value, then `K`. It is the index a kernel addresses its
  bodies by (Concordance 1.13: state addressed by coordinate).
  **An address routes and never identifies:** the key locates a body and
  finds its neighbours; it never says what the body is, and a body after
  another in the order is neither more nor less than it.
- **Neighbours are a range on one component.** At a body `at`, the range
  on component `c` of lane `L` over `[lo, hi)` holds the bodies sharing
  `at`'s origin, bell and lanes before `L`, and `at`'s components of `L`
  keyed before `c`, whose component `c` lies in `[lo, hi)`. They stand
  together in the order, so two bisections find them; any other
  component is a filter within the range. Never all pairs.
- **Decisions on the caller's intervals.** `within` reads where a body
  stands against bounds the caller states at an origin: an exact interval
  `[lo, hi)` per component of one lane. The reading is a side per
  component (Below, Within, Above), and the body is within as a whole only
  where every component is Within; otherwise the reading names each
  component that is not. Bounds keyed otherwise than the lane are
  refused by name.
- **Base 60 is display only.** A component's value is read for display in
  places of base 60: at depth `d` it lies in one sector
  `[m·60⁻ᵈ, (m + 1)·60⁻ᵈ)`, written `whole;p₁,p₂,…`; an H component reads
  directly as degrees;minutes,seconds, and a lane's components are joined
  by ` × `. Nothing is compared, stored or decided by it, and no number
  base is assumed (Concordance 1.6; ipsal-autic's R1).
- **Origins are never compared.** A reading between two bodies, or a body
  and bounds, of different origins is refused by name. The key's order
  groups each origin's bodies by the origin written out; it measures
  nothing across origins.
- **The advance, which composes** (netstratum docs/120 §2). A body at
  bell `h` with components `x` under rates `r`, keyed as the body's
  components are, advanced to bell `t ≥ h`, stands at
  `x(t) = x(h) + r·(t − h)` per component, at the same origin.
  - `K` is re-read per H component.
  - `t < h` is refused by name: there is no backward advance.
  - It composes: `h → a → t` equals `h → t`, for `h ≤ a ≤ t`.
  - Rates keyed otherwise than the body are refused by name.
- **An event's bell.** The bell at which component `c` reaches value `v`
  under rate `r` is `h + (v − x)/r`, solved exactly in first degree with
  no period assumed. It may fall between whole bells. There is none where
  `r = 0`, or where `(v − x)/r < 0`. The reading is named, never a
  boolean.
- **A merger with offsets** (netstratum docs/120 §5). Two bodies at one
  origin and one bell, keyed alike, with holdings `w_a` and `w_b`, merge
  per component to `x = (w_a·x_a + w_b·x_b)/(w_a + w_b)` at that origin
  and bell, `K` re-read per H component. The merger also returns each
  body's offset per component, `x_a − x` and `x_b − x`; their
  holding-weighted sum is zero, and no residual is discarded. A merger
  across origins, across bells, across shapes, or where `w_a + w_b = 0`
  is refused by name.
- **Fission is the inverse of merger.** The merged point, the holdings
  and the offsets give back both bodies exactly, per component.
- **Re-origin across a throat** (LAW-PASSAGE v3, 78.172; `forms::passage`
  v4's `Poles::cross`). A body whose S component holds the throat plane's
  span `H − R`, at the frame state of hold `H` and reach `R`
  (`H·n_origin + R·n_∞`, the poles by role, 78.171 §1a), passes the
  throat into another enclosure:
  - S's span reverses, and `|S|` is kept;
  - T gains `−2·span`, the span before the pass;
  - the new origin reads reach and hold exchanged, with `H + R` kept and
    the face `δ = H/(H + R) ↦ 1 − δ`;
  - H and `K` are untouched.

  Within one enclosure the passage is the face exchange alone: the body is
  unchanged. A frame state whose span is not the body's S is refused by
  name.
- **Dilation invariance** (xylarium's joint fixture). The share is of
  degree zero in length: `δ(λH, λR) = δ(H, R)` for every `λ > 0`, and the
  flow between two dilated faces, `δ ⊕ (1 − δ)` with
  `x ⊕ y = (x + y)/(1 + x·y)` for pushes in `(−1, 1)`, equals the
  undilated one.

Ruled 2026-11-05, beside the law above:
- **The stamp law is kept: TUniversal stamps, never drives.** An act
  carries the last TUniversal final point read (Concordance 1.11), and
  that point stamps it; it never advances a body. The advance above runs
  on a universe's own bell `h`, under the universe's own rates, never on
  TUniversal's height. Where a universe pairs its bell with TUniversal, it
  does so by its chronometric anchors (MU-CHRONOMETRY §3), never by
  advancing on the network's clock.
- **An address routes, never identifies** (above).
- **Base 60 is display only** (above). `FORMS-COORDINATE` v2 decided
  `within` on the sector at a depth; v3 decides on the caller's
  intervals, and the sector is the display reading alone.
- **Lanes open beyond S, G, T and H** only by a ruled act agreed between
  kernels (above). The form states the type; the act is the next step.
- **`K` means closed orbits everywhere.** A body's `K` is the whole turns
  of each H component (above); a line's receipt index is `i`, never `K`.

## 2. What it replaces

- **Catalogue iteration.** Walking every body a kernel holds to find the
  ones near one body is replaced by a range on one component of the
  ordered key.
- **All-pairs loops.** Testing each pair of bodies for nearness is
  replaced by the same range: its cost grows by bisection steps with the
  bodies held, never with their count (fixture 6).
- **A floor on a grid.** Netstratum's `READING`, recorded in its docs/84
  as a floor on base 60 to depth 6 and never landed, is replaced by
  decisions on the caller's exact intervals, with the sexagesimal
  reading kept for display. `SPAN` and `TURNING`, recorded beside it, are
  not part of this law.
- **A scalar lane** (v1 and v2). One rational per lane folded H's planes
  into one number; v3 holds each plane, and the turning between planes,
  as a keyed component.
- **A turning keyed on a frame plane** (v3). v3 keyed H's phase on the
  frame plane `e_Z1Q1`, which is hyperbolic and holds no turning; v4
  keys it on the turning about the axis, `e₊₂e₊₃`.

## 3. The form

| Part | Held as | Never |
|---|---|---|
| The origin | `forms::coordinate::Origin`: genesis (32 bytes), epoch, the enclosures written out as `Local` coordinates, outermost first | A digest of the enclosures |
| A body's place | `Local { h, lanes: Lanes, k: Vec<u64> }`, `K` one count per H component in key order | One number for a lane; a float |
| A lane set | `Lanes`, a list of `(Lane, Vector)` in canonical order, built by `Lanes::of(lanes, &Openings)` or `Lanes::base(s, g, t, h)`; `Lane` is `S`, `G`, `T`, `H` or `Opened { name, version }` | A fixed `[_; 4]`; a lane opened by convention |
| The openings | `Openings::ruled()`, the ruled openings (none yet) | A list a caller states |
| A lane's vector | `Vector`, components `(Key, Q)` in the order of the keys written out, built by `Vector::of` | A map; components summed |
| A key | `Key::axis(Axis)` (`e_S1`) or `Key::generator(Axis, Axis)` (`e_+2+3`); `Axis::of(letter, index)`, or a frame unit `Axis::unit(Square, k)` (`+k`, `-k`); `Key::plane() → Plane`: `Turning(Square)`, `Frame`, `Mixed` or `Unsigned` | An axis of no writing; a turning keyed on a plane of opposite squares |
| The coordinate | `Coordinate { origin, local }`, ordered as the canonical key | A key ordered by its text past `h` |
| The writing | `origin/…/h:S:G:T:H:K`: genesis in 64 lowercase hex digits, `.`, epoch in 8; each enclosure and the body as `h` in 16 lowercase hex digits, each lane its vector (one component bare, `n/d` in lowest terms in lowercase hex, a negative numerator signed `-`; more as `(n/d,n/d)` in key order), any opened lane after H, and `K` (one count bare in 16 lowercase hex digits; more as `(…,…)`) | A digest; a rounded component |
| A decision | `Coordinate::within(&Bounds) → Sides`, `Bounds::of(origin, lane, [(Key, Interval)])`, `Interval { lo, hi }`; `Sides::whole() → Whole` | A sector deciding; a boolean |
| The display | `Sector { depth, index }`, `Sector::places`, `Sector::written` (`0;08,34,17`), `Coordinate::reading → Reading` (` × `) | A key, a comparison, a stored value |
| A neighbour range | `Bodies::range(at, lane, key, interval)` over the bodies held in canonical order, by two bisections; `Neighbours::filter(lane, key, interval)` | A pass over every body or every pair |
| The advance | `Coordinate::advance(rates, t)`, `Rates { lanes: Lanes }` keyed as the body; `K` re-read per H component | A step loop; a period assumed |
| An event's bell | `Coordinate::bell_of(lane, key, value, rates)`, the reading `EventBell`: `At(bell)`, exact; `Unmoving` (`r = 0`); `Behind { bell }` | A boolean; a bell rounded to a whole bell |
| The merger | `merge(a, w_a, b, w_b)`, the `Merger { point, offset_a, offset_b }`, each offset a lane set | A merged point whose offsets are discarded |
| Fission | `fission(merger, w_a, w_b)`, both bodies back | A body restored from a record of it |
| A passage | `Coordinate::pass(poles, frame, through, into) → Passed { body, frame, share }`, `Through { s, t }` the keys of S's span and T's flux | A reach moved; a sandwich on the coordinate |
| The flow | `flow(x, y) = (x + y)/(1 + x·y)` | A square read as a quantity |

The base 60 is the shell's `BASE`, classed `FORMAT` (display only) by the
numbers gate, and 360 is `DEGREES_PER_TURN`, classed `EXTERNAL` as the
degree's definition.

from the definitions in §1 alone, without linking `forms`. `R` is the

## 8. Proposed amendment: H's state held as the motion

**Status: Proposed** (2026-11-06), not ruled. The stored form in
`forms::coordinate` is unchanged until it is ruled: H stays per-plane
degrees (v4) in code. It is to be read with netstratum and xylarium and

### 8.1 Why: a per-plane reading does not give back the motion

Fixture 11b's law composes motions whole, as `R₁R₂` in the even algebra
(§5, 11b). The generator of a composite is not the per-plane values
composed into it:
- **Disjoint planes (11b-ii).** `½ e₊₁e₊₂` and `⅓ e₋₁e₋₂` compose to
  `R₁R₂ = 12/25 + 16/25 e₊₁e₊₂ + 9/25 e₋₁e₋₂ + 12/25 e₊₁e₊₂e₋₁e₋₂`, whose
  generator reads `4/7` and `3/7` per plane, never `½` and `⅓`. Recomposed
  plane by plane, those readings give another motion,
  `(33 + 56 e₊₁e₊₂)/65 · (20 + 21 e₋₁e₋₂)/29 = (660 + 1120 e₊₁e₊₂ + 693
  e₋₁e₋₂ + 1176 e₊₁e₊₂e₋₁e₋₂)/1885`. The generator held whole gives back
  `R₁R₂`, its volume `12/25 e₊₁e₊₂e₋₁e₋₂` kept.
- **Planes sharing a unit (11b-i).** The composite gains a part on the
  third plane, `±12/25 e₊₁e₊₃`, its sign fixed by the order. No
  assignment of one degree per plane holds an order-dependent part.

So H held as per-plane degree values loses the volume, and the turning
between planes, of any composite that is not a product of turnings on
pairwise disjoint planes read factor by factor.

### 8.2 The proposal

- **H's state is the even versor `R`** of the body's turnings, exact in
  the even subalgebra of `Cl(p, q)`, or equivalently its generator `B =
  (R − 1)(R + 1)⁻¹` where `R + 1` has an inverse (`R = −1` has no
  generator and is held as `R`). Its parts are keyed by blade, the
  turning planes' generators among them, and the volume's grade-4 blades
  beside them. Motions compose as `R₁R₂` (§5, 11b), never plane by plane.
- **The rate is a generator.** A body's rate on H is an even generator
  `B_ω` per bell. One bell's motion is `cayley(B_ω)`; over `n` bells the
  motion is that rotor composed `n` times, held as its base and `n`
  (netstratum docs/117 §11.4), its readings rendered on observation.
- **Degrees are a reading.** A plane's degrees are read from the held
  motion when asked, as certified sexagesimal sectors (`tan(θ/4) = b` on
  an elliptic plane), never stored, compared, summed or decided on
  (§1, base 60 display only).
- **Fixtures.** 11b (§5) and the recomposition of §8.1, as claims in
  `forms/src/tests/turning.rs` (`disjoint_planes_commute_and_keep_the_volume`).

### 8.3 Open joint points

- **The angle a degree names.** The Cayley motion of `b·e` on an elliptic
  plane is `R = cos φ + e·sin φ` with `tan(φ/2) = b`, and its sandwich
  `R·v·R̃` turns a vector of the plane by `2φ`. So `tan(θ/4) = b`, with
  `θ` the turning the vector undergoes, as netstratum reads it.
  LAW-FIELD's carrier (78.171 §2) holds a turning's motion one-sided, as
  `ζ = cos θ + B·sin θ`, so its angle is `φ` itself, half of `θ`. Neither
  convention changes in code. Coordinator's recommendation: the shared
  degree is the turning a vector undergoes (the sandwich angle), and the
  carrier's one-sided angle is restated as half of it.
- **A rational motion is a Pythagorean angle.** `R` is exact over ℚ when
  `tan(θ/4)` is rational, not when `θ` is a rational number of degrees.
  Of the rational rates, only `b = 0` and `b = ±1` (`0°` and `±180°`) are
  rational in degrees. This is the deeper reason to hold the state by
  rate: a state held in rational degrees has no exact motion to compose
  on planes that share a unit.
- **`K` per component.** A versor forgets whole turns (`R` at `θ` and at
  `θ + 720°` are one value). How `K`, the orbits closed, is held beside
  `R` is open.
- **The key and its ranges.** How `R` is written into the ordered key,
  and what a range on one component ranges over (a blade's coefficient of
  `R` or of `B`), is open.
- **The advance between whole bells and the merger.** The closed form
  `θ(h) + ω·(t − h)` and the holding-weighted mean of degrees have no
  stated counterpart on `R`; both are open.
- **Generators in six or more frame units.** The generator read back from
  a composite may carry a grade-6 part (its reverse is still its
  negation). The law names `B` a bivector, which holds in up to five
  units.
