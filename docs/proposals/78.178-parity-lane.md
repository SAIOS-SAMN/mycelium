# 78.178 — The parity lane P: the first opened lane

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the `forms` crate (`forms::coordinate`, LAW-COORDINATE v4, [78.173](78.173-law-coordinate.md); its ruled openings, `Openings::ruled()`, and the refusal `LaneNotOpened`); μ's modes (`economy::modes::Modes`; [MU-ECONOMY](../MU-ECONOMY.md) §1); every kernel whose motions include reflections. Cross-project: to be agreed with netstratum and xylarium.

**Status: Proposed** (2026-11-06). A lane beyond S, G, T and H opens
only by a ruled act agreed between kernels (78.173 §1, *Open lanes*;
MU-ECONOMY §1). This proposal is that act for the first opened lane,
taken through
[MU-RELEASE §3a](../MU-RELEASE.md#3a-how-a-hypha-standard-becomes-a-kernel-standard)
at step 2. The operator ruled on 2026-11-05 that reflections (improper
motions) get a lane of their own, the first opened lane, with the
proposed name P (parity), and that until P opens a mirrored closure's
pay is held as a claim, never paid without a mode (proposal 78.174 §1
item 11). The keying, the law version and the fixtures below are
proposed, not agreed; no code opens P until the peers' readings agree.

## 1. The law

- **P carries reflections.** An improper motion, a versor of odd grade,
  is carried by the reflections it was composed of: a reflection in a
  frame unit `n` is the sandwich `x ↦ −n·x·n⁻¹`. The base lanes carry no
  reflection: a reflection is never folded into S, G, T or H.
- **One component per reflecting normal.** P's components are keyed by
  the reflecting normal, one frame unit (78.173 §1, *axis*): `e_+k`,
  plane `k`'s positive unit, or `e_-k`, its negative unit. A key naming a
  plane (a generator of two axes) or an axis named by a letter is refused
  on P, `NotReflectionNormal`, naming the key.
- **The count is an exact signed integer.** A component counts the
  reflections in its normal that the motion traversed: each traversal
  forward adds `1`, each traversed backward subtracts `1`. A component
  with a denominator other than `1` is refused, `NotWhole`, naming the
  key and the value. The count is never reduced: two reflections in one
  normal compose to the identity motion and the component reads `2`, as
  `K` keeps the winding of H (78.173 §1).
- **Parity is a reading, never stored.** A component's parity is its
  count modulo 2, and the motion's own parity (proper or improper) is the
  versor's grade parity, read off the motion. Neither is a component, a
  field or a stored flag.
- **The advance, the merger and fission** hold on P per component as on
  every lane (78.173 §1), each component staying a whole number: a rate
  on P is whole per bell, a merger's offsets are whole where the weights
  give whole results and refused `NotWhole` naming the component
  otherwise.
- **The opening.** P is opened as the entry `("P", 5)` of
  `Openings::ruled()`: the lane named `P` at law version 5 of
  `FORMS-COORDINATE`, the version that admits it. A lane set holding P is
  ordered S, G, T, H, then P (78.173 §1, *The ordered key*). Before the
  entry is ruled, a lane set holding P is refused
  `LaneNotOpened { lane: Opened { name: "P", version: 5 } }`, as every
  unopened lane is today.
- **μ on P.** A closure carried by reflections pays the modes on P its
  reflections moved, a mode's amount its share of the frame (78.174 §1
  item 9). Until P opens, a mirrored closure's pay is held as a claim and
  never paid without a mode (78.174 §1 item 11).

## 2. Refusals

| Refusal | When |
|---|---|
| `LaneNotOpened` (existing) | A lane set holds P where `Openings::ruled()` has no entry `("P", 5)` |
| `NotReflectionNormal` (new) | A P component keyed by a generator or by a lettered axis, naming the key |
| `NotWhole` (new) | A P component, rate or merged value with a denominator other than `1`, naming the key and the value |

## 3. Joint-read fixtures

Every input and output is exact. Lanes are written as in 78.173 §5; P's
components are written `e_±k: n`.

1. **Not yet opened.** Under today's `Openings::ruled()` (no entry), the
   lane set `S, G, T, H, P` with `P = {e_+1: 1}` is refused
   `LaneNotOpened { lane: Opened { name: "P", version: 5 } }`.
2. **One reflection.** A body with `P = {}` carried by the reflection in
   `e₊₁` stands at `P = {e_+1: 1}`; the component's parity reads odd, and
   the motion reads improper.
3. **The count is kept.** Carried by the reflection in `e₊₁` twice, the
   body stands at `P = {e_+1: 2}`; the motion is the identity, the
   component's parity reads even, and the count is not reduced to `0`.
4. **Backward.** From `P = {e_+1: 2}`, the reflection in `e₊₁` traversed
   backward leaves `P = {e_+1: 1}`.
5. **Two normals.** Carried by the reflection in `e₊₁` then in `e₋₂`,
   the body stands at `P = {e_+1: 1, e_-2: 1}`, the keys in canonical
   order; the motion reads proper.
6. **A plane is no normal.** `P = {e_+1+2: 1}` is refused
   `NotReflectionNormal { key: e_+1+2 }`.
7. **Whole only.** `P = {e_+1: 1/2}` is refused
   `NotWhole { key: e_+1, value: 1/2 }`.
8. **The advance.** At bell `h = 4`, `P = {e_+3: 1}` under the rate
   `{e_+3: 2}` per bell, advanced to `t = 7`, stands at `P = {e_+3: 7}`.

## 4. Open items

- **A composite's other lanes.** Two reflections in distinct normals
  compose to a turning on their plane (fixture 5's motion is proper).
  Whether that composite also advances H by its degrees, beside P's
  counts, or whether P alone carries a motion spelled in reflections, is
  for the peers' reading.
- **The law version.** `5` names the `FORMS-COORDINATE` version that
  admits P; whether the opening carries a version of its own instead is
  for the peers' reading.
- **Room and the ask on P** follow MU-ECONOMY §12 items 5 and 8, open.
