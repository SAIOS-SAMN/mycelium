# 78.177 — From conversation to court: a held question closed by another mind, and a convergence, carried where a court reconstructs them

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents, and the courts verify what they produce. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the client (`mycelium-client`): its conversation carrier and its hypha carrier (MU-CLIENT §5.1, §5.3); the court's probe and attestation paths (`src/server/cognition/book/demand.rs`, `src/server/cognition/book/agreement.rs`). No game term.

**Status: Proposed** (2026-11-06), for issue #144 (MU-CLIENT §7.1,

## 1. The gap

Chatter is conversation: sealed, mind to mind (`chatter::send_over`,
`ask_over`; #154), and the hypha carries nothing between minds (78.161).
Contradiction is held between minds, and nothing heard enters a genome as
given (law 8). Two things reach a court from it today:

- **an agreed word**, by its own union (`hold_for_union`), asserted by its
  coiner with the hearer's signed HOLD; the court seals the attestation
  only on a word true on its scene, read on its own copy, and a
  convergence once, as the speaker's `Attest` (`attest_agreements`, K1–K2);
- **nothing else.** A question one mind holds (`Mind::question`, its
  residue on its own line) that another mind's answer closes
  (`take_answer`: the factors compose to the residue exactly) is learned
  by the asker (`rung::learn_by`) and ends there. E2 (78.168 §5) rules
  that it pays the citation; no evidence path carries it.

The court half of E2 is built for listed demands: a probe that closes a
demand vertex of another voice's listing pays the closer the cited word's
discharge on the listing's family (`Court::settle_demand` →
`pay_convergence`), the listing's own speaker paid nothing (5.7(d)).

## 2. The question: carried by acts that exist

Clients think, hyphas verify (law 8): the court never takes either mind's
word, and a mind never pays itself. The path uses only acts on the wire
now (MU-ANASTOME §6), so it is no wire change:

1. **The question stands under evidence.** Only a question raised on a
   heard word that carried its evidence (`chatter::Evidence`: the scene's
   32-byte citation and the word's record; V1) can be carried: a family
   the court does not hold measures nothing (E1). A question with no
   evidence stays the conversation's.
2. **The asker lists it.** The asker asserts its held question as a typed
   demand vertex of a sentence under that family (`ACT_SPEECH`, 23.8,
   50.5): the vertex's bodies are the links the heard word's section on
   the scene leaves open where the asker's own words do not reach. The
   asker is the listing's speaker.
3. **The closer probes it.** The mind whose answer the asker took probes
   that vertex (`ACT_PROBE`), citing the court-held words its answer
   composed of (`heard_citation`: exact, or nothing). The court speaks
   each cited form on the demand's bodies (`settle_demand`) and admits
   only where they reproduce the after side; closed, it pays the closer
   the cited word's discharge on the family (`pay_convergence`; E2). The
   asker, the listing's speaker, is paid nothing; the closer is paid
   only on another mind's listing.
4. **The closed thought's twist under its family.** Where the answer
   closed the asker's ring with a tilt (an echo of finite order), the
   asker asserts the closed ring as a sentence under the same family
   (E1), its twist content (MU-CLIENT §4.4(3)), paid as any install.

An answer whose factors are letters or atoms alone cites no court word:
it closes nothing at a court and is paid nothing; the asker holds what
it learned, and its residue may be adjoined as its own letter (§4.7).

## 3. The convergence: promotion and K3

A convergence is two words `W_A`, `W_B`, not one vertex, with the same
section on one scene (MU-CLIENT §5.5). The minds' side holds it (V1–V4);
the speaker asserts its word under the scene with the hearer's HOLD, and
the court reads both on its own copy and seals the convergence once
(K1–K2). Consilience is courts agreeing: a second court re-deriving the
same section is the 47.3 witness's reconstruction. What the court does
with a verified convergence is K3, and it is not decided here:

- **(a) the second vantage.** A verified convergence is the second vantage
  an install waits on (47.3): `W_A` installs on the court's reconstruction
  of `W_B`'s section, the two keys distinct (78.165);
- **(b) a share.** A verified convergence is paid a POWC share to the
  converging mind, out of the install's mint and never added to it (as
  POWV's share is, MU-ECONOMY §5.1), the first word's discoverer keeping
  its base share.

## 4. What a ruling must settle

1. **K3**: (a), (b), or both (§3).
2. **The demand's bodies** (§2 step 2): whether a chatter question is
   stated to the court as the open links of the heard word's section on
   its scene, or by another carrier of the residue.
3. **How the closer reaches the listing** (§2 step 3): by the court's
   quote (listings read by their chart, no router), or by the asker
   naming the listing in a chatter frame (a chatter field, a change to
   the minds' wire).
4. **Who speaks the closed twist** (§2 step 4): the asker alone, or the
   asker with the closer's signed HOLD as an agreement (E3).
