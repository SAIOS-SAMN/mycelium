## 2. What the review questions (a)–(d) find

**(a) The letter road is reached from speech only.** The road is:
- **Ineffable words.** `Entity::transcribe_taught` (`entity/think.rs`) returns ineffable motions.
- **Open sentence.** `hold_perceived_from` (`think.rs`) speaks them as an open sentence. The court hears it through `hear_open` (`court/winding.rs`, called from `book/speech.rs`).
- **Construction.** They are constructed by `construct_and_adjoin` (`think.rs`).
- **Pivot demands.** `Contemplation::docket_demand` (`bubble/docket.rs`) → `take_demands` (`docket.rs`) → `Entity::take_demands` (`think.rs`) → `construct_and_adjoin`. It is run each drive.

What feeds the docket: only pivots (`bubble/drive.rs`) and unspelled calluses (`entity/callus.rs`).

The task path's leftovers do reach `hold_perceived` (`speak.rs`; `hold_chain` in `think.rs`). But their Demand vertices only add **rotor** candidates at their chart: `bubble/hold.rs` → `contemplation.rs`, which keeps `w.rotor()`. The full transport `car.v` is dropped, and **nothing is docketed**. A demand-only form has no orbit, so no pivot ever dockets it either.

**Missing call:** in `bubble/hold.rs`'s `Vertex::Demand` arm, docket the demand with its whole transport as the residue. The class is `ClassVector::of_word` (`sentence/class.rs`) of the transport's word, formed as `callus.rs` forms one. The cover is `[chart]`. Then `take_demands` constructs it on the next drive (unit U6).

Taught words come from the sentences' composition, so a demand-only form teaches nothing and reaches `hear_open` only after a letter exists. The deformation, creation and source kinds have no transport where no core motion exists (`carriage_with_core` is None). Those leftovers have **no versor residue to construct**, which is (b)'s limit.

**(b) `construct_and_adjoin` beyond rotors.**
- **What it does.** It constructs any versor (`cga::construct`). Its `(None, Some(c))` arm stands a residue that turns nothing — a slide or a dilation — as a **motion letter** (`sentence/address.rs` `motion_letter_form`) in the dialect.
- **Where it stops:**
  1. A translation becomes a stood word, citable by glue, but never a **letter** a peel spells with. `adjoin_letters` takes `Adjoined = (alphabet::Rotor, Invariant)`. `letter_form` writes `Derivation::adjoined(rotor)` with the motion as carriage (`address.rs`). `factor_versor` / `peel_versor` spell the rotor and leave the rest to the member's carriage.
  2. With no chart the residue is dropped.
  3. A non-versor change (creation, source, deformation's ΔΒ) has no residue at all.
- **The lift, named and not supplied.** The letter's carrier goes from `alphabet::Rotor` to a full Cl(4,1) versor (the "word is one product" defect, 78.68). It touches `Derivation::adjoined`, `adjoin_letters`' `Adjoined` and the peel. That needs a ruling, not a wiring unit.
- **Open for ruling:** what a letter is for a change no versor carries. The flux readings exist as a candidate content (`family/flux.rs:perceive_fluxes`, 78.91).

**(c) Perception that pre-decides.** Three sites:
- `family/perceive.rs` (`if h != 0`): hue 0 is the void.
- `blade/body.rs` `components`: hue-blind, "every adjacency joins", so colours merge into one body.
- The same reading sits on the court (`statement.rs` perceives the same way; `Region::Interior`; `shells::enclosed_interior`).

Law 6 governs this: "the readings of the scene cover are governed as the letters are: a fixed roster, grown only by a court receipt on a probe." To make both perceived:
- the hue fibre's per-hue connectivity and the void become readings offered by **probe** to the court and ruled with a receipt;
- they must not be a code change on one side.

A unit until ruled: U13.

**(d) The seventeen** (`alphabet/letters.rs`).
- **What they are.** Identity, six 90° turns, six (3,4,5) and four (5,12,13) rotors. Equivalently, the Gaussian primes 1+i, 2+i and 3+2i on the pair planes of e0..e3.
- **Derivable.** Each is a product of two reflections in small lattice vectors, by Cartan–Dieudonné, which `cga::construct` already certifies:
  - (1,0)·(1,1) gives 90°;
  - (1,0)·(2,1) gives the (3,4,5) turn (tan 1/2 doubled is tan 4/3);
  - (1,0)·(3,2) gives the (5,12,13) turn.
  - So a mind that perceives body axes can construct them. The identity is the empty product.
- **What they pre-decide.**
  - *Which primes:* 2, 5 and 13, and not 17 = (4+i), etc.
  - *Which planes carry 13:* four of six.
  - *Slots:* rows on e0, columns on e1, frame on e3, orientation e2 (`slot_of_axis`, `letters.rs`).
  - *Motion is rotation:* no reflection, translation or dilation letter.

Under the rule, the seventeen are a seed the minds could re-derive. A test: construct each from two perceived reflections and check `projective_eq`. Reseeding with fewer letters needs a ruling; nothing is changed here.
