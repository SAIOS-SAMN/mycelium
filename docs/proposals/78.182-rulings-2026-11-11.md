# 78.182 — The operator's rulings of 2026-11-11

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the economy (`economy::modes`, the tier bond, MU-ECONOMY §1, §8); the court's holds and its idle park (`court_lock`, `exchange.rs`); the crossing record's X2 (`anastome::dialogue::crossing_record`); the tether (`physics::pivot_fibre`); LAW-COUPLING v2 (78.181); LAW-TRAVERSAL (78.176); the release model (MU-RELEASE §10); the derivation register (MU-DERIVATIONS §7).

**Status: Ruled** (mycelium's operator, 2026-11-11). This record gathers in one place the answers the operator gave to the questions open before v0.5.0. Each issue-bound ruling is also recorded on its issue. Where a ruling binds the peer kernels (§3), it is mycelium's side of a joint ruling: netstratum's and xylarium's operators concur or amend for their kernels before it binds them.

## 1. Rulings that block v0.5.0 work

1. **The tier bond (#237).** A carried line is admitted only while its tier's bond covers its exposure on every active lane, `B_l ≥ X_l`, where `X_l` is the exposure the slash reads (MU-ECONOMY §8). It is refused `Underbonded { lane, bond, exposure }` otherwise. The bond's strain reads the exposure `X_l`, not the realized defect (MU-DERIVATIONS D2.1 O1), so the admission gate and the strain read one denominator.
2. **Modes for S's creation share and H's shell count (#256).** Live closures are never held at the founding mode, which is for genesis and bootstrap endowment only. S's creation share is held at `Mode::SymbolCreation { from_ground, to_symbol }`, and H's shell count at `Mode::ShellBoundary { boundary_class }`, keyed by the closed shell's boundary class (MU-ECONOMY §1).
3. **The tether (#250).** `length2` and `capacity` retire. The tether's strength becomes the first-degree held face share `c_ij = (m_i + m_j)/(r_ij + m_i + m_j) = 1/(1 + t_ij)`, with `t_ij = r_ij/(m_i + m_j)` and `r_ij` the L1 axial reach across the active planes. Its projection becomes the ratio of first-degree reach poles, `μ(X) = t_jX/t_iX = m_i·r_jX/(m_j·r_iX)`.
4. **X2 (#160).** The record's versor is read as its turns on the frame's planes and checked by `verify_turns`. A versor with no exact reading as turns on a plane is refused `NotTurning { plane }`. Point lifts stay refused.
5. **A wait behind a long hold (#221).** Each court slot carries an atomic, lock-free `StrokeMeter`, `(stroke_limit, spent_work)`, which the holding thread updates at each metered step. A waiter reads it without a lock. If the holder has spent past its stroke, the waiter does not block: it is answered `Deferred { holder, spent, stroke }`, and the holder is flagged for preemption at its next yield point.
6. **The idle court (#234), option (a).** With a next due event, the court parks until it, or until an inbound wake. With none, it parks unbounded on the wake handle.

## 3. Joint items (mycelium's side)

9. **LAW-COUPLING v2 (#241).**
   - **Spread bodies:** each isoclinic plane carries its own first-degree hold share `W_p` (`Σ W_p = 3W`), its own reach `r_p` and its chiral bivector pair. The quarter-turn exchange runs on each plane's own chart, and the cross-plane transfer is weighted by the first-degree share `α_p = W_p/3W`, never by an L2 norm across planes.
   - **The refusal** replacing `NotFrames` is `UnbodiedDipole { pole_plane }`.
10. **The basket entry (#238).** `(protons Z, neutrons Q, bound_electrons E_bound = 3Z − U, mass M = 3(Z + Q))`, with the invariants recorded on #238.
11. **The economic law form (78.174 amendment).** Three rules generate the shared fixtures:
    - **μ's modes:** every balance is keyed by (tier, mode) across S, G, T and H, never netted across lanes.
    - **The reserve:** it holds the convergent face share `c_k = M_k/(r_k + M_k)` as locked backing, and releases the divergent share `d_k = r_k/(r_k + M_k)` into circulation.
    - **Conversion at the core tube:** it executes at the involution's fixed point, `t_k = 1` (`c_k = d_k = 1/2`), with no slippage and no quadratic fee.
12. **The pole-plane names (#239).** Approved across all three repositories: pole plane, pole unit, `PassageState { hold, reach }`.
13. **LAW-TRAVERSAL's open items (78.176 §5).**
    - **Polarity.** Each kernel declares its polarity in the tube's opening to its peer: a sign dipole per pole plane, or a vector of exact shares in `(−1, 1)`. The declaration is countersigned into the local court's record.
    - **The traversal's writing** (v0.5.0) is one act carried in an envelope: the tube, the bell epoch, the pole plane, the `PassageState { hold, reach }` exactly, the twist's four exact components, and the payload's commitment. A digest here only commits (MU-ANTI-FLATTENING 13); the payload is carried whole beside it.
    - **Alignment** is read once per tube, since one kernel holds tubes at different reaches to different peers.
    - **The antipolar face:** mycelium's position is that xylarium's core is the canonical antipolar face (`t ↦ 1/t`, the conjugate twist), with the mind kernel on the continuum face. Xylarium's operator states it for xylarium (78.176 §1).

## 4. The release model (MU-RELEASE §10)

14. **The quorum of a superposition:** coverage across the union of the superposed states' active bond lanes (`max_b X_{l,b} ≤ B_l`). It collapses to one branch only when a branch's weight share passes the throat's balance, above 1/2.
15. **The shared origin after a fork:** the branch holding the greater conserved hold at the fork's bell keeps the origin. The lighter branch re-roots as a child at that bell.
16. **A release changing address derivation:** a genesis boundary, a major epoch. It needs a sealed bridge snapshot (the `PassageState` exported), never an in-place merge.
17. **μ across branches:** μ minted after a fork is branch-local. It carries back only where its full proof is reconstructed and admitted again against the target branch's state.

19. **D1.3 O2:** plain TCP stays for loopback and for one-host workstations. Across an external boundary an authenticated tube is required.
20. **D1.4 O2:** engineering numbers go in a sibling register beside `OPEN_PHYSICS`, which keeps only the physical invariants.

23. **D4.3 O1:** a `0 × 0` grid is refused at the boundary (`EmptyGrid`); the least grid is `1 × 1`.
24. **D4.8 O1:** `Encl`'s width is strictly positive. A zero-width enclosure is a point and is refused.
25. **D4.8 O2:** `gamma_p`, `erf` and `poisson_quantile` retire. They have no callers, and they are continuous approximations outside the exact kernel.
