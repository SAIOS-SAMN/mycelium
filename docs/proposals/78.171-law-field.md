## 1. The law, in one sentence

A field is a curved, nested manifold whose geometry is produced by the
orbits it holds. Its sites are held by phases within the charts of its
atlas, each in degrees on a named plane with its winding, and the
transitions between charts (versors of `Cl(p, q)`, the signature read per
plane) are its connection. It curves into a well around each attractor
whose local orbit it holds and is flat where nothing is held. A membrane,
the throat's fixed sphere, encloses it where it encloses, and infinity
stands only inside it. Each orbit is read by the holonomy of its
transitions per lap, in degrees on its plane with its winding, and its
open readings are held as a vortex. Nothing in it is a scalar except the
gauge of a one-dimensional reading. A sphere stands in it only as a
holding's own enclosure; no circle, sphere or torus is a reference
surface it is measured against.

## 1a. The poles, the signature and the membrane

- **A plane's units and poles** (amendment (a); 78.172 §1; netstratum
  docs/117 §10.1). A plane is held by its two units, `e₊` (`e₊² = +1`) and
  `e₋` (`e₋² = −1`). Its poles are the null directions
  ```
  n_Z = e₊ + e₋   (closed),     n_Q = e₊ − e₋   (open)
  n_Z² = n_Q² = 0,              n_Z·n_Q + n_Q·n_Z = 4
  ```
  Origin and infinity are these two poles, each up to its gauge. Where
  this law writes `e₀` or `e∞`, it means a plane's pole on this basis,
  never a fixed pair of generators beside the planes.
- **The poles named by role** (agreed with netstratum 2026-11-05,
  docs/117 §10.3). The origin is the wholly closed pole and infinity the
  wholly open one:
  ```
  n_origin = n_Z = e₊ + e₋   (wholly closed, the singularity)
  n_∞      = n_Q = e₊ − e₋   (wholly open)
  ```
  Mycelium's standard conformal labels, `e∞ = e₊ + e₋` and
  `e_o = ½(e₋ − e₊)`, differ from these by `e₋ ↦ −e₋`: under it the
  standard `e∞` is `n_∞` and the standard `e_o` is `−½·n_origin`, the
  origin at gauge `−½`. This is the one place the map is stated; 78.171,
  78.172 and 78.173 name the poles by role, `n_origin` and `n_∞`, and
  `n_Z`, `n_Q` only beside them as netstratum's names.
- **Which pole is the origin rides the state** (amendment (b)).
  Exchanging the two poles is `e₋ ↦ −e₋`, the pole swap of 78.172. Where a
  plane's negative pole is chosen per state, the labelling of origin and
  infinity rides the state, and the metric stays fixed per plane: each
  value carries its negative mask (`algebra::Clifford`,
  `transducer::cga::Mv::negatives`), and a product reads each generator's
  square from it.
- **The membrane is the throat's fixed sphere** (amendments (a), (d)).
  The throat, `H = R`, is the frame state on `e₊` alone: the fixed sphere
  of the pole swap, not null (78.172 §1). It is the field's membrane. A
  frame state is null only at a pole. Infinity is held only inside an
  enclosure, at a pole of a plane the membrane encloses; a point needing
  it outside every enclosure is refused `Unenclosed`.
- **Spheres are enclosures only** (amendment (d), operator ruling). A
  sphere enters the law only as a holding's own enclosure: the throat's
  fixed sphere, the membrane. Netstratum's `S⁷ ⊂ S¹¹` (docs/117 §4) reads
  as nested enclosures, `S¹¹ = S⁷ ∗ S³` the riding state's enclosure about
  the machine's. No sphere, circle or torus is a surface a phase, a
  distance or a curvature is measured against.

## 3. The readings

1. **Holonomy:** the product of an orbit's transitions over one lap,
   read by its modes: where elliptic, an exact angle in degrees on its
   named plane, held with its winding in ℤ, and its order (MU-RECURSION
   10.3, 12.6).
2. **Monodromy of a candidate:** flat (order 1), torsion (order `ℓ`) or
   rupture (infinite order), read in `Spin(p, q)` of the values' own
   signature.
3. **An orbit:** a lap through the base charts and a fibre winding (ℤ),
   the phase advanced in degrees and its winding kept. Its shear is
   alignment toward the motion its attractor compels, and its precession
   per lap is the residue of that lap's transitions. The shear is
   netstratum docs/121's: the coupling on the turning lane H, the
   difference in degrees a bell between a body's rate and its range's
   field's rate, composed by the flow law (docs/121 §4, §10.4, §11);
   attraction and repulsion are one law and its inverse, the sign read
   from how the dipoles stand (docs/121 §3, §10.1–§10.2). A path with
   zero precession is a loop, refused.
4. **Axiomatic gravity:** the local orbit's shear on the orthogonal orbit
   class, read from the orbits held.

## 4. The refusals

| Refusal | When |
|---|---|
| `NotExact` | A motion or angle the carrier cannot hold exactly; the residue is named |
| `Loop` | An orbit with zero precession, returning exactly |
| `Unenclosed` | A point, motion or reading that needs a plane's pole as infinity (`e∞`) outside every enclosure |
| `ScalarCollapse` | A reading that collapses a multivector to one number other than the gauge of a one-dimensional reading |
| `SignatureRefusal::Mismatched` (the algebra) | Two values whose signatures disagree on a generator both hold, naming those generators; never coerced |

## 5. The wire

- The rank `n`.
- For each point: each phase in degrees on its named plane, an exact
  rational in the rational lane codec (MU-ECONOMY §1), with its winding
  `K`, then the nesting levels.
- An orbit's lap: its charts and transitions in order, each transition a
  versor over its cyclotomic field, with the signature per plane as the
  value carries it.
- A reading: its typed value (an angle in degrees on its plane, a
  winding, an order).
- Conformance is by decoded exact values (Concordance 1.4).

## 6. Joint-read fixtures

Each fixture is an input and its exact decoded output. Every kernel must
reach the same objects. Angles are in degrees on the plane named; the
plane `e₊₁e₊₂` is the turning between planes 1 and 2, whose two units
square alike, so it holds a rotor (netstratum docs/117 §10.3). Each angle
`θ` is held with its winding `K = ⌊|θ|/360⌋`.

1. **One lap, three transitions.** One lap of an orbit through three
   charts whose transitions are rotors by 30° each on `e₊₁e₊₂`: the
   product is the rotor by `3 × 30° = 90°` on `e₊₁e₊₂`, exact over
   `ℚ(ζ₁₂)`. The holonomy (the precession per lap) is 90° on `e₊₁e₊₂`,
   `K = 0`, of order 4.
2. **A flat path.** A path through a flat region of the field: holonomy
   0° on every plane, `K = 0`, no precession, so it is a loop and is
   refused `Loop`.
3. **Five fifths.** A rotation by 72° on `e₊₁e₊₂` composed five times:
   the motion is the identity, exact over `ℚ(ζ₅)`; the angle composed is
   `5 × 72° = 360°`, held with its winding `K = 1`, never read back as 0°.
4. **A phase taken apart.** A phase of period 9 taken apart as `3 · 3`,
   coarsest first: site 7 = `2·3 + 1` maps to `(2/3, 1/3)`, each of its
   own level's period. On `e₊₁e₊₂` that is 240° at the coarse level and
   120° at the fine level, each `K = 0`; recomposed, `240° + 120°/3 =
   280° = (7/9)·360°`.
5. **Two candidates.** Two candidates over one demand with monodromy 0°
   and 120° on `e₊₁e₊₂` (orders 1 and 3): one flat, one torsion with
   period 3.
6. **Infinity outside an enclosure.** A point that needs a plane's pole
   as infinity (`e∞`, on the pole basis `n_∞`, `n_Q` in netstratum's
   names, or `n_origin` where the state's pole swap labels it so) where
   no membrane encloses it: refused `Unenclosed`. The same pole inside an
   enclosure is a lawful frame state, for example `4/5·n_∞` (78.172
   fixture 3).

## 8. Open questions

- *Settled (ruled 2026-11-04):* μ is the metabolic cost of traversal, the
  share of the frame a change covers. Holonomy is content, adjudicated and
  never paid (MU-SENSING §3.7).
- *Settled:* a phase's denominator is its axis's extent, so the wire's
  `u16` extent field bounds it, and a value past it is refused, never
  widened (MU-SENSING §6).
- *Open, for the joint reading:* the plane a sample's axis turns on at
  rank `n`. Mycelium's lift (stage 1) adds one frame plane, the lift's
  own, `n + 1`, and turns axis `i` on `e₊ᵢe₊₍ₙ₊₁₎`, so no axis is
  privileged and a rank-1 axis stands on `e₊₁e₊₂` as §6 names it.
  LAW-COORDINATE v4's turning "in plane k" (the plane of the other two,
  3-space duality) holds a body's H at three planes and is not this
  reading. The convention is mycelium's until the reading rules it.
- *Open, for the joint reading:* the product of turnings on two planes
  that share one unit. It is exact as a versor but turns on a third plane
  whose angle is in general no rational number of degrees; mycelium's
  stage-1 carrier refuses it `NotExact`, naming both planes, and S7 stage
  2 composes it.
- *Open:* the wire carries `Cl(n+1, 1)` values and no signature today
  (MU-SENSING §3.7). Carrying the signature per plane (§5) is a wire
  change, stated as a *Network contract* before it is built.
