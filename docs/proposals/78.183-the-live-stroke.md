# 78.183 — The live stroke: a per-lane, fluid reading of the reserve, never a number sealed at boot

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes, each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts). In this document a *stroke* is the metered work a court or a mind may do on one turn of a carrier, and the *reserve* is the per-lane metabolism that pays for it. Nothing here is a claim about physical energy or real minds.
>
> **Components:** `physics::Budget`, `physics::Reserve`, `physics::Physics` (stroke, regeneration, pressure, calibration), the court lock's hold stroke and `StrokeMeter` (`server::court_lock`, `physics::meter`), every metered read under them, and the mind's metabolism (`client::sdk::mind`).

**Status: ruled 2026-11-13** (§7). Scoped at the operator's direction of 2026-11-12 ("scope it in its entirety so that you have the correct resolution and definition"); the semantic alignment and the answers to §6 are ruled in §7. Nothing in this document is built yet; the build is #308.

## 1. The defect, as the tree stands (main at 2b06f077)

1. **The stroke is a scalar sealed at boot.** `Physics::stroke()` mints `Budget { remaining: capacity / 8 }`. `capacity = ⌊M_proc / b⌋` is calibrated once at boot (`physics::calibrate`, `SEALED`) and never moves. Minting draws nothing from the reserve, and every stroke is the same size whatever the court's or mind's state.
2. **`Budget` carries no lane.** `spend()` takes one undifferentiated read: 108 call sites, with 373 parameters of type `&mut Budget`. Chapter 65.4 already names this "a scalar standing where a vector belongs", and 65.5 records that `Budget` and `Reserve` are "not yet joined" (PHY1, Open).
3. **The live, per-lane quantities never size a stroke.** These already exist and move with state:
   - the reserve `[S, G, T, H]`;
   - pressure, deficit and shortfall per lane;
   - the yield surface, room and occupancy;
   - the wells' escrow;
   - cohesion `c_k` and fluidity `d_k` per plane.

   Today they gate draws, price reads and pace dwells. None of them is read when a stroke is minted. Cohesion has no production reader at all, only tests.
4. **Strokes nest unmetered.** 34 court methods and 33 mind methods mint their own full stroke inside a call, and 39 leaf helpers mint `Physics::default().stroke_n(need)`. A court hold's one stroke (78.153b) therefore stands beside many fresh strokes on the same thread.
5. **The meter flattens.** `StrokeMeter` compares one scalar `spent` (every read on the holding thread, any token) against one scalar `stroke` (the hold's). An overrun is one bit, and the waiter is told one number.
6. **The proxy that exposed it.** Capping a hypha's memory (`MemoryMax=160M`) shrank its sealed capacity 47-fold. That changed the scalar's size, not its nature, and no hold overran. The capped hypha was killed, and on restart re-signed places it had signed. That second fault is #273's (rejoin before signing) and is outside this scope.

## 2. What is already ruled

- **Lanes.** μ⃗ = (S, G, T, H) is never summed, ordered or reduced to a scalar (law 5; R6). A draw is a vector, all or nothing, refused naming the lane, and an action with a zero projection on a depleted lane stays affordable (65.4; 78.66 PHY1). Only the lanes a motion moved are drawn, the lanes being the kinds of its modes (78.71; MU-CLIENT §4.1–§4.2). Rate is per lane (MU-ECONOMY §3.3). Each of the four covers has its own stroke budget (42.6).
- **Moving with state.** A governing quantity is a live reading, derived once per change of its inputs (MU-STANDARDS 2.12; MU-DERIVATIONS §0.5). Velocity is a reading and a period is measured (45.7). A construction's stroke is read off its residue (`modes_need`; 78.111, 78.116; D2.5 O1 (2), D3.1). Regeneration is by return only, never by a floor, a timer or a constant (78.42(6)). An idle court spends nothing (MU-EQUATIONS §7.6). The mind rests when short on a lane (MU-CLIENT §4.12). Hardware sets rate, never content (72.1e, A.18).
- **Fluidity.** The held share `c_k = M_k/(r_k + M_k) = 1/(1 + t_k)` and the open share `d_k = ν(t_k)` are read per plane, never summed (MU-CLIENT §1; MU-ANASTOME §9.4).
- **The hold.** A hold is taken with one stroke (78.153b). A waiter behind a hold that has spent past its stroke is answered at once, and the holder is preempted (78.182 item 5).

## 4. The definition proposed

1. **A stroke is a draw, not a mint.** A carrier's turn draws its stroke from the reserve as a vector over the lanes its work will move (PHY1). It draws nothing on a lane it will not move. `Budget` and `Reserve` are joined: a `Budget` is the portion of the reserve drawn for one turn, per lane, and it returns to the reserve what the turn did not spend.
2. **A read spends on its lane.** Each metered read names the lane of the mode it moves, and spends there. A read that moves nothing spends nothing. Where a read's lane cannot be named, it is a defect to fix at its site, never a default lane.
3. **The size of a stroke is a reading, per lane.** On each lane the stroke is the work's own need where a closed form exists (`modes_need`, D2.5 O1 (2), 78.111). Otherwise it is the reserve's live reading on that lane (Q1 chooses which). It is never `capacity / 8` sealed at boot.
4. **The reserve moves with state.** It depletes per lane as strokes are drawn and regenerates by return (78.42(6)): an orbit's close, a lapsed escrow, a minted closure. There is no fixed regeneration unit, and a lane strains under its own pressure (78.73). Capacity, the bound the reserve cannot exceed, is read from the process's memory as a live reading (Q2), not sealed.
5. **Fluidity enters as the open share.** On each plane the court's or mind's open share `d_k` is how much of its held reserve a turn may draw, and the held share `c_k` is what stays bound (Q6 decides whether this binds the stroke, or only reads beside it).
6. **Nesting is one draw.** A method called under a stroke spends that stroke and never mints its own. The 34 court and 33 mind self-mints, and the 39 `Physics::default().stroke_n` leaves, take the caller's `&mut Budget`. A leaf whose need is closed-form draws that need from the caller's stroke, on its lane.
7. **An overrun is read per lane.** The meter carries the hold's stroke and its spend per lane. An overrun is the set of lanes past their portion, with each one's amount. A waiter is deferred only where the hold has overrun a lane the waiter's own act needs, and is told those lanes. A holder is preempted on the lanes it overran, and keeps working on the others (65.4: "goes on working in the ones it still holds").

## 6. Questions for the ruling

1. **Q1, the unit and the size per lane.** Is a stroke's size on a lane the work's need where one exists in closed form, and otherwise:
   - (a) the reserve's live holding on that lane;
   - (b) the open share `d_k` of it (Q6);
   - (c) something else?

   What is the unit of a read: one metered step on the lane of the mode it moves, or the mode's own amount?
2. **Q2, capacity.** Is capacity read live, once per change of its inputs (MU-STANDARDS 2.12), or sealed at boot (D2.5, 72.1d)? If live, which inputs: the process's memory as it stands, the occupancy, the held escrows?
3. **Q3, regeneration.** Is it by return only, which retires `regen_unit`, `ceiling_l`'s sum across lanes and "an idle orbit spends the tick"? Which returns count: an orbit's close, a lapsed escrow, a minted closure, others?
4. **Q4, memory and the reserve.** 78.168 §4 forbids holding a thought by drawing the reserve, yet 25 door 3 and `return_escrow` do exactly that. Which stands, and if 78.168, what bounds a hold instead?
5. **Q5, the hold.** Does a hold keep one stroke (78.153b), now drawn per lane, with a waiter deferred only on the lanes the hold overran? Or does a hold draw per act within it?
6. **Q6, fluidity and the stroke.** Does the open share `d_k` per plane bind how much of the reserve a turn may draw, with the held share `c_k` the part that stays bound? Or is cohesion a reading beside the stroke that never sizes it? If it binds, how do planes map to lanes: are the lanes the planes' kinds, as 78.182 item 18's faces suggest (G and T sphere faces, S and H flat faces)?
7. **Q7, order.** Should #273's rejoin before signing land first? Today a crash makes an honest hypha equivocate, which burns its bond.

## 7. Ruled 2026-11-13

**The direction** (operator): physics, not laws. The stroke is built on what
the hypha already serves (MU-HOSTING §5a.2a): the flow law `x ⊕ y` and its
inverse (FORMS-COUPLING `flow`, `relative`), the keep `x* + kⁿ(x₀ − x*)`,
and the faces (`Parcel`, held and open share). It must be semantically one
thing and smooth across the coordinate `origin/h:S:G:T:H:K`.

**7.1 One name, one meaning.** "Stroke" named three categories: a quantity
of work, a unit of time and a pass of a loop. It now names one:

| Before | After | On the coordinate |
|---|---|---|
| Stroke as work (`Budget`, a sealed scalar `C/8`) | **Stroke**: the reserve's motion for one act, a lane vector on `S:G:T:H`, each lane drawn from its open share, nested strokes composed by the flow law, regenerated by return along the keep | a motion of the lanes |
| Stroke as time (waits, periods, lapse and aperture "in strokes") | the **bell** `h` for mesh time (TUniversal); **`K`** for a carrier's own count of closed orbits | `h`, `K` |
| Stroke as a pass of a loop (court stroke, `Circuits::stroke`, the mind's stroke) | **cycle** (the court's pass), **winding** (the mind's pass), **circuit** step (a carrier's) | — |
| `park_floor` | **park floor**: the scheduler's bound, not physics | — |
| calibration stroke | **calibration**: the boot reading of memory and a read's size | — |

"Draw" is not used for the stroke: the GLOSSARY gives it to what attracts
agents to a universe. The minimal stroke keeps its name, as the least
stroke in the ruled sense.

**7.2 Smoothness.** A stroke is never cut into sealed quanta.
- **Its size follows the load.** On each lane it follows the lane's open
  share as the share moves.
- **Nested strokes compose by `⊕`.** An inner stroke is bounded by its
  lane and metered as part of its caller's, never minted.
- **Regeneration is continuous.** It approaches the lane's rest along
  the keep, with no regeneration unit.
- **One motion, three readings.** The same lane vector moves a body's
  coordinate, the reserve and the ledger, so the three agree.

**7.3 The answers to §6.**
- **Q1:** the size on a lane is the work's need where a closed form exists. Otherwise it is (b), the open share `d_k` of the lane's holding. A read is one metered step on the lane of the mode it moves.
- **Q2:** capacity is read live, once per change of its inputs (the process's memory as it stands), never sealed.
- **Q3:** regeneration is by return only, along the keep. The returns are an orbit's close, a lapsed escrow and a minted closure. `regen_unit` and "an idle orbit spends the tick" retire.
- **Q4:** not settled by this ruling; it is taken up at P4 with its own reading.
- **Q5:** a hold keeps one stroke, drawn per lane. A waiter is deferred only on the lanes the hold overran, and told them (65.4).
- **Q6:** the open share binds: `d_k` sizes what a turn may draw, and the held share `c_k` stays bound. The lanes read the planes' faces as 78.182 item 18 states them.
- **Q7:** done. The rejoin before signing landed first (#315).
