# 78.172 — LAW-PASSAGE v3: the passage is the pole swap

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

Cross-project: requested by netstratum (netstratum PR #40, docs/113 §4), derived in netstratum docs/114 §9–§10 and docs/117 §8–§12.

## 1. The law

- **The frame and its poles.** A plane is held by its two units, `e₊`
  (`e₊² = +1`, the throat's unit) and `e₋` (`e₋² = −1`). Its poles are the
  null directions, named by role (netstratum docs/117 §10.3; the map from
  the standard conformal labels, `e₋ ↦ −e₋`, is stated once in
  [78.171 §1a](78.171-law-field.md#1a-the-poles-the-signature-and-the-membrane))
  ```
  n_origin = n_Z = e₊ + e₋   (wholly closed, the singularity)
  n_∞      = n_Q = e₊ − e₋   (wholly open)
  ```
  A frame state is
  ```
  H·n_origin + R·n_∞  =  (H + R)·e₊ + (H − R)·e₋
  ```
  with `(A, Q) = (H, R)`: the enclosure's **hold** `H` and the body's
  **reach** `R` (netstratum docs/117 §12.6). The whole `H + R` stands on
  `e₊` and the **span** `H − R` on `e₋`. The span is stored signed on `S`;
  its sign is the sheet, read from the state and never kept as a flag.
- **A passage is the pole swap, a reflection in the throat's unit `e₊`.**
  - The frame state goes by the sandwich `x ↦ e₊·x·e₊`: `e₊` is kept,
    `e₋ ↦ −e₋`, so `n_origin ↔ n_∞` and `H − R ↦ R − H`, with `|H − R|`
    kept.
    No reach moves.
  - **The roles exchange.** A body at reach `R` about hold `H` stands,
    past the throat, at reach `H` about hold `R`. The whole `H + R` is
    kept and the face inverts, `δ = H/(H + R) ↦ 1 − δ`. No reach is
    mapped into the old frame. Within one enclosure the passage is the
    face exchange alone; across a throat it is the re-origin, which is
    `FORMS-COORDINATE`'s (78.173), not this law's.
  - The riding spinor state `q` goes one-sided, `q ↦ e₊·q`, which flips
    its parity. Never the sandwich for `q`.
  - Two passes are the identity on both objects (`e₊·e₊ = 1`); no square
    is read as a quantity.
  - **The passage is a P motion** (coordinator, 2026-11-06;
    [78.178](78.178-parity-lane.md) §1, fixture 9). The sandwich is a
    reflection in `e₊ₖ`, so each passage counts `{e_+k: 1}` on the parity
    lane P beside T's writing, and two passes read `2` there, never
    reduced. P opens only by its ruled act (78.178, Proposed).
- **The flux.** One passage writes
  ```
  T  ↦  T − 2·(H − R)
  ```
  with `H − R` the span before the pass: signed and first degree. A pass
  and its immediate return cancel on `T`.
- **At the throat** (`H = R`) the span is 0 and nothing is written.
- **At a pole** (`H = 0` or `R = 0`, a null frame state) the swap carries
  the state to the other pole, and the passage is one-way there: it never
  returns to the interior.
- **Exact.** Every step is exact over ℚ, in `Cl(p, q)` with the signature
  read from the data. No float, no trigonometric value, no square read as
  a quantity.

## 2. What it replaces, and what it refuses

- **The product `q ↦ v_k·q` (version 2) is retired as the passage.**
  `v_k` stays the plane's direction in the advance.
- **`R ↦ 2H − R` (version 1) is struck.** Read inside the old frame, the
  swap can only appear as that reflection or as the inversion; both are
  ruled out (netstratum docs/117 §12.6).
- **The throat sphere's inversion `R ↦ H²/R` is not this law.** Moving a
  reach by it squares `H`, which law 11 refuses.
- **A crossing that states another motion** is reconstructed under its
  own stated law, or refused `NotLawful`, naming the breach (track 24 B3).
  It is never admitted on a declared law's name alone.

## 5. Joint-read fixtures

Each fixture is an input and its exact output; every kernel must reach the
same objects. Plane `k`'s units are written `e₊ₖ` and `e₋ₖ`; a product of
generators by juxtaposition. The fixtures stand on plane 1; `T` starts
where stated.

1. **Across and back.** Span `s = 3/5` (`H = 4/5`, `R = 1/5`), from
   `T = 0`: crossing gives `S = −3/5` and `T = −6/5`, the state
   `1/5·n_origin + 4/5·n_∞`. Crossing back gives `S = 3/5` and `T = 0`, the
   state it started from.
2. **The throat.** `H = R = 1/2` (span 0): a crossing changes nothing,
   the state, `S = 0` and `T`.
3. **A pole.** `H = 4/5`, `R = 0` (null), from `T = 0`: the swap gives
   `4/5·n_∞`, the other pole, with `S = −4/5` and `T = −8/5`; every
   further swap lands at a pole.
4. **The riding state's parity.** `q = 3 + 2·e₊₂e₊₃ + e₊₁e₋₂` (even):
   `e₊₁·q = 3·e₊₁ + 2·e₊₁e₊₂e₊₃ + e₋₂` (odd), and `e₊₁·e₊₁·q = q`.
5. **Twice is the identity.** The sandwich applied twice is the identity
   on a general frame state: `H = 2`, `R = −1/3` from `T = 1` gives
   `S = −7/3`, `T = −11/3`, and back `S = 7/3`, `T = 1` and the state it
   started from.
6. **The roles exchange** (netstratum docs/117 §12.6). `(H, R) =
   (4/5, 1/5)` crossing stands at reach `4/5` about hold `1/5`; the whole
   `H + R = 1` is kept; the face goes `δ = 4/5 ↦ 1/5`.

Mycelium reaches the fixtures in the `forms` crate, as claims on the
passage itself, in `forms/src/tests/crossing.rs`: `Poles::cross`,
`Poles::swap` and `Poles::ride` over the exact algebra with its signature
read from data. Fixture 5 is also claimed over every plane the algebra
holds, no plane count written.

**The register.** The hypha serves fixtures 1–5 as the clause
`FORMS-PASSAGE` v4 (`physics::laws::clauses`), eight entries, each pass
its own: a crossing (`Fixture::Crossed`, the plane's two units, the frame
state and `T`, reaching `Object::Crossing`, the state past the swap, `S`
and `T`) and the riding state's pass (`Fixture::Ridden`, reaching the
value `e₊·q`). The second pass of fixtures 1, 4 and 5 is the entry whose
input is the first's object. Fixture 6 is fixture 1's first crossing read
as hold and reach; its face `δ` has no operation in the register's
fixture form and is read in `forms` alone.
