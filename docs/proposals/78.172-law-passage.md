## 1. The law

A crossing moves a reach `R` through a throat of reach `H` by the
**linear passage**:

```
R  ↦  2H − R
```

- It is the reflection of the reach about `H` on one line: first degree
  and exact over ℚ. It never squares (law 11).
- It is an involution: two passages return the reach, `2H − (2H − R) = R`.
  The reverse crossing is the same law.
- `H` is its one fixed point: a reach at the throat stays there.
- On a share, the same passage reads `δ ↦ 1 − δ`, the reflection about
  `½`, also first degree.
- The number is the gauge of a one-dimensional reading, the reach along
  one line (MU-SENSING §3.7). It is never a scalar standing for a
  multivector.

## 2. What it replaces, and what it refuses

- **The throat sphere's inversion `R ↦ H²/R` is not this law.** Moving a
  reach by it squares `H`, which law 11 refuses, and netstratum's own
  docs/78 forbids it.
- **A crossing that states another motion** is reconstructed under its
  own stated law, or refused `NotLawful`, naming the breach (track 24 B3).
  It is never admitted on a declared law's name alone.

## 3. The form

| Part | Held as | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Reach `R`, throat `H` | Exact rationals (ℚ), each the gauge of one reach line. A reach is **signed**: past `2H` the passage is negative, which is how a kernel reads a body that has passed the centre | A float; a squared norm; a clamped value or a magnitude (the involution returns `R` only if the sign survives) |
| The passage | `2H − R`, exact | `H²/R`; any product of two reaches |
| A share `δ` | Exact rational in `[0, 1]`; passage `1 − δ` | A float share |
| The reverse | The same law (involution) | A second, inverse law |

## 4. Refusals

| Refusal | When |
|---|---|
| `NotLawful` (existing, track 24 B3) | A crossing's stated motion is not the linear passage it claims. The breach is named: the claimed reach and the reconstructed `2H − R`. |
| `NotExact` (LAW-FIELD v1) | A reach or throat not held exactly |

## 5. Joint-read fixtures (to be agreed)

Each fixture is an input and its exact decoded output; every kernel must
reach the same objects.

1. `H = 3/2`, `R = 1/2`: the passage gives `5/2`.
2. The same `H`, `R = 5/2`: the passage gives `1/2` (the involution).
3. `R = H = 7/3`: the passage gives `7/3` (the fixed point).
4. A share `δ = 1/5`: the passage gives `4/5`.
5. A claim stating `H²/R` for `H = 2`, `R = 1` (that is, `4`) where the
   law is the passage (`3`): refused `NotLawful`, naming `4` against
   `3`.
6. `H = 1`, `R = 3`: the passage gives `−1`. The reverse, `R = −1`, gives
   `3`. The sign is held and never clamped (amended with netstratum,
   2026-11-04).

Status of the fixtures: netstratum agrees all six (`flows::through`
reaches them; witnessed on netstratum PR #40). Xylarium's reading is
owed.
