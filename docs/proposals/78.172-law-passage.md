## 1. The law

- **The state.** A body's state is an even multivector `q`.
- **The plane's direction.** Plane `k`'s direction is the degree-1
  element
  ```
  v_k = H_k·e_Zk + R_k·e_Qk,     e_Zk² = +1,   e_Qk² = −1
  ```
  The metric is fixed per plane. Which of its two generators is `Z` is a
  labelling the state carries, never a second metric.
- **One crossing.** One crossing in plane `k` is the **one-sided
  product**
  ```
  q  ↦  v_k·q
  ```
  never the sandwich `v_k·q·ṽ_k`. It takes even degrees to odd ones: the
  two sheets of the crossing are the state's two parities, read from the
  state, never a flag.
- **The horizon.** Where `v_k` is null (`H_k = ±R_k`), `v_k·v_k·q = 0`: a
  state carried through cannot be carried back the same way, and the
  crossing is one-way.
- **Order.** Passages in different planes anticommute,
  `v_k·v_l = −v_l·v_k`: their order is held in the state's sign.
- **First degree, exact.** The product is exact over ℚ. Nothing in it
  squares a reach (law 11), and no reach moves: `H_k` and `R_k` stand in
  `v_k·q` as the coefficients they are.

**Not yet law** (no fixture until derived): the size of a double pass
off the horizon, `v_k·v_k = H_k² − R_k²`, which still squares; and the
rule for ties of two planes at their horizons in one bell (netstratum
docs/117 §9.5).

## 2. What it replaces, and what it refuses

- **`R ↦ 2H − R` (version 1) is struck.** A reach moved on one line is a
  scalar standing for the state.
- **The throat sphere's inversion `R ↦ H²/R` is not this law.** Moving a
  reach by it squares `H`, which law 11 refuses, and netstratum's own
  docs/78 forbids it.
- **The sandwich `v_k·q·ṽ_k` is not this law.** It keeps the state's
  parity and cannot exchange the hold with a reach (netstratum docs/114
  §9.4).
- **A crossing that states another motion** is reconstructed under its
  own stated law, or refused `NotLawful`, naming the breach (track 24 B3).
  It is never admitted on a declared law's name alone.

## 5. Joint-read fixtures

Each fixture is an input and its exact output in the algebra; every
kernel must reach the same objects. `Z_k` and `Q_k` name plane `k`'s two
generators by role, `e_Zk² = +1`, `e_Qk² = −1`; a product of generators
is written by juxtaposition (`e_Z1Q2 = e_Z1·e_Q2`).

1. **One-way at the horizon.** `v = e_Z1 + e_Q1` (null) gives `v·v·q = 0`,
   for the `q` of fixture 2.
2. **Even to odd.** `v = e_Z1 + 2·e_Q1` and `q = 3 + 2·e_Z2Z3 + e_Z1Q2`:
   `v·q` has terms on degrees 1 and 3 only.
3. **Two boosts sharing an axis leave a turning.**
   `[e_Z1Q1, e_Z1Q2] = −2·e_Q1Q2`, with the commutator unhalved,
   `AB − BA`.
4. **A null pair.** `(e_Z1Z2 + e_Q1Z2)² = 0`.

Mycelium reaches the four fixtures in the `forms` crate, as claims on the
passage itself: `forms::passage::pass(v, q)`, the one-sided product over
the exact algebra with its signature read from data (`algebra::Clifford`,
the crate below both `forms` and the library), in
`forms/src/tests/crossing.rs`. The fixtures' generators are named there
by role as the fixtures write them, each plane's `Z` pole positive and
its `Q` pole negative. The law's claims beside the fixtures (the horizon
one-way, two planes anticommuting) run over every frame plane the
algebra holds, no plane count written. `pass` refuses, by name, a `v`
that is not degree 1 (`NotDirection`), a `q` that is not even
(`NotEven`), and two values whose signatures disagree (`Mismatched`).
