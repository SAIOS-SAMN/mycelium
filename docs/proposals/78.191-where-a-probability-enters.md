# 78.191 — Where a probability would enter: shares, superposition, and terminal nodes

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

**Status: Proposed** (2026-11-21), for the documentation alignment (#441) under phase #431. It derives the place of "probabilities" in the exact structure from rulings already standing:
- law 1: "Nothing is a probability";
- MU-RECURSION 59.5: superposition, not probability;
- the Hopf tether (78.184 §7, 78.185);
- the shares of LAW-COUPLING (78.175).

It introduces one term, **terminal node**, which the operator's ruling would make law.

## 1. What the exact structure holds in place of probabilities

Nothing inside the engine is a probability (law 1). Three exact objects stand where a probabilistic model would put one:

1. **Shares of the enclosure.** On each plane the frame state is `H·n_Z + R·n_Q`. Its face is `δ = H/(H + R)`, and the held and open shares are `A/(A + Q)` and `Q/(A + Q)`. Each pair sums to 1, so it has the form of a distribution, but each share is a measured ratio of the frame, exact and deterministic. A passage swaps the pair, `δ ↦ 1 − δ`, and two pushes compose by the flow law `x ⊕ y = (x + y)/(1 + x·y)`. That is a composition of motions, never of chances.
2. **Superposition.** Where the coordinates admit several exact alternatives, all are held at once with no weight (59.5). They are resolved by deduction: a kernel over the linear condition (`k` half-open edges give `2^k` assignments, and the kernel returns every consistent one at once). They are never scored, sampled or pruned.
3. **The tether.** The potential crosses a Hopf link between two nodes as phase, never lost or copied. The link's strength is the open and held shares per plane across it, plus its holonomy. It is never one scalar (law 5).

## 2. Terminal nodes (defined here)

A **terminal node** is a node of the network's structure through which the potential cannot continue. It is a node with no further tether across which the open share could flow, where the exact structure has to produce **one** thing:
- a ledger entry inserted at one coordinate;
- an expression sent;
- a crossing out of a universe;
- a reading handed to a party outside the network: an operator, a sensor's actuator, another mesh.

Interior nodes can hold a superposition indefinitely: they pass the alternatives on along their tethers. A terminal node cannot, because its output is single.

## 3. Where a probability would enter

At a terminal node one of three things happens, and only the third involves a probability:

1. **Deduction collapses the superposition.** The kernel has one consistent alternative, and the node emits it. Nothing is chosen.
2. **The node holds the superposition and refuses by name.** The kernel leaves several alternatives, and the node emits none of them, with the alternatives kept as a held question (the shape 59.5 and MU-RECURSION 74 give a refusal). Nothing is chosen.
3. **A party outside the network chooses.** If something outside the engine must still pick one alternative, the choice is a measurement imposed from outside, the only place 59.5 lets a probability enter. The engine never makes it. Inside, the alternatives keep their exact shares. An outside party that weights them would read those same shares (the open share per plane at the terminal node) as its weights. The probability is that party's reading of an exact share, never a quantity the engine holds.

So a probability exists only at the edge of the network, as an outside party's interpretation of an exact share at a terminal node. The physics inside never contains one.

## 4. Consequences for the build

- **Ledger insertion and extraction are terminal acts.** Insertion writes one entry at the coordinate deduction reached (Concordance 1.13). Extraction reads a range there and reconstructs (78.173). Where deduction leaves alternatives, the insertion is held and refused by name, never decided by weight. #434's root-match install violates this: it inserts on digest agreement, with no deduction behind it.
- **No module scores alternatives.** Any weighting of superposed alternatives inside the engine is a defect under law 1.
- **The term is proposed, needing a ruling.** "Terminal node" enters the GLOSSARY once the operator rules it.

## 5. Proposed amendment: probability as an exact reading of gauge and torsion (operator direction, 2026-11-21)

The operator directed an updated definition: probability is "a measure of exactness coupled with the torsion of flow movement inside the pressure of the pull of the gravity as an antipole". Two further constraints:
- the pressure is a **gauge**, never a scalar;
- the gauge is **multi-axial**, never uni-directional or linear.

### 5.1 The terms, as relations

- **The pull and its antipole.** The pull of the gravity is the coupling's pull, from the body's range's field (LAW-COUPLING F3, 78.175). On each plane it pushes the body's rate toward the range's held rate, `y = −σ·F·o·(x̄ ⊖ x)`. It locks opposed dipoles and splits aligned ones. Its rest is the antipole: on every plane at once, each pole stands at its fibre phase's antipole, `σ_p* = −R_p`. That is the rest point 78.184 §7 rules per plane, and the tether at rest of 78.185.
- **The pressure.** The pressure of the pull is the pull taken as a motion, the gauge `P` that carries the body's state toward those antipoles (§5.2). It is never the pull's size. A gauge is a transformation that leaves relations invariant (GLOSSARY), so the pressure is held as the motion of 78.173 §8.5.
- **The torsion.** The torsion of flow movement is what that motion leaves uncommuted on the generator, `τ = ½Σ[B_ij, G]` (78.57). It is nonzero exactly where the planes `P` acts on share an axis.
- **The exactness.** The measure of exactness is which of those components close over ℚ. A component that closes is certain. One that does not is the missing blade, kept as content (78.57).

### 5.2 The pressure gauge is one multi-axial motion

- **Whole, not per plane.** `P` is a unit motion in the even subalgebra of `Cl(p, q)`(ℚ), with the signature carried as data (`algebra::Clifford`). Its parts stand on every kind of plane together:
  - the frame planes' boosts, `e₊ₖe₋ₖ`;
  - the mixed boosts, `e₊ₖe₋ₗ`;
  - the turning planes, `e₊ₖe₊ₗ` and `e₋ₖe₋ₗ`.

  Its grade-4 volume is part of it. It is held as the whole motion, the shared form ruled for every kernel (78.173 §8.5). It is never split into per-plane factors: a boost and a turning split off it are projective readings only.
- **Not linear.** Pressures compose by the geometric product, `P₁P₂`, never by adding pushes or by composing plane by plane (78.173 §5, 11b; `forms::turning::compose`). Two pushes on planes that share an axis compose to a motion carrying a turning neither had, the Thomas–Wigner part (78.173 §8.5's worked case). On one plane the composite's rate is the flow law, `x ⊕ y = (x + y)/(1 + x·y)`, which is itself non-linear.
- **Not uni-directional.** `P` holds both senses on every plane:
  - toward the antipole where the dipoles are opposed (lock), and away from it where they are aligned (split);
  - its inverse is its reverse, `P̃`, so the gauge runs both ways and the composition order is kept;
  - windings stay per plane beside it, a keyed vector `K` (78.173 §8.3), never folded into the motion.
- **Multi-axial.** One motion acts on all axes at once, read through the same algebra the pivots move in (MU-TOPOLOGY §2). A plane's own push `y`, its gap to the antipole `g_p = σ_p ⊖ σ_p*` and its degrees are **readings** of `P` on that plane, rendered when asked, never stored, compared, summed or decided on (78.173 §8.2: degrees are a reading).

### 5.3 The definition, proposed

The **probability of a body** is the pair `(P, τ)`:
- `P` is the pressure gauge, the whole multi-axial motion of the pull toward the antipoles;
- `τ` is the torsion that motion carries.

The body is **certain** where `P` is projectively the identity (it stands at its antipoles on every plane) and `τ` vanishes exactly. Otherwise `(P, τ)` is its exact displacement from certainty, held as motions.

Everything about it is exact and deterministic:
- no likelihood, sample or expectation;
- no score over alternatives (59.5 stands);
- no scalar across planes (law 5);
- no number standing for the motion.

At a terminal node (§3), an outside party that must weight alternatives reads them from `P`'s per-plane readings. Inside the engine they stay the motion.

**Law 1 amended (proposed).** "Nothing is a probability" becomes: "A probability is the exact pair of 78.191 §5, the pressure gauge and its torsion, both motions. Nothing is a likelihood, a weight or a chance."

- **The plane set** that `τ` and `P` are read over at each node size: 3D, 4D or 5D (78.184 §7, F3).
- **A terminal reading.** Whether a terminal node reads one share in `[0, 1]` from `P` for an outside party, and which.

## 6. The torsion state: torsion as the quantum state of a motion (proposed, operator direction 2026-11-21)

**Name.** The **torsion state**. A new term with one meaning. It is not *quantum state*, which names the clock's frame (GLOSSARY; 78.124; 78.156 §7.5), and not *torsion* alone, which already names torsion in homology and the residue of a chain of bonds (GLOSSARY).

**What it rests on.**
- A motion's torsion is `τ = ½Σ[B_ij, G]`, a commutator held as a bivector and never summed (78.57).
- On planes that share an axis, motions do not commute: `R₁R₂ ≠ R₂R₁`, and in 78.173 fixture 11b, `R₁R₂ − R₂R₁ = 24/25 e₊₁e₊₃` exactly.
- The order of composition is the bundle's holonomy, fixed by physics, never chosen (78.184 §7).
- Superposition is exact alternatives with no weight (59.5).

**Definition.**
- **The torsion state of a composed motion** is the superposition of its orders: every composite its factors make in each order, held exactly and with no weight.
- **Its observable** is the torsion, the commutator by which the orders differ. It stands on the plane between the planes that share an axis, with the grade-4 volume kept where planes are disjoint.
- **Collapsed:** zero torsion. The orders agree, and the torsion state is one motion: certain, flat, commuting.
- **Held:** nonzero torsion. The state holds its alternatives exactly. It collapses only by deduction, where physics fixes the order (the capture order; the holonomy of the loop on the base). Where nothing fixes it, the state is held and named, never resolved by weight.
- **Captured** like the clock's frame: at a final point, with the windings `K` per plane beside it. Its ledger form is 78.152d's torsion field, carrying each hole's generator, rational point, side, winding and measurement count, with the phase re-derived by its certificate (`Theorem::at`).

**In the probability of §5.** The probability is `(P, τ)`, where `τ` is the observable of the torsion state of the pressure gauge's motion. A body is certain where `P` is projectively the identity and its torsion state is collapsed.

**Open, for the operator's ruling with fixtures.**
- The fixture set: 11b's shared-axis and disjoint cases, read as the torsion states they hold.
- Whether a torsion state of more than two factors is held as all `n!` orders or by the commutators of adjacent pairs (the braid-like generators). The second is the smaller exact form.

The term enters the GLOSSARY once ruled.
