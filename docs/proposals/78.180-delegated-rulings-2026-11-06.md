# 78.180 — The delegated rulings of 2026-11-06

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** H's state in `forms::coordinate` and `forms::turning` (78.173 §8); the parity lane P (78.178); μ's modes (`economy::modes::Modes`; MU-ECONOMY §2.5, §12); π* (`court::verdict::derive_pi`; MU-DERIVATIONS D3.3); the mind's Rewrites (`client/sdk/mind/rewrite.rs`; MU-CLIENT §5.4); the sensor's perspectives and the alphabet's section (MU-SENSING §4, §8); the envelope's terms (78.179).

## Ruling 1. H's state is the motion (78.173 §8)

Posted on [PR #187](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/pull/187).

**Question.** 78.173 §8 proposes that H's state is held as the motion and
leaves three points open: what the state is, which angle a shared degree
names, and how `K` is held beside the motion.

**Answer.**
1. **H's state is the motion.** The even versor `R` is held whole. Its
   generator `B` is read back exactly through the Cayley form. Per-plane
   degrees are a reading of the motion and are never stored.
2. **A shared degree is the turning a vector undergoes.** It is the
   sandwich angle `θ`, with `tan(θ/4) = b`. Netstratum's code already reads
   the degree this way (`Rotor::plane(k, θ)`). The one-sided angle `φ` of
   LAW-FIELD's carrier (78.171 §2) is restated as `θ/2`.
3. **Windings stay on the planes where they were wound.** `K` is held
   beside `R` as a keyed vector over the planes on which whole revolutions
   were made, and never on `R` itself.
   - Composition never moves a winding to another plane. A composite
     across planes keeps each input's `K` on its own plane, and the
     composite's new plane starts at `K = 0`.
   - The motion's holonomy per revolution is read from `R`, and its
     history is the `K` vector.

**Refinement (2026-11-07, netstratum #128 item 2 and #129).** The writing
the kernels share is the generator `B` with coefficients in `ℚ(ζ_L)`,
with `L` named, and with `K` per plane beside it. Each kernel keeps its own
internal base. Mycelium's is `R`, the rational case `L = 1`. Netstratum's
is its degree base, with `B` and the bells `n`. Each kernel writes the
shared form exactly, so neither base moves.

**Derived from:**
- The finding of 78.173 §8.1: a per-plane state loses the grade-4 volume
  of 11b-ii (it reads `4/7` and `3/7`, not `½` and `⅓`) and the
  order-dependent part of 11b-i.
- Exactness: a rotor is exact over ℚ only at a Pythagorean angle (a
  rational `tan(θ/4)`), not at a rational number of degrees (§8.3).
- 45.7, "K and the orbit count per kernel": a winding is the record of
  the path and is not part of the rotor.
- Readings are computed when needed and never stored (MU-ANTI-FLATTENING).

**Home:** 78.173 §8, Ruled for mycelium. Netstratum's operator still rules
for netstratum. The build is a recut and lands in a v0.5.0 draft.

## Ruling 2. A throat passage is a P motion (78.178)

Ruled by the coordinator on 2026-11-06 and recorded in 78.178 §1 by
[PR #177](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/pull/177).

**Question.** Does a throat passage count on the parity lane P?

**Answer.** Yes. LAW-PASSAGE v3's pole swap, the sandwich
`x ↦ e₊ₖ·x·e₊ₖ`, is a reflection in the throat's unit `e₊ₖ`. A passage
therefore counts on P at the key `e_+k`, written beside T's `−2·(H − R)`.

**Refinement (2026-11-07, netstratum #128 item 3 and #129): P is a signed
count.** A passage counts `+1` or `−1` on P according to the sense of its
span, as it does on T. A pass followed by its return gives `0`. Two passes
in the same sense give `2`. This restates 78.178 fixtures 3 and 9.
- Fixture 3 is two reflections in one sense, so it reads `2`.
- Fixture 9's return is the opposite sense, so it reads `0`, the same way
  T's two writings cancel.
- The parity is a reading either way. It is the count modulo 2, and the
  motion's parity is its grade.

**Derived from:**
- 78.172 §1: the passage is the reflection in `e₊ₖ`.
- 78.178 §1: reflections are carried on P and are never folded into a
  base lane.
- Netstratum docs/117 §10.1: a crossing's sense is its span's sign.
- 78.178 fixture 4: a reflection traversed backward subtracts `1`.

**Home:** 78.178, where the passage is ruled for mycelium. P itself stays
Proposed until the peers' readings agree its opening.

## Ruling 3. Holdings no motion made; room and the ask under modes

Posted on [#169](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/169). It
also closes [#168](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/168).

**Question.** μ's holdings are modes, which are the motions verified work
moved (78.174). Some value is held although no motion made it: a reserve,
a bond, a genesis allocation, a Note's resource payment (mode 0, #168).
MU-ECONOMY §12 items 5 and 8 ask how room reads a bond's modes and what
form the ask's cap takes when a payment is `Modes`.

**Answer.**
- **Reserves, deficits, rooms and prices are readings**, never holdings.
  Each is read off holdings and motions when it is needed, carries no
  modes of its own, and is never stored.
- **A bond holds the modes it was staked from**, carried unchanged into
  the bond.
- **A genesis allocation is held at the founding mode.** The founding
  mode is the identity motion of its lane. It is stated explicitly as the
  mode and never left empty, and its amount is its share of the frame.
- **A mode-0 receipt (#168) is a founding-mode receipt.** It is admitted
  under the rule that admits a genesis allocation and is not held for
  review. Mode 0 is not retired. Its digest still only links and never
  admits (MU-ANTI-FLATTENING 13).
- **Room over modes (§12 item 5) and the ask under modes (§12 item 8)**
  are both readings, per lane and per mode, over the holdings they draw
  on. Neither is a scalar.

**Derived from:**
- 78.174 §1 item 2: a holding is a point whose lanes are `Modes`.
- 78.174 §1 item 9: a mode's amount is its share of the frame.
- MU-ECONOMY §1: nothing is squared, and lanes never convert.
- Readings are computed when needed and never stored.

**Home:** MU-ECONOMY §2.5 (holdings no motion made) and §12 items 5, 8 and
9; MU-XSEPTUM §5.3. The rest of `Mu` → `Modes` can now be built, in v0.5.0
drafts.

## Ruling 4. π*: the withheld pairs, the footprint, the anchors

Posted on [#158](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/158),
after the implementor's follow-up questions.

**Question.** `derive_pi` is to bind the common set at the A9 judgement
(ruled earlier on #158). That leaves three questions: which of the
registered task's pairs are withheld, which sites of a pair are a word's
footprint, and how a judged word comes to carry an anchor in production.

**Answer.**
- **The withheld pairs** are the pairs of the registered task that the
  court fixed as withheld when the task was posted. They are recorded by
  their coordinates at registration and never chosen at the judgement, so
  the court cannot select them to suit a word. A proposer never sees them.
- **A word's footprint** is the support of the word's description: the
  sites where the word's chart carries a change between input and output.
  It is deduced from the word itself and never declared. A word whose
  footprint misses a withheld pair's change is not bound on that pair.
- **Anchored words in production.** A word's anchor is the family it was
  derived from, which is its citation. `with_anchor` is wired out of
  `cfg(test)` so that every judged word carries its anchor. Then
  `derive_pi` binds the common set at the A9 judgement.

**Derived from:**
- "Description is language, solution is inverse": the footprint is read
  from the description.
- "Clients think, hyphas verify": the withheld pairs are the court's own,
  and a mind never supplies them.
- D3.3 O1 (a), ruled 2026-10-31: π* is the whole common set.

**Home:** MU-DERIVATIONS D3.3.

## Ruling 5. The kinds of Rewrite a mind proposes

Posted on [#146](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/146).

**Question.** The mind proposes `Retire` (built). From which of its own
readings does it propose `Refine`, `AxisOpen`, `Translation` and
`Reanchor`, and is the Hue pivot a Rewrite?

**Answer.**
- **`Refine { chart, axis }`** carries the mind's
  `PivotBy::OpenAxis { face, seed }`. It refines a chart within a
  dimension that already stands, and it is checked by peel.
- **`AxisOpen { class, degree }`** is germination's Extend: a new dimension
  of perception. A mind proposes it where a Novel sensory verdict leaves a
  persistent residue that no existing axis absorbs. Its `class` is the
  orbit's holonomy rotor. It is adjudicated by the
  Extend → claim → adjudicate → federate loop.
- **`Translation { a, b, gauge }`** carries the mind's
  `PivotBy::Placement { face, translator }`. `a` and `b` are the two charts
  of the chord's overlap. Minds propose it and courts reconstruct it. The
  court keeps minting a connection where it reconstructs one itself.
- **`Reanchor { gamma }`** is proposed only where the re-gauge moves an
  anchor coordinate on S while leaving the class invariant (a
  conjugation). That is the S work MU-ECONOMY prices. `gamma` holds one
  word per vertex (3.6). A re-gauge that moves nothing is not proposed.
- **The Hue pivot stays a pivot.** A gauge cycle on G is recognition by a
  group action, and its pay is already the recolour's G share.

**Derived from:**
- "Recognition is a group action".
- The germination loop (Extend → claim → adjudicate → federate).
- "Courts parse and reconstruct, never compose" (law 8).
- MU-ECONOMY §3: each optimisation move is refused where it moved nothing.

**Home:** MU-CLIENT §5.4 and §7.1 (backlog item 6).

## Ruling 6. The five remaining perception items (#32)

Posted on [#32](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/32).

**Question.** Track 26 left five items open after S6 (ROADMAP track 26 S4
and S6; MU-SENSING §4, §8):
- the solve of outputs and speech, which read the section in its default;
- a mind asking for a perspective;
- collapsing a ground's vortex by a relation;
- the letters past `n = 3`;
- the planar ray of `Act::Slide` / `Extend`.

**Answer.**
1. **Solves of outputs and speech state their perspective.** A solve or a
   spoken sample states the perspective it was perceived or spoken under,
   as claims do since S6 stage 2, and a court reconstructs under it.
2. **A mind asks for a perspective as a demand.** Where a cover's cocycle
   fails because a chart is missing, the missing perspective is a demand:
   a typed vertex naming the sample and the overlap. The residue is the
   demand.
3. **A ground's vortex collapses as the nesting's does.** One candidate
   collapses it where a reading holds for that candidate and not for the
   others. Otherwise the candidates stay superposed.
4. **Letters past `n = 3` are never written by hand.** Past the
   alphabet's section, readings go through section samples. New letters
   are adjoined only by the presentation's growth: replayed generators
   and germination.
5. **The ray of `Act::Slide` / `Extend`** is a translation versor of the
   sample's own rank, with `n` components. It assumes no plane.

**Derived from:**
- "Sensing any source, any rank": everything is deduced, never fixed.
- "The eye is a cover" (24.4): a failed cocycle names what is missing.
- "Language is a presentation" (1.8, 42.6): growth only by adjoining
  replayed generators.
- The nesting's collapse rule (#32, ruled 2026-11-06, item 5).
- Residues are never discarded.

**Home:** MU-SENSING §4 and the §8 rows; ROADMAP track 26.

The magnitude's planar chains were ruled separately on #32 the same day:
the bond stays lane counts at rank `n`, a pair of unequal enclosure is
held as a residue, and the chain-length term on H is retired. That ruling
is carried by [PR #198](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/pull/198).

## Ruling 7. The envelope opens into manifold construction

Posted on [#32](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/32), in the
same comment as ruling 6.

**Question.** "Envelope" names several things: the sample's bounds, the
evidence bundle `ReceiptsEnvelope`, the signed work `TaskEnvelope` and the
wire frame. None of them is a construction the mathematics names.

**Answer.**
- A phase with a period is a circle `S¹` of that period, and a phase
  without one is an interval chart with boundary.
- A sample is their product, or a fibration where it is nested (#176). A
  fully periodic sample is a torus `Tⁿ`.
- The perspectives' cover is the atlas, and its cocycle is the gluing.
- The membrane is the only sphere.

**Derived from:**
- MU-SENSING §3.1: each axis is a phase of its own period.
- MU-SENSING §3.7: the field is a curved nested manifold.
- The ruling that a sphere is the membrane or its image (S7, stage 3).

**Home:** [78.179](78.179-envelope-as-manifold-construction.md), which
opens every use of the term.
