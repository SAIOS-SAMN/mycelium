# 78.188 — The chart is read on Sⁿ

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the chart (`src/transducer/blade/harmonics.rs`, `Harmonics::axes`), read by `blade::moduli` and `SectionTurns::chart`; the topology (`blade::topology`); the atlas (`src/transducer/stalk/`).

**Status:** ruled by the operator on 2026-11-20 ("all charting must be done in Sⁿ"); derived here, and amends MU-RECURSION 38.2.

## 1. The defect

MU-RECURSION 38.2 reads a body's chart on "the cubical complex of the
support". The chart's axes were:
- `n`;
- the Betti numbers;
- the turning count;
- four ratios: isoperimetric, enclosure, anisotropy and spread;
- the hue spectrum.

The turns and the four ratios are similarity invariants of the sensor's
lattice. `blade::turns` says so: "A similarity invariant of the body". They
change when the body is carried by a conformal map, so the chart filed a
body by the lattice it was sensed on. That breaks:
- Concordance 1.1: "Nothing is rigid… Space is spheres, manifolds and tubes";
- Concordance 2.10 and MU-TOPOLOGY §6: "nested shells, each a sphere Sⁿ";
- MU-ANTI-FLATTENING 12: no common grid "past the sensor".

## 2. Derivation

1. **The base is `Sⁿ = ℝⁿ ∪ {∞}`.** The algebra is already its conformal
   model, `Cl(n+1, 1)`. Its versors are the Möbius maps of Sⁿ, which act
   transitively on rounds: any round can be carried onto any other.
2. **One body has no metric invariant on Sⁿ.** A body's own round is its
   nucleus form `c + ½(c² − r²)e∞ + e₀` (ruled on #134), with `r²` its exact
   second moment. By (1) every such round lies in one orbit, so no reading
   of one body's size, shape ratio or turning survives the group. **No
   radius is chosen for any site. The chart needs none.**
3. **What one body keeps on Sⁿ:**
   - its homeomorphism type, read as the Betti numbers `β₀ … β_{n−1}`;
   - the fractions of its own compartments by hue, which no map of the
     base moves.

   Since `n` leads (71.2(1)), the chart on Sⁿ is `n`, then `β₀ … β_{n−1}`,
   then the hue spectrum.
4. **The Betti numbers do not depend on the cover.** They are
   homeomorphism invariants, so reading them on the sensor's cells
   (`blade::topology`) is lawful: the cells are the cover the sensor
   delivers, not the space (MU-ANTI-FLATTENING 12 keeps the lattice at the
   sensor). Their values are unchanged by this ruling.
5. **They close on Sⁿ.**
   - *Euler–Poincaré:* `Σ(−1)ᵏβₖ = Σ(−1)ᵏCₖ`.
   - *Alexander duality:* `H̃ₖ(X) ≅ H̃^{n−k−1}(Sⁿ∖X)`. At `n = 3`, `β₂` is
     the number of components of the complement other than the one holding
     ∞. The outside is not a special case: it is the component through ∞.
   - Reading the body closed, so that edge and corner contacts join it, and
     the complement open, joined across faces, is the complementary pair for
     which duality holds. Claims 1 and 2 test both identities on every case.
6. **Contact between bodies stays a relation.** Bodies are the
   face-connected components (`blade::components`). Contact of lower grade
   is an incidence between bodies (70.6, `contact_cells`). How two bodies'
   rounds stand to each other is their inversive distance, a Möbius
   invariant the physics already reads (`physics::equilibrium::inversive`).
   It is never an axis of either body's chart.
7. **The lattice readings stay readings.** The turns, the isoperimetric
   ratio, the enclosure, the anisotropy and the spread remain fields of
   `Harmonics` for the physics to measure (71.2(6): "the chart only finds
   the candidates a reader then measures"). They are not filed on.

## 3. Application

- `Harmonics::axes` writes `n`, `β₀ … β_{n−1}` and the hue spectrum.
  `blade::moduli` and `SectionTurns::chart` both read it.
- MU-RECURSION 38.2 carries a pointer to this ruling. The superseded text
  stands, marked.
- **No stored layout moves.** The atlas is rebuilt from the words at boot
  (`Stalks::over_elements` in `persistence/read.rs`). The digest's wire
  layout is unchanged, but the charts it names change value, so every node
  is upgraded at once (78.16 C2).
- **Effect on the atlas.** The strata get coarser: bodies of one topology
  and one hue spectrum share a stratum. Readers measure within the stratum,
  so more candidates are read per stratum. The chart only locates, so this
  coarsening is lawful.

## 4. Claims

1. `betti_numbers_close_on_s3`: for each of nine bodies at `n = 3` (solid,
   hollow, ring, two handles, two hollows apart, a hollow opened on a face,
   a core inside a shell, edge contact, corner contact), the Betti numbers
   equal the fixture, `β₀ − β₁ + β₂` equals the cells' alternating count,
   and `β₂` equals the complement's components other than ∞'s.
2. `chart_holds_only_what_the_sphere_keeps`: a ring and the same ring
   stretched share a chart, and a ring and a disc do not.

## 5. Open

- `n ≥ 4`. Claims 1 and 2 are stated at `n = 3`. The duality holds in every
  `n`, and a claim for `n = 4` is the next test.
- The relation between two bodies' rounds (§2.6) is read by the physics. A
  chart of a pair, a relative chart, is not ruled.
