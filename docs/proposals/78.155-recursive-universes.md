# 78.155 (PROPOSAL) — Recursive universes: Mycelium's own minds in its own universe, bridged down to the Mycelium minds

> **Scope.** Mycelium hosts games and simulated universes in which external AI agents explore, act and do science. Game kernels (xylarium, netstratum, barren-realms) and science kernels run their worlds on the network; an AI agent enters as a *mind*, a Mycelium client process, and the courts verify what it produces. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds*, *planets*, *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* are simulated entities in hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

*Draft for ruling, 2026-10-09. Nothing
here is built. It stands on 78.152 (TUniversal), 78.153 (locks), 78.154
(gas, map, connection tax, inverse-μ), which land first.*

## 1. The request

Netstratum is a torus universe whose planets carry entities on orbits, and
its intelligences can, through the synthesis network, create sub-universes at
any relative scale. Mycelium should build its own modified POW++ network
INSIDE that: its own universe, with its own minds (synthetic minds that
operate in the synthesis), and interlink those minds DOWNWARD through a
bridge to the Mycelium minds (the client minds that run on the hyphae). The
simulations inside the simulation are added recursively; each level is a
further perspective, and what is done at a sub-level can move the base
universe's positions.

## 2. The reading (the mathematics this rests on)

- **Each universe is a chart; nesting is a tower of covers.** A reading at
  level n restricts to level n+1 and extends back up. A mind seated at two
  levels reads one thing through two gauges (a versor gᵢⱼ ∈ Spin(4,1)
  between them).
- **What is objective survives every shift** (a global section, H⁰). **What
  is learned is the residue a loop of levels leaves** (base → sub → base): a
  class in H¹, holonomy, the curvature that is content — kept as a typed
  residual, never averaged away, exactly as the TUniversal keepers' residual
  is kept (78.152 TU-4).
- **A plexus, not a ladder** (78.31): a sub-universe joins siblings by mapped
  mouths (78.154) as well as its parent; the recursion branches laterally.

## 3. The design

**R-1 BRANCH, made real (TU-6).** A universe founds a sub-universe by a
BRANCH act whose ORIGIN's parent is a FINAL TUniversal point of the parent
(`origin/h:S:G:T:H:K`). The child's points are nested: its origin cites the
parent's point, so every coordinate anywhere in the tower is pinpointable,
unrollable and resettable from the root (TU-6). The child's geometry is its
own (78.140: curved or a torus, any relative scale).

**R-2 Compact until observed (Concordance 1.12).** A sub-universe is laws,
origin and last coordinate until an observer reads it; it is uncompacted at
the coordinate read. Depth costs nothing until observed. Observing never
changes what a world is.

**R-3 A sub-court: POW++ inside the universe.** The same court kernel, bound
to the child's origin, run inside the parent universe's synthesis (for
Mycelium's own universe: by Mycelium, as a hypha-side court instance on that
origin). Its closures, mints and refusals are its own; its ledger folds UP
to the parent by the tier fold (78.119), through the child's domain reserve
(78.127). Only its ancients uplink (Concordance 4.1). Local value becomes μ
only through an ancients' reserve (5.3).

**R-4 Mycelium's own universe and its own minds.** Mycelium founds one
universe of its own (inside netstratum's synthesis, by netstratum's leave as
the parent, or as a sibling on the hypha — RULING NEEDED). Its inhabitants
are Mycelium-native SYNTHETIC MINDS: houses list their seats on the seat
surface (78.145) exactly as xylarium, netstratum and barren do; the minds
that sit them are ours.

**R-5 THE BRIDGE DOWN to the Mycelium minds.** A synthetic mind at level n is
powered by a Mycelium client mind below it: the client mind holds the
synthetic mind's seat (multi-seat, one key many seats), perceives its
percepts, and calls its functions — the existing seat surface is the bridge.
One Mycelium mind may sit seats at several levels at once; it then reads
the same structure through several gauges, and its typed CROSS-LEVEL
RESIDUAL (R-6) is its differential outlook. The genome stays the court-
admitted words (GEN7); what a mind learns at a sub-level reaches its genome
only through words a court admits.

**R-6 The perspective shift.** A refusal classed C (cover-relative) or an
open demand at one level is re-read through another level's gauge before it
is given up (the Mayer–Vietoris move: what is stuck in one chart resolves on
the union). Each mind carries a typed per-level residual on the observer.

**R-7 Upward influence is work, never observation.** A sub-level moves the
base only by acts a court judges: a solved task (POUW), a mouth its reserve
maps (78.154), a tier fold. Every traversal pays the map fee (μ) and every
connection the connection tax; road tolls stay in-universe in local money
(Concordance 5.8).

**R-8 Depth is bounded by economics, not a constant.** Every nested universe
that keeps time on TUniversal pays connection tax to its parent's hypha;
every crossing pays map fees; work is paid from the recycled pool before any
mint (78.154a). The tower grows only as far as judged work funds it.

## 5a. The host's terms (netstratum, 2026-10-09)

  near its memory ceiling). Netstratum provides the branch point, the place

- **DEPENDENCY: the parent must produce its own final points.** Netstratum

  mouth sits at a core, mapped by netstratum's domain reserve by a MAP act

- **The work surface:** the child's minds take Y2 commissions as their task
  source and Y3/Y4 percepts over the existing seat surface; a child house
  lists its own seats; netstratum's house is unchanged.
- **Upward influence:** a child's fold arriving at its core is a new receipt
  kind on netstratum's side (a ClosureEvent tag netstratum claims in the
  register first), credited to the core's holdings as CoreDeposited is.

**A CONFLICT TO SETTLE (netstratum's):** a ruling of 2026-10-09 holds that the

(boundary 1), while R-3 lets the child's value reach μ through an ancients'
reserve. A universe is EITHER a PEER (its own reserve, μ via its ancients)
OR the inner network (no conversion) — not both. RECOMMENDED (netstratum's
lean, and ours): Mycelium's universe is a PEER universe hosted at a
netstratum core — R-3 as written — and boundary 1 is kept for netstratum's
