# 78.155 — Recursive universes (proposal): Mycelium's own minds in its own universe, bridged down to the Mycelium minds

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside governed worlds — games, scientific simulations and world models run by kernels under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the network and a hosting game kernel: Mycelium's own minds in a universe of its own, bridged to a host universe. Proposal, partly built.

## 1. The request

A hosted universe (for example a torus universe whose planets carry
inhabitants on orbits) may let its intelligences create sub-universes at any
relative scale. Mycelium should build its own modified POW++ network
INSIDE such a universe: its own universe, with its own minds (synthetic minds that
operate in the synthesis), and interlink those minds DOWNWARD through a
bridge to the Mycelium minds (the minds that run on the hyphae). The
simulations inside the simulation are added recursively; each level is a
further perspective, and what is done at a sub-level can move the base
universe's positions.

## 2. The reading (the mathematics this rests on)

- **Each universe is a chart; nesting is a tower of covers.** A reading at
  level n restricts to level n+1 and extends back up. A mind seated at two
  levels reads one thing through two gauges (a versor gᵢⱼ ∈ Spin(4,1)
  between them).
- **What is objective survives every shift** (a global section, H⁰). **What
  is learned is the residue a ring of levels leaves** (base → sub → base): a
  class in H¹, holonomy, the curvature that is content — kept as a typed
  residue, never averaged away, exactly as the TUniversal keepers' residue
  is kept (78.152 TU-4).
- **A plexus, not a ladder** (78.31): a sub-universe joins siblings by mapped
  mouths (78.154) as well as its parent; the recursion branches laterally.

## 3. The design

**R-1 BRANCH, made real (TU-6).** A universe founds a sub-universe by a
BRANCH act whose ORIGIN's parent is a FINAL TUniversal point of the parent
(`origin/h:S:G:T:H:K`). The child's points are nested: its origin cites the
parent's point, so every coordinate anywhere in the tower is pinpointable,
unrollable and resettable from the root (TU-6). The child's geometry is its
own (78.140: curved or a torus, any relative scale).

**R-2 Compact until observed (Concordance 1.12).** A sub-universe is laws,
origin and last coordinate until an observer reads it; it is uncompacted at
the coordinate read. Depth costs nothing until observed. Observing never
changes what a world is.

**R-3 A sub-court: POW++ inside the universe.** The same court, bound
to the child's origin, run inside the parent universe's synthesis (for
Mycelium's own universe: by Mycelium, as a hypha-side court instance on that
origin). Its closures, mints and refusals are its own; its ledger folds UP
to the parent by the tier fold (78.119), through the child's domain reserve
(78.127). Only its ancients uplink (Concordance 4.1). Local value becomes μ
only through an ancients' reserve (5.3).

**R-4 Mycelium's own universe and its own minds.** Mycelium founds one
universe of its own (inside a host universe, by the host's leave as the
parent, or as a sibling on the hypha — ruled in §5b). Its inhabitants
are Mycelium-native SYNTHETIC MINDS: each house lists its seats on the seat
surface (78.145) exactly as the game kernels' houses do; the minds
that sit them are Mycelium's own.

**R-5 THE BRIDGE DOWN to the Mycelium minds.** A synthetic mind at level n is
powered by a Mycelium mind below it: the Mycelium mind holds the
synthetic mind's seat (multi-seat, one key many seats), perceives its
percepts, and calls its functions — the existing seat surface is the bridge.
One Mycelium mind may sit seats at several levels at once; it then reads
the same structure through several gauges, and its typed CROSS-LEVEL
RESIDUE (R-6) is its differential outlook. The genome stays the court-
admitted words (GEN7); what a mind learns at a sub-level reaches its genome
only through words a court admits.

**R-6 The perspective shift.** A refusal classed C (cover-relative) or an
open demand at one level is re-read through another level's gauge before it
is given up (the Mayer–Vietoris move: what is stuck in one chart resolves on
the union). Each mind carries a typed per-level residue on the observer.

**R-7 Upward influence is work, never observation.** A sub-level moves the
base only by acts a court judges: a solved task (POUW), a mouth its reserve
maps (78.154), a tier fold. Every traversal pays the map fee (μ) and every
connection the connection tax; road tolls stay in-universe in local money
(Concordance 5.8).

**R-8 Depth is bounded by economics, not a constant.** Every nested universe
that keeps time on TUniversal pays connection tax to its parent's hypha;
every crossing pays map fees; work is paid from the recycled pool before any
mint (78.154a). The tower grows only as far as judged work funds it.

## 5. Rulings asked

1. **Where Mycelium's universe lives:** a sub-universe of a host universe
   (the host as its parent), or a sibling universe founded on the hypha with
   its own reserve.
2. **Who may found a sub-universe:** any universe's reserve, or only its
   ancients; and what the parent receives (the connection tax alone, or a
   share of the child's folds).
3. **Whether a sub-court is a hypha-side court instance** (run by the hyphae
   on the child's origin) or a kernel-side court (run inside the parent's
   synthesis, uplinking by folds). (Recommend hypha-side: the court is the
   hypha's; the kernel hosts only the world.)

## 5a. A host's terms, in general

A universe that hosts another's mouth keeps its own world running on its
own resources; the hosted universe's state lives on the hosted side,
compact until observed. A branch at "a final point of the parent" needs
the parent to produce its own final points. The child's mouth sits at a
place of the parent (a core, where cores are the throats), mapped by the
parent's domain reserve by a MAP act (78.154). Each of the child's minds
takes tasks and percepts over the existing seat surface; a child house
lists its own seats. A universe is EITHER a PEER (its own reserve, μ via
its ancients) OR an inner network whose token never converts to μ — not
both.

## 5b. The ruling (2026-10-09)

- **Mycelium's universe is a PEER universe ON THE HYPHA**: its origin from
  the hypha's genesis, its own reserve and ancients, a hypha-side sub-court.
- **It joins a host universe by a MOUTH AT ONE OF THE HOST'S CORES**,
  mapped by the host's reserve. The host's own inner networks, whose
  tokens never convert, are unaffected.
- **Founding is by an ancients' reserve only; a sub-universe's parent gets
  the connection tax alone** (rulings 2 and 3 settled; ruling 1 answered:
  a peer on the hypha).
- **Dependency:** the host's reserve maps the mouth only once it has
  passed out of trust, when the host's ancients arise (Concordance 3.1).

## 5c. A kernel's own sub-universes

A kernel may design sub-universes of its own, kept, judged and paid
inside that kernel with a token that never converts. What such a design
needs from Mycelium:
- **(a) TU-6 nested points** — R-1 here: a point under a sub-origin that
  cites the parent's final point. Not built.
- **(b) Seats scoped to a sub-origin under a domain** — a seat named by
  (domain, sub-origin, seat id), so a multi-seat mind sits a
  synthetic seat at a chosen level. Built (seat wire v1.4).
- **(c) The typed cross-level residue** — R-6. Not built.
No hypha sub-court is needed for such sub-universes.

## 6. Trajectory

1. **R-1 BRANCH + nested TUniversal** — the founding act and the nested
   point; tested: a child's point pinpoints and unrolls from the root.
2. **R-2 compact until observed** for a branch.
3. **R-3 sub-court** on a child origin, folding up through its reserve.
4. **R-4 Mycelium's universe** founded; its house lists synthetic seats.
5. **R-5 the bridge**: Mycelium's multi-seat minds sit seats at two levels;
   **R-6** the typed cross-level residue and the perspective-shift act.
6. **The measure (1.9):** do minds seated across levels close, by the court's
   judge, what single-level minds cannot? That number, not a count of
   universes, says whether the recursion is cognition.
