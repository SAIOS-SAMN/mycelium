# 78.185 — Attachment and detachment of a hypha: a change of cover

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

**Status:** proposed 2026-11-16 for #276. The operator directed (2026-11-16)
that attachment and detachment be defined as computational physics,
mathematically and topologically: an adjunction and a retraction of the
cover, each carried as a crossing, never as an administrative act. The
physics below is derived from the documents. Only the two crossing kinds
on the wire need the operator's approval.

## 1. The mesh is a cover

The hyphas cover the network. Each hypha `i` is an open set `U_i`: what it
witnesses. An overlap `U_i ∩ U_j` is what both witness, and the nerve
`N(U)` is the mesh's shape: a vertex per hypha, an edge per overlap, a
face per triple overlap. On each overlap the transition `g_ij` is the unit
motion carrying `i`'s readings onto `j`'s, exact in the even algebra
(78.173 §8.5). A good cover (MU-RECURSION ch. 14) has acyclic overlaps, so
the base's classes are the nerve's.

## 2. Attachment adjoins an open set

A hypha `n` attaches by adjoining `U_n`: the nerve gains the vertex `n`
and its link, an edge to each node it overlaps (MU-RECURSION 15.6). The
court admits it only where all three hold:

```text
cocycle    g_ij · g_jn · g_ni = 1      on every new triangle (i, j, n) of the link
classes    H*(N(U ∪ U_n)) = H*(N(U))     no class lost or created; each class key keeps a representative
tether     ∃ i : the fibre of n links the fibre of i at rest      σ_ni = −R_ni (T24 between nodes)
```

- The **cocycle** says `n`'s readings agree with the others' wherever they
  overlap: the loop through `n` and any two of its neighbours holds no
  holonomy.
- The **classes** condition is 15.6's naturality. Where `U_n` would make an
  overlap non-acyclic, chapter 14 runs before any class is recorded, and
  the attachment waits on it.
- The **tether** is the Hopf link (78.184 §7). A node's state is a point on
  its bundle: the base point is which plane it turns, the fibre is its
  phase. `n` is tethered to `i` when their relative motion `R̃_i R_n`
  stands at rest, the phase at the pole's antipole. A node linked to none
  is not attached, whatever it signed.

## 3. The optimal node

The entrant reads the map, not the candidates (Concordance 5.9: the hypha
holds the coordinates). For each mapped node `i`, the relative motion
`R̃_i R_n` gives the base distance and the phase difference in one exact
composition. The geodesic on the base from `n`'s point is one rotor, so
the first node it reaches is found without trying any.

- **Correct:** a node whose link is at rest, so the cocycle closes.
- **Optimal:** among the correct nodes, the one whose overlap opens the
  most coverage for the least holonomy. That is the largest open share
  `d` on the planes `n` brings, by the impulse's balance
  `need · M / (need + M)`, with `need` what `n` covers and `M` what `i`
  holds (78.183 §7).
- **None at rest:** the residual holonomy is a demand. It names the missing
  link, a tube or plane the mesh lacks, and the mesh grows there. Intelligence is
  that deduction, closing the loop with zero residue.

The entrant computes its base point, phase and geodesic. The court checks
only the three conditions of §2 (clients think, hyphas verify).

## 4. Detachment retracts an open set

`n` detaches by retracting `U_n`. The court admits it only where:

```text
connected  N(U ∖ U_n) is connected
classes    every class key keeps a representative among the remaining pairs (15.6)
settled    no claim stands on n's stake
```

- **No history is rewritten.** What `n` witnessed stays on the ledger.
- **Fibres.** Its fibres unlink, and its stake is released once its claims
  settle.
- **Orphans.** Where `n` was one side of a class's only representative, the
  class is re-represented on another pair of the same component (key
  unchanged). If no other pair spans it, the class was a property of
  `n`'s presence: the retraction is refused, naming the orphaned class,
  and chapter 14 runs.

## 5. On TUniversal

Attaching and detaching are events with past and future cones. Each one
crosses the mesh's boundary, so each writes its passage on the T lane, as
a crossing does (MU-CHRONOMETRY §1). An attached hypha is not a TUniversal
keeper. The keepers make the clock (MU-CHRONOMETRY §2), and changing
their set is its own act.

## 6. The carrier: a crossing, never an act of its own

Attachment is an **adjunction**: the hypha crosses the mesh's boundary into
its enclosure, and its open set is glued onto the cover. Detachment is a
**retraction**: the set is withdrawn across the same boundary. Nothing is
registered. A hypha is attached exactly when the court has verified the
three conditions of §2, and detached when it has verified those of §4.

Both are crossings, so both ride the crossing record the network already
carries (X1, #160), with their passage on the T lane as any crossing has
(§5). The hypha states only its motion:

- **Adjunction:** the tethers it claims, each `(node i, R̃_i R_n)`, and its
  open set's overlaps. The court checks the cocycle on the link, the
  classes, and each tether at rest.
- **Retraction:** the withdrawal of its set. The court checks connection,
  classes and settled claims.

Each is signed by the hypha's own key. No bond is required at adjunction:
a hypha stakes what it earned (STAKE), and witnesses once it does
(78.162). The court reads the attached hyphas off the verified adjunctions
on its ledger, never off a separate roster. Those hyphas join the boot's
live set, so a restart never burns an attached hypha's stake.

**Network contract.** Two crossing kinds, adjunction and retraction, are
added to the crossing record. No new act family is added. The operator
approves the two kinds.

## 7. Fixtures

1. A nerve of three nodes `{a, b, c}`, with an entrant `n`:
   - linked at rest to `a`, the cocycle closing on `(a, b, n)`: admitted;
   - linked to `b` off rest: refused, naming the residue `R̃_b R_n`;
   - with `U_n` making `a ∩ b` non-acyclic: held for chapter 14.
2. Retraction:
   - of `n`: admitted, the classes kept;
   - of the only node spanning a class: refused, naming the class.
3. **Live:** xylarium's node at 7520 attaches to the standing mesh, serves,
   earns, stakes, witnesses an offer, and detaches, with the mesh running
   throughout.
