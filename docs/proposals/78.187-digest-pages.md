# 78.187 — The digest reply is paged by chart

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the digest act (`ACT_DIGESTS`, 0x10; `src/anastome/dialogue/cover.rs`); the court's answer (`src/bin/mycelium-server/judge/syllabus.rs`); the askers: the hypha's exchange (`src/bin/mycelium-server/exchange.rs`, `src/bin/mycelium-server/judge/fold.rs`) and the mind's session (`src/client/sdk/session.rs`, `src/client/sdk/mind/learn.rs`).

**Status: Proposed** (2026-11-20), for #349. This changes the wire, which needs the operator's approval (MU-RELEASE §1). It is not a Concordance amendment and not a law version.

## 1. The defect

`judge/syllabus.rs` answers a digest request from any voice but the
court's own with one `StratumDigest` (chart, count, root) for every
stratum the court holds. The reply grows with the book.

On hypha a on 2026-11-19 the reply was 1.3–2.1 MB against `BODY_BUDGET`
(768 KiB). The writer refused it whole every time (#385 names the act in
the refusal). The refusal at the writer is correct (MU-RECURSION 78.22
LD0). Widening the budget is ruled out: "Widening a length prefix treats
the symptom". What is wrong is a reply that writes the whole cover where
one page belongs.

The documents already state the shape of the fix. A transmission digest's
payload is "a root over the whole cover", and "a prefix carrying every
chart a hypha holds grows with the book … and the whole digest is refused
by every peer" (47.1). Large readings are read "through the prefix key, so
… not cut by the reply's body budget" (78.16). `ACT_RECEIPTS` answers "as
many as fit the body budget, from `from` upward" (47.2). The receiving
court already resumes its confirmation past a chart (`confirm_cursor`,
78.153b(4); `Court::confirm_peer_holds_on`).

## 2. Statement

- **The request.** It has two forms, told apart by an explicit sub-kind
  byte, never by length alone:
  - `[ACT_DIGESTS][voice 32]` reads from the first chart. It is the request
    as it stands.
  - `[ACT_DIGESTS][voice 32][DIGESTS_PAST u8][chart]` reads the strata past
    the named chart. The chart is written by its coordinates (A21; LD0:
    "a chart … by its coordinates").
- **The order.** Strata are read in the order of their chart keys. A
  chart's key writes its rank `n` first, so the strata of one `n` follow
  one another and strata of different `n` never interleave (71.2(6)).
- **The reply.** The layout is the same as today:
  1. `[n u32]`, then the strata in key order, as many as fit the body
     budget once the signed cover digest and its receipt section have been
     counted;
  2. the cover digest and receipt section, as today;
  3. a trailing **page end**, the same four readings as D4.2's evidence
     page:
     - `Store`: the cover was read to its last chart;
     - `Full`: the next stratum does not fit;
     - `Unframable { width }`: a stratum that no reply can carry, refused
       by name at the writer (LD0), with the page ending before it;
     - `Spent`: the hold's stroke ran out.
- **The writer's cost.** Each stratum read costs one `spend` on the hold's
  stroke, as `confirm_peer_holds_on` reads (78.153b(4)). The writer reads
  from the named chart forward and never copies the whole cover to answer
  one page (78.153: `strata` copies every member).
- **The asker.**
  - It holds the last chart it read from each hypha as a coordinate,
    volatile, like `confirm_cursor`.
  - Its next ask names that chart. On `Store` it starts again from the
    first chart, so a full round covers the cover once.
  - Asking stays paced as documented: per peer by that peer's Λ, counted in
    the court's own K, or when the court's own roots move, never by a
    timer; a deferral makes the asker wait (MU-ENGINEERING §4; 45.7, 23.8).
  - It fetches the discordant strata of each page as today
    (`sheaf::discordant`). Digests only decide what to fetch (17.7 M7). Gluing
    and admission are unchanged: by reconstruction and 13.9 on the
    sections fetched, never by roots matching.
- **The signed payload is unchanged.** `cover_signed_prefix` signs the
  cover roots, the ruled root and the room, a root over the whole cover
  (47.1). Every page carries that digest.

## 3. What does not change

- The court's own-voice read (`ruled_digests_for`), the fetch act, the
  gluing, and the confirmation of ruled strata (`confirm_peer_holds_on`).
- Specified elsewhere and not built: `StratumDigest.depth_max` (chapter 26)
  and the replication of the presentation rather than the words (78.100
  S5). Each is a separate item.
- Not claimed here: a sync whose cost follows only the divergence. No
  document specifies one. A round's total work grows with the cover. Each
  reply is bounded by the body budget, and each page by the stroke.

## 4. The minds' clock is a separate fault

Every reply carries the hypha's TUniversal point (MU-CLIENT:427–430, 498),
so the refused digest reply does not by itself explain why minds read "no
clock carried". The mind's session does learn the route and the room from
the digest reply (`Session::absorb_digest`, `refresh_covers`). Whether a
refused digest leaves a mind without a route is read live after this
lands. It is not assumed.

## 5. Network contract

- **What changes on the wire:** a new sub-kind of the `ACT_DIGESTS`
  request, and a trailing page end on its reply.
- **Version step.** Under MU-ENGINEERING §4 ("a new act, mode or trailing
  field an older reader ignores steps the minor"), this is a minor step of
  `WIRE_VERSION`. Claim 5 tests that an older reader ignores the trailing
  page end. If it does not, the step is major.
- **Older askers.** A 33-byte asker that does not read the page end gets
  the first page in place of today's refusal. It reads only the strata on
  that page until its readers change (mycelium-client).
- **Activation.** The change takes effect at a TUniversal final point in a
  release (`activates_at`, MU-RELEASE §5). Every node is upgraded at once
  (78.16 C2).
- **Approval.** The operator approves it as a wire change (MU-RELEASE §1).

## 6. Claims (written first, as tests)

Take a cover of five strata, `S1 < … < S5` in key order, and a budget that
holds two:
1. The 33-byte request reads `S1, S2`, `Full`. Past `S2` it reads
   `S3, S4`, `Full`. Past `S4` it reads `S5`, `Store`. The pages together
   hold every stratum once, in key order.
2. A request past `S5` reads no stratum, `Store`.
3. Every reply body is at most `BODY_BUDGET`, cover digest and page end
   included.
4. A stratum wider than any reply reads `Unframable` with its width. The
   page ends before it, and the next request past it reads what follows.
5. The reader as it stands (`take_digests_reply`, then
   `decode_cover_digest`) decodes a paged reply's strata and its cover
   digest.
6. A stroke spent at `S3` reads `S1, S2`, `Spent`, and the next request
   past `S2` reads on from `S3`.
7. Strata of `n = 3` and `n = 4` are read in key order with no
   interleaving, and a page boundary between them loses no stratum.

## 7. The live reading

On hyphas a, b and c, with minds working, after the build:
- no reply is refused past the budget for act `10`;
- each hypha's exchange reads a full round of each peer's cover, page by
  page to `Store`, and fetches the discordant strata;
- then, read and reported as found: whether b and c read a at a's own
  height, and whether the minds carry a clock.
