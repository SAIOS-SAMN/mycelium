# 78.179 — The envelope opened into manifold construction

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the sensor's lift (`transducer::field::carrier`, `transducer::perspective::cover`); a word's evidence (`transducer::element::ReceiptsEnvelope`); the task the registry serves (`anastome::dialogue::TaskEnvelope`, `ACT_TASK`); the mind's intake (`Mind::perceive_envelope`); the shells of MU-RECURSION 64 and MU-CLIENT §3.2.

**Status: Ruled** (coordinator, 2026-11-06, under the operator's
delegation; posted on [#32](https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium/issues/32);
[78.180](78.180-delegated-rulings-2026-11-06.md) ruling 7). "Envelope"
named five different things, and none of them is a construction the
mathematics names. Each use opens into the manifold construction it stood
for. The code's identifiers keep their names until they are renamed
(§6; #165), and the documents cite them by those names until then.

## 1. The sample's envelope becomes the sample's manifold

The sample's "envelope" was its bounds: the extents, and the sphere the
dilation squared (MU-SENSING §8, a defect under law 11, now closed). The
sample is built as a manifold instead, one factor per phase. A phase's period is
read from the content, never from the extent alone, so an axis is not a
circle merely because it has an extent:
- **Circle.** A phase is a circle `S¹` of period `p` only where `p` is the
  least proper divisor of the extent `e` such that every site's symbol
  equals the symbol `p` steps on, taken round the extent. The far seam
  then glues to the near one. Site `s` is the turn `s/p`, with its winding
  `⌊s/p⌋` kept.
- **Interval.** Otherwise the phase is an interval chart of extent `e`,
  with boundary. This includes content whose period does not divide `e`.
  Its seam is a named boundary, held, never wrapped and never infinite
  (MU-SENSING §3.1).
- **The sample is the product of its phases.** A sample whose every phase
  is a circle is a torus `Tⁿ`. Where an axis is taken apart (MU-SENSING
  §3.6, #176), the sample is a fibration instead: the coarse phase is the
  base, the fine phase is the fibre, and the nesting is the bundle's
  coordinate.
- **The carriers** are built on branch `sensing-sample-manifold` (#32
  item 6): `SampleManifold`, with one `AxisManifold` per axis (`One` for
  a single phase, `Fibred` for a nested axis), each phase a
  `PhaseManifold` (`Circle { period }` or `Interval { extent }`), and
  `SampleManifold::torus` for the fully periodic case.
- **The perspectives' cover is the atlas**, and its cocycle is the gluing
  (MU-SENSING §4; MU-CLIENT 24.4). Each perspective's reading is a chart,
  and the turn on each overlap is its transition.
- **The seams are residue.** Where two charts meet and the gluing does
  not close, what is left on the seam is held as the cover's residue
  (`perspective::cover`'s `Residue` of contacts), never discarded.
- **The membrane is the only sphere.** No sphere is built about a
  sample's centre or from a squared radius. A sphere is the membrane or
  its image (S7, stage 3; `cga::membrane`, `cga::membrane_at`).

Two samples of different extent relate by the re-period of each phase
(MU-SENSING §3.1; 78.170 O4): a map between two products of circles and
intervals, never a dilation between two spheres. The "envelopes'
dilation" (`envelope_dilation`) was the defect this replaces, and PR #192
removed it (MU-SENSING §8).

## 2. `ReceiptsEnvelope` becomes the word's stalk

`ReceiptsEnvelope` (code citation "54.5 Road (d)"; MU-RECURSION 54.2,
54.5) carries what a word's address excludes: its citations (the nerve's
1-simplices with their transitions), the triple overlaps with a cell
attached (2-simplices), the derivations, the pivot certificates and each
demand's bodies. It is built as a sheaf:
- **The star.** The word is a vertex of the cover's nerve. Its star is the
  word together with the simplices that contain it: the words it cites
  (the edges) and the rounds it closes (the triangles).
- **The stalk.** The evidence is a sheaf over that star, and the word's
  stalk is what the sheaf holds at the word: the derivations, the
  certificates and the demands' bodies, each over the simplex it is
  evidence for.
- **Restriction.** Evidence over an edge restricts to each of its ends:
  a citation's transition is read on the cited word's chart as well as on
  the citing word's.
- **Gluing.** Two vantages' evidence agrees on their overlap, and where it
  agrees it glues into one section. Gluing is consilience (MU-RECURSION
  37.7; GLOSSARY *Consilience*): two independent courts re-deriving one
  word glue their sections over its star, and a disagreement is held as
  residue, never averaged.

So the word's evidence is its star in the nerve together with its stalk,
and restriction and gluing make it a sheaf. It is never part of the
address (54.2): two minds that reach one vertex by two derivations reach
one address and two stalks.

## 3. `TaskEnvelope` becomes the task: a family of manifolds

`TaskEnvelope` and "the signed work envelope" (MU-ANASTOME §6, `ACT_TASK`)
carry the task as the registry serves it: its citation, the train pairs
and the test inputs, never a withheld output. The task is built as a
family of manifolds:
- **Each sample is a manifold** (§1).
- **Each pair is two manifolds and the map between them** that the rule
  reconstructs: the input's manifold, the output's, and the motion ∘
  residue that carries one onto the other (MU-SENSING §3.9).
- **A test input is a manifold whose map is withheld.** The court holds
  the withheld output (78.180 ruling 4).

On the wire it is "the task". The registry serves the task; the mind
perceives the task.

## 4. The wire envelope is retired

The wire frame, one act's bytes with their length, is `Delimited` since
the names pass (#161; MU-ENGINEERING §9.3), and #178 retires its lane
byte. It is transport and carries no meaning. A reply's header length
(MU-DERIVATIONS D1.3, D4.6) is transport in the same way and is outside
this ruling.

## 5. MU-RECURSION 64's luminal envelope becomes an embedding

MU-RECURSION 64 named the outer conformal hull "the luminal envelope":
the luminal group `Spin⁺(4,1)` contains the harmonic `Spin(3)`, and "that
containment is the envelope". It is built as an embedding:
- **The harmonic core is an embedded submanifold**, the spacelike bodies
  (`s² = +r² > 0`), inside the conformal space.
- **The luminal ray lies on the null cone.** A ray is a null mode, carried
  between the bodies and never inside them. The core's strength, its
  harmonic moments, is what keeps a nucleus off the cone (64.2).
- **The containment is the embedding.** `Spin(3)`, the stabilizer of the
  core, sits inside `Spin⁺(4,1)`, the group that moves the conformal space
  (64.1).
- **Refraction is the restriction to the submanifold** (64.3): the ray's
  transport broken into the nuclei's frames, with the residue `γ_ab` the
  part that does not restrict.

## 6. The identifiers, renamed later (#165)

The documents cite the code by its present names until these renames
land. The names, and why they are chosen:

| Now | Renamed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `ReceiptsEnvelope` | `WordStalk` | It is the word's stalk (§2). A bare `Stalk` reads as the element of `transducer::stalk::Stalks`, the atlas that files each word's address over its chart, which is a different object. The accessors `Element::receipts` and `set_receipts` become `stalk` and `set_stalk` |
| `TaskEnvelope` | `TaskFamily` | It is the task as a family of manifolds (§3). A bare `Task` is already `transducer::family::Task`, the task held on the alphabet's section, and the served task is not that one. `TaskEnvelope::section` keeps its name; `encode_task_envelope` and `decode_task_envelope` become `encode_task` and `decode_task`; `PostedTask::envelope` becomes `PostedTask::family` |
| `perceive_envelope` | `perceive_task` | The mind perceives the task (§3); the argument it takes is the `TaskFamily` |

`envelope_dilation` needs no rename: PR #192 removed it with the
re-period.
