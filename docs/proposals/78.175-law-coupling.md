## 1. The law

- **Shared degrees are round** (ruled above). Every degree in this law is
  a rotation angle on a named turning plane. `hemisphere_l1` and `θ_L1`
  are named L1 readings.
- **F1 and F2 are one law: the dipole parcel and its viscosity, per plane**
  (docs/121 §9.1, §10.1).
  - A kind's frames are `A = W = 3Z − U` held and `Q = U` open.
  - A body carries `(z_k, q_k)` on each of its turning planes `k`,
    founded by the even split `((3Z − U)/n, U/n)` over its `n` planes
    (three, the turnings about axes 1, 2 and 3: `e₊₂e₊₃`, `e₊₃e₊₁`,
    `e₊₁e₊₂`). A bond or a peel changes one plane only: a bond closes
    open frames there, `q_k ↦ q_k − c`, `z_k ↦ z_k + c`; a peel is
    `c < 0`.
  - On each plane, with `t = q/z`, the **pole** is
    `σ_k = (z_k − q_k)/(z_k + q_k)`, continuous in `[−1, 1]`: `+1` at the
    closed pole, 0 at the throat, `−1` at the open pole. It is a ratio,
    which no metric enters, and its sign is `sign(z − q)`. It stands on
    the frames, never on the electrons' phase `R`, which is the parcel's
    phase. Where an angle is named for it, it is the L1 arc
    `θ_L1 = 180°·q/(z + q)`, a named L1 reading.
  - The **strength** is the open share `o_k = ν(t) = t/(1 + t) =
    q_k/(z_k + q_k)`, and `σ = 1 − 2o`.
  - The **inversion** reverts it: `ν(1/t) = 1 − ν(t)`; the frames
    exchanged take `o ↦ 1 − o` and `σ ↦ −σ`.
  - An open-heavy kind (`σ < 0`) needs `U > 3Z/2`.
- **F3, the dipole coupling on a turning plane** (docs/121 §9.2,
  §10.2–§10.4, §11).
  - The field of a body's range acts as one, on one named turning plane
    (two frame units of one square, `forms::coordinate::Plane::Turning`):
    ```
    F = Σ_j w_j·σ_j·o_j·n_j·h_j / Σ_j w_j
    ```
    with `w_j > 0` the holdings, `n = M/(|g| + M)` the ring law's nearness
    and `h = hemisphere_l1(Δ) = 1 − Δ/90°` the alignment: the named L1
    reading of the round separation `Δ ∈ [0°, 180°]` on that plane,
    aligned toward `+1`, uncoupled at 90°, opposed toward `−1`, refused
    outside `[0°, 180°]`. Each term stands in `[−1, 1]`, so `|F| ≤ 1`.
  - A **rate** is a share `x ∈ (−1, 1)` of the luminal bound. It moves by
    LAW-COORDINATE's flow law (78.173 §1, `x ⊕ y = (x + y)/(1 + x·y)`):
    ```
    x′ = x ⊕ y,     y = −σ·F·o·(x̄ ⊖ x),     a ⊖ b = (a − b)/(1 − a·b)
    ```
    with `x̄` the range's holding-weighted rate and `⊖` the flow law's
    inverse. With `|F| ≤ 1`, `o ≤ 1` and `|x̄ ⊖ x| < 1`, `|y| < 1`, so the
    rate stays inside the bound by construction. Opposed dipoles
    (`σ·F < 0`) lock, drawn toward `x̄`; aligned ones (`σ·F > 0`) split.
    A body alone in its range is untouched.
  - **Neighbours** are the bodies whose own enclosures meet:
    `|R_i − R_j| ≤ R_i·e_i + R_j·e_j`, with `e = m/(m + H(<R))`.
  - Per docs/121 §11 and §13 the same law reads at every level whose lane
    holds a rate; the state level carries no radial law (§13), so v1
    states the coupling on the phase level's turning planes.
- **F4, the light ledger, the coupling's conservation** (docs/122 §2
  laws 3–4, §4). Every bell, light made = received + drained. Light is
  received on a body's unlocked face, `1 − δ` on the continuum and `δ` on
  the vacuum, with `δ = H/(r + H)` the carry or pinch share
  (`forms::passage::Frame::share`); the rest drains.
- **Exact.** Every reading is exact over ℚ: no float, no trigonometric
  value, no square read as a quantity.

## 2. What it replaces

- **A bare sign for the pole and the alignment** (docs/121 §9.1: a dipole
  is never a bare sign). The pole is the frames' ratio per plane; the
  alignment is the hemisphere's L1 reading of a round separation.
- **`repel`, `shear`, the field exchange and the band** (docs/121 §7,
  §10.5, §13), in each kernel that holds them: one coupling whose sign
  the dipoles' standing gives, bending rates by the flow law, with no
  halved step and no squared rest.
- **Frames setting cooling and starlight** (docs/122 §0, §3): light is
  taken by the unlocked face and booked on one ledger.

## 8. Open items

- *Settled (ruled 2026-11-06):* which circle shared degrees stand on. They
  are round, rotation angles on Clifford turning planes; an L1 projection
  is a named reading (`hemisphere_l1`, `θ_L1`). The ratios `σ` and `o`
  are metric-free and stand either way.
- **Stated dipoles off the frames.** Cases 1–5 of §5 state the body as
  `σ = +1`, `o = 1/2` and neighbours as `σ = ±1`, `o = 1/2`. No frames
  hold these: on frames `σ = 1 − 2o`. The form takes them as stated
  dipoles (`Dipole::stated`). Whether a kernel's dipole is ever stated
  otherwise than by its frames is for the peers to settle; case 6 is the
  same law read on frames.
- **Which plane's dipole couples.** A body carries a dipole per plane
  (F1); the field on τ₁₂ reads each body's dipole there. That the field
  on plane `k` takes each body's `(σ_k, o_k)` is the reading this form
  assumes; the peers confirm it with the fixtures.
- **The range's holding-weighted rate conserved.** docs/121 §10.6 names a
  witness: `x̄` kept where the field exchanges between neighbours. v1
  takes `x̄` as an input and states no exchange between neighbours; the
  exchange and its witness are the next statement.
- **The levels.** docs/121 §11 reads the coupling at the quantum level on
  `G`, which holds no rate yet (docs/120 §9); v1 states the phase level.
- **The ledger's other forms.** docs/122 §1 and §2 book bond, heat,
  motion and potential beside light; v1 states the light ledger alone.
  The chemical grip's energy, where emitted light starts its passage, and
  potential's zero stay open (docs/122 §6).
