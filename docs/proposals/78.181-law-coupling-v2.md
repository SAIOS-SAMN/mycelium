## 1. What v1 collapsed

v1 F1 held a kind as `A = 3Z − U` and `Q = U`: one count, `3Z`, split by
the electrons' unpaired ends `U`. The open pole was the remainder of the
held one, so it was never derived, and the electrons, which are neither
nucleon, stood in for a nucleon pole. Every reading over it (the pole
`σ`, the fluidity `o`, the even split per plane, `Parcel::kind`,
`NotFrames`) inherits the collapse. Xylarium's base equations
(`W = 3Z − U`, `W/3Z`, `t = U/W`) read the same single face; netstratum's
`chem::frames` read it with the letters exchanged (corrected in
netstratum #176).

## 2. The law

Physical labels come first; each kernel's letters are a seam reading
(§3).

- **The poles are the nucleons'.** Each nucleon stands in three
  dimensions. A body of `p` protons and `n` neutrons, `w = p + n`, holds
  `3p` proton dimensions and `3n` neutron dimensions, `3w` in all. Both
  counts are read from the body; neither is a remainder of the other.
- **The split stands on the join's axis plane.** On xylarium's
  `S¹¹ = S¹_axis * S⁹_iso`, the two strata are the axis plane's shares:
  the proton share `p/w` and the neutron share `n/w`, summing to 1. The
  ratio is `t = p/n`, protons over neutrons, one physical ratio in every
  kernel's letters.
- **Each kernel's orientation is declared, and its antipole applied.**
  Which stratum a kernel holds and which stands open is that kernel's
  orientation, declared as data (LAW-TRAVERSAL v1, [78.176](78.176-law-traversal.md) §1). Its held
  share `c` and open share `d = 1 − c` are then fixed, and the antipole
  is applied by the face map `I`: share `↦ 1 −` share, pole `↦` the
  other pole.
- **The dimensional inversion between kernels reads `t ↦ 1/t`, and is
  carried by the twist of the singularity.** Across a tube joining two
  kernels of opposite orientation, the same physical stratum stands on
  the other pole: `c ↔ d`. It is the only inversion; `t` itself is never
  inverted at the seam.
  - **A motion, never a substitution.** The exchange is not written as
    `t ↦ 1/t` on a stored ratio, nor as a relabelling of the poles. It
    is reconstructed as the twist about the singular stratum of the
    manifold: the wholly closed pole `n_origin` (78.172 §1, "the
    singularity"), where the join degenerates to one factor, the place a
    bare proton `(1, 0)` stands. On the join it is the weighted Seifert
    action (xylarium #183, #128; `xyl_core::hopf::turn`), a rotor, so
    the exchange is read off the motion's result, never stated beside it.
  - **Turns, winding kept.** The twist is held as turns of its planes,
    exact fractions composed by addition, with the winding
    `K = ⌊|θ|/360⌋` kept beside the phase and never reduced (law 11).
    Two twists in one sense return the shares and carry one winding more;
    only the reverse twist undoes one.
  - **The linking is kept.** A twist is a rotation, so the strata's
    linking `Lk = ±1` (xylarium's `STRATA_LINKING`) is kept through it
    and the poles are never decoupled. A reflection would reverse `Lk`;
    the pole swap of LAW-PASSAGE v3 (a reflection in `e₊`, a P motion)
    stays the passage within one enclosure and is not this inversion.
  - **No open-ended singularity** (MU-CONCORDANCE 2.4): the twist about
    the singular stratum is how a body reaches the far face of its
    throat; every horizon has its far face, and the twist is the motion
    that joins them.
- **R, the electrons' phase, is the axis plane's phase `φ₀`, in the
  turning only.** It is a turn, an exact fraction of 360°, joining the
  common turn; never a count, never a pole and never a factor in a share
  (xylarium #183: the Z–Q stretch is the axis plane's share, not its
  phase).
- **The electrons offset the poles by their bound share `g`.** `g` is
  the polarity equilibrium, the held share at the body's own place
  (netstratum docs/105 §3), with no phase and no halving; `u = 1 − g` is
  the share left open. `g ∈ [0, 1]`.
- **The push reads the open charge, and its antipole the held.** The
  protons' charge left open by their bound electrons is
  ```
  π = p·(1 − g)/w = p·u/w,        π̄ = (n + p·g)/w = 1 − π
  ```
  read at the unlocked face. A bare nucleus (`g = 0`) pushes with its
  whole proton share; a neutral body (`g = 1`) does not push. `I` exchanges the two across the faces:
  in netstratum's orientation `π` stands on the continuum and `π̄` on the
  vacuum; in an orientation that holds the protons, the same `π` stands
  on the held face, by the antipole.
- **A bond moves share through the electrons.** A bond binds electrons;
  each carries its proton's charge from the open stratum to the held one
  through `g` at the body's place, so a bond moves `π` into `π̄`. `3w` and both nucleon counts are kept.
- **The electrons' unpaired ends `U` are the coupling's strength, apart
  from the poles.** `U/3w` reads the bond ends available, and enters F3
  as the strength where v1 entered the fluidity `o`; it is never a pole.
- **Exact.** Every reading is exact over ℚ, every phase a fraction of a
  turn; no float, no trigonometric value, no square read as a quantity.

## 3. The seam

| | proton | neutron |
|---|---|---|
| mycelium, xylarium letter | Z | Q |
| netstratum letter | Q | Z |
| netstratum's pole | open | held |
| xylarium's pole (proposed: netstratum's antipole, docs/97) | held | open |

`t = protons/neutrons` in every row: netstratum's `Q/Z`, mycelium's and
xylarium's `Z/Q`. A kernel states its axis share by its physical
meaning, so the seam shows the exchange `c ↔ d` and never carries it in
the letters.

**Mycelium's orientation** (confirmed by mycelium's operator,
2026-11-11). `forms` reads in xylarium's letters and in xylarium's
orientation: the protons held, the neutrons open. Xylarium's operator
confirms it for xylarium. `forms` still states every reading on the
physical labels and takes each kernel's orientation as data.

## 4. Where it meets xylarium #183, and where not yet

- **Meets:** the split is the axis plane's share split; `φ₀` is the
  axis plane's phase in the common turn; `Lk = ±1` is the strata's
  linking.
- **Not yet: the planes.** v1 founded a body by an even split over three
  turning planes (`e₊₂e₊₃`, `e₊₃e₊₁`, `e₊₁e₊₂`). #183's join has six
  complex planes on twelve axes, the axis plane and five on `S⁹_iso`.
  v2 states the nucleon split on the axis plane alone; what the five
  isoclinic planes carry of a body, and how a bond's move on one plane
  reads on the join, is open (§7).
- **Not yet: xylarium's Q-face bench.** Both candidates benched on
  2026-11-11 (xylarium branch q-face-bench, 593532c), `W_Q = 3cap − U`
  and `W_Q = 3cap − E`, put an electron count into the open pole. Under
  §2 neither is the open pole: the open pole is the other nucleon count,
  offset by `g`. The bench's third column is `3n`, with `π` from `g`.
- **Corrected from v1 and LAW-TRAVERSAL v1:** the whole kept across the
  antipolar exchange is `3w`, both nucleon counts, not `3Z`.

## 5. Proposed fixtures

Pure arithmetic on §2, for the joint read; `(p, n)` a body's nucleons,
`g` its electrons' bound share.

| Body `(p, n)` | `w` | proton share | neutron share | `t` | `t` under `I` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(2, 2)` | 4 | `1/2` | `1/2` | `1` | `1`, the fixed point |
| `(3, 4)` | 7 | `3/7` | `4/7` | `3/4` | `4/3` |
| `(1, 0)` | 1 | `1` | `0` | none | none: the bare proton stands on one pole |
| `(26, 30)` | 56 | `13/28` | `15/28` | `13/15` | `15/13` |

The push on `(3, 4)`:

| `g` | `π` | `π̄` |
|---|---|---|
| `0`, bare | `3/7` | `4/7` |
| `1/3` | `2/7` | `5/7` |
| `1`, neutral | `0` | `1` |

- `π + π̄ = 1` in every row; a bond that raises `g` from `0` to `1/3`
  moves `1/7` from `π` to `π̄`, with `w` and both counts kept.
- The twist and its reverse compose to the identity on the shares, on
  `t` and on the winding (LAW-TRAVERSAL v1 fixture 1, read as a motion).
  Two twists in one sense return the shares and `t` and read one winding
  more, `K + 1`, never reduced.
- Refused: a body of no nucleons (`w = 0`); `g` outside `[0, 1]`; a
  phase not an exact fraction of a turn.
