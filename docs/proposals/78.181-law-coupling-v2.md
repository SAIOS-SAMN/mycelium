## 1. What v1 collapsed

v1 F1 held a kind as `A = 3Z − U` and `Q = U`: one count, `3Z`, split by
the electrons' unpaired ends `U`. The open pole was the remainder of the
held one, so it was never derived, and the electrons, which are neither
nucleon, stood in for a nucleon pole. Every reading over it (the pole
`σ`, the fluidity `o`, the even split per plane, `Parcel::kind`,
`NotFrames`) inherits the collapse. Xylarium's base equations
(`W = 3Z − U`, `W/3Z`, `t = U/W`) read the same single face; netstratum's
`chem::frames` read it with the letters exchanged (corrected in
netstratum #176).

## 2. The law

Physical labels come first; each kernel's letters are a seam reading
(§3).

- **The poles are the nucleons'.** Each nucleon stands in three
  dimensions. A body of `p` protons and `n` neutrons, `w = p + n`, holds
  `3p` proton dimensions and `3n` neutron dimensions, `3w` in all. Both
  counts are read from the body; neither is a remainder of the other.
- **The split stands on the join's axis plane.** On xylarium's
  `S¹¹ = S¹_axis * S⁹_iso`, the two strata are the axis plane's shares:
  the proton share `p/w` and the neutron share `n/w`, summing to 1. The
  ratio is `t = p/n`, protons over neutrons, one physical ratio in every
  kernel's letters.
- **Each kernel's orientation is declared, and its antipole applied.**
  Which stratum a kernel holds and which stands open is that kernel's
  orientation, declared as data (LAW-TRAVERSAL v1, [78.176](78.176-law-traversal.md) §1). Its held
  share `c` and open share `d = 1 − c` are then fixed, and the antipole
  is applied by the face map `I`: share `↦ 1 −` share, pole `↦` the
  other pole.
- **The dimensional inversion between kernels reads `t ↦ 1/t`, and is
  carried by the twist of the singularity.** Across a tube joining two
  kernels of opposite orientation, the same physical stratum stands on
  the other pole: `c ↔ d`. It is the only inversion; `t` itself is never
  inverted at the seam.
  - **A motion, never a substitution.** The exchange is not written as
    `t ↦ 1/t` on a stored ratio, nor as a relabelling of the poles. It
    is reconstructed as the twist that carries one singular stratum of
    the join onto the other. The join degenerates to one factor at each
    end (`S¹_axis` alone, or `S⁹_iso` alone), the place a bare proton
    `(1, 0)` stands being one (78.172 §1: `n_origin`, "the
    singularity"); the exchange is read off the motion's result, never
    stated beside it.
  - **The twist is the mixing quarter turn, not a Seifert turn**
    (xylarium's reconstruction, `xyl_core::hopf` in xylarium #184, main 16c31d2, test
    `no_weighted_turn_moves_a_plane_share_and_a_mixing_quarter_turn_exchanges_them`).
    A weighted Seifert turn `z_p ↦ e^{i·w_p·θ}·z_p` keeps every plane's
    share: the shares are its invariants, not its moves. The exchange is
    the quarter turn of the axis plane into one isoclinic plane `q`, each
    of the axis plane's two axes turned 90° toward plane `q`'s
    corresponding axis: `(z₀, z_q) ↦ (−z_q, z₀)` (in `hopf`,
    `Turn{1 → 2q+1, 90°}` then `Turn{2 → 2q+2, 90°}`). It carries
    `S¹_axis` onto plane `q`'s circle in `S⁹_iso`, the one degenerate
    stratum onto the other, and fixes the other four isoclinic planes
    pointwise: that is the exact sense in which it twists about the
    singular strata. No rotation fixing either stratum pointwise can
    exchange the shares. It is complex linear, so it commutes with the
    Hopf action and descends to `CP⁵`.
  - **Turns, winding kept.** The twist is held as turns, exact fractions
    of 360° composed by addition, with the winding `K = ⌊|θ|/360⌋` kept
    beside the phase and never reduced (law 11). Its effect on the shares
    is read exactly from the motion, `(c, d) ↦ (d, c)`; no point of the
    join is placed by a square root of a share, and no `|z|²` is read as
    a quantity in `forms`. Twice it is the half turn `(z₀, z_q) ↦ (−z₀,
    −z_q)`, the shares returned and the phase not; four times it is the
    identity, read with `K + 1`. Only the reverse twist undoes one.
  - **The linking is kept.** A twist is a rotation, so the strata's
    linking `Lk = ±1` (xylarium's `STRATA_LINKING`) is kept through it
    and the poles are never decoupled. A reflection would reverse `Lk`;
    the pole swap of LAW-PASSAGE v3 (a reflection in `e₊`, a P motion)
    stays the passage within one enclosure and is not this inversion.
  - **No open-ended singularity** (MU-CONCORDANCE 2.4): the twist about
    the singular stratum is how a body reaches the far face of its
    throat; every horizon has its far face, and the twist is the motion
    that joins them.
- **R, the electrons' phase, is the axis plane's phase `φ₀`, in the
  turning only.** It is a turn, an exact fraction of 360°, joining the
  common turn; never a count, never a pole and never a factor in a share
  (xylarium #183: the Z–Q stretch is the axis plane's share, not its
  phase).
- **The electrons offset the poles by their bound share `g`, the held
  share at the body's own place.** Electrons bind at the held face and
  free at the unlocked face, so their bound share is the face's held
  share (netstratum docs/105 §3), with no phase and no halving; phase
  stays in heat stripping and decay. At the body's place it is read on
  LAW-PASSAGE v3's face, `δ = H/(H + R)` (78.172 §1), `H` the hold
  enclosed within the body's reach `R`: the core's hold and every body
  nearer the throat (netstratum `Enclosed::hold`, xylarium
  `Substrate::held_within`), one set on both sides, the inversion applied
  to the shares only:
  - in netstratum's orientation the held share is `g = 1 − δ`, so
    `u = δ`;
  - in xylarium's orientation, by the antipole, the same place reads its
    held share as `δ`, so `g = δ`, `u = 1 − δ` (`xyl_core::shares::faces`:
    the convergent face is `δ`, the divergent `1 − δ`).
  `g ∈ [0, 1]`.
- **The push reads the open charge, and its antipole the held.** The
  protons' charge left open by their bound electrons is
  ```
  π = p·(1 − g)/w = p·u/w,        π̄ = (n + p·g)/w = 1 − π
  ```
  read at the unlocked face: netstratum's `π = p·δ/w`, xylarium's
  `π = p·(1 − δ)/w`. The two kernels' pushes are each other's face
  inversion (`I` on the proton share alone, `δ ↔ 1 − δ`), and need not
  sum to 1; each kernel's antipole is its own held stratum,
  `π̄ = (n + p·g)/w = 1 − π`. A bare nucleus (`g = 0`)
  pushes with its whole proton share; a neutral body (`g = 1`) does not
  push. The electrons' unpaired over the whole, `U/w`, is not the push. `I` exchanges the two across the faces:
  in netstratum's orientation `π` stands on the continuum and `π̄` on the
  vacuum; in an orientation that holds the protons, the same `π` stands
  on the held face, by the antipole.
- **A bond moves share through the electrons.** A bond binds electrons;
  each carries its proton's charge from the open stratum to the held one
  through `g` at the body's place, so a bond moves `π` into `π̄`. `3w` and both nucleon counts are kept.
- **The electrons' unpaired ends `U` are the coupling's strength, apart
  from the poles.** `U/3w` reads the bond ends available, and enters F3
  as the strength where v1 entered the fluidity `o`; it is never a pole.
- **Exact.** Every reading is exact over ℚ, every phase a fraction of a
  turn; no float, no trigonometric value, no square read as a quantity.

## 3. The seam

| | proton | neutron |
|---|---|---|
| mycelium, xylarium letter | Z | Q |
| netstratum letter | Q | Z |
| netstratum's pole | open | held |
| xylarium's pole (proposed: netstratum's antipole, docs/97) | held | open |

`t = protons/neutrons` in every row: netstratum's `Q/Z`, mycelium's and
xylarium's `Z/Q`.

**Windings at the seam: `K_ψ = 2·K_vector`.** Netstratum reads the
exchange on the share circle, its Möbius pass `(δ, ψ, s) ↦ (1 − δ,
ψ + ½, −s)`, whose angle `ψ` is twice the join vector's: half a turn of
`ψ` is the vector's quarter turn. Four quarter turns read the vector's
`K + 1` and netstratum's `K_ψ + 2`. One turn carries three angles: the
versor's half-angle (45° for the exchange), the vector's (90°, where
xylarium's `rotor::Turn` keeps `K`) and the share circle's (180°). This
law states `K` on the vector. A winding carried across a tube is
stated with its circle named, or it doubles or halves.

Xylarium's pole axis `X(t)` (`circuit::pole_point`,
the held pole at `t = 0`) takes open over held as its coordinate, which
is `n/p = 1/t` in this law's letters: a seam reading of the same
quantity, never an inversion applied at the seam. A kernel states its axis share by its physical
meaning, so the seam shows the exchange `c ↔ d` and never carries it in
the letters.

**Mycelium's orientation** (confirmed by mycelium's operator,
2026-11-11). `forms` reads in xylarium's letters and in xylarium's
orientation: the protons held, the neutrons open. Xylarium's operator
confirms it for xylarium. `forms` still states every reading on the
physical labels and takes each kernel's orientation as data.

- **The push offset by its resistance** (open for all three; xylarium's
  operator, xylarium #195/#196, 2026-11-11: every push is offset by its
  resistance through the flow law, `p_net = p ⊕ (−r)`, balancing at
  `p = r`, a negative net carrying the holder back). Share and rate are two
  readings of one dipole (`xyl_core::shares::Faces::push`): a share `c`
  with complement `d = 1 − c` has the push `p = c − d = 2c − 1` and the
  pole `t = d/c`, and the flow law's `D(p) = (1 + p)/(1 − p) = c/d = 1/t`.
  So the complement and the flow inverse are one antipole (the share
  `1 − c` has the push `−p`), and since `D(x ⊕ y) = D(x)·D(y)` a share `a`
  offset by a resistance share `b` reads `t_net = t_a/t_b`,
  `c_net = 1/(1 + t_a/t_b)`: exact, with no square. On protons 3,
  neutrons 4 at `δ = 1/4`, the proton share `a = 3/7` (`t_a = 4/3`):
  - xylarium (`g = δ`, `t_b = 3`): `t_net = 4/9`, `c_net = 9/13`, push

  - netstratum (`g = 1 − δ`, `t_b = 1/3`): `t_net = 4`, `c_net = 1/5`,
    push `−3/5`, carrying the holder back.

  (An earlier reading here composed the raw shares `3/7` and `1/4` as
  pushes; a share of `3/7` is the push `−1/7`, so that reading is
  withdrawn.) Xylarium proposes: `π` stays a share of charge with
  `π̄ = 1 − π`, and the resistance offsets it in push coordinates, which
  in shares is the pole ratio. Against §2's linear `π = (p/w)·(1 − g)`
  this is a different value (`9/13` against `9/28` for xylarium).
  - **Netstratum, derived from its own laws** (its docs/104 §1.2,

    `π̄ = (n + p·g)/w = 1 − π`; resistance composes by the flow law on the

  - **Standing:** netstratum's derivation keeps §2's linear push and puts
