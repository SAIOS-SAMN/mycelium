# 78.174 — The economy as motions

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the court on each hypha (holdings, settlement, the POW++ proofs); the economy's carriers (`economy::modes::Modes`, retiring `economy::Mu`); the coordinate (`forms::coordinate`, LAW-COORDINATE v4, [78.173](78.173-law-coordinate.md)); the compute lease; the recycled pool. The specification it rules is [MU-ECONOMY](../MU-ECONOMY.md); this proposal records the operator's rulings on it.

**Status: Ruled** for the points of §1 (operator, 2026-11-05), recorded
2026-11-06. §2 lists what stays open. The rulings were given on

## 1. The rulings (operator, 2026-11-05)

1. **No flat ledger, rows or cells.** Receipts are per-signer lines in a
   causal partial order (cones), never totalised. TUniversal height is a
   shared coordinate, never a total order. The ledger is one
   multi-dimensional ledger: mass per lane, per register layer and per
   grade, never summed (the archived MU-RECURSION 73.9). MU-ECONOMY §2.1.
2. **A holding is a point, a coordinate.** Its lanes are vectors
   (`Modes`, never `Mu` scalars). Estates nest as origins, and a region is
   a reading. A balance and the supply are readings, never stored.
   MU-ECONOMY §2.2, §6. The modes of a holding are its coordinate's
   components (closing MU-ECONOMY §12 item 1).
3. **The four proofs are relations between motions:**
   - useful work (POUW): an orbit carrying a deformation with less
     residue;
   - verification (POWV): an observer re-traversing to the same point;
   - convergence (POWC): independent orbits falling into one well;
   - reduction (POWR): one orbit absorbing a family.

   A payout is proven by re-traversal. MU-ECONOMY §0, §5.3.
4. **One closure, one point.** A concurrent second claim of one closure
   is an equivocation, and it is burned. Claims unordered in time are
   held as a residue, never paid. F2a's equal shares are retired.
   MU-ECONOMY §3.4, §11.4.
5. **`K` means closed orbits.** The receipt index is renamed by function:
   the line position `i`. MU-ECONOMY §2.2, §12 item 3.
6. **POWC is widened.** It includes lineages sharing a cause, provided
   they disagreed before meeting; MU-RECURSION 74 (CV4) stands.
   MU-ECONOMY §0, §12 item 6.
7. **Pay by level** (the ledger's grade axis):
   - letters are never paid;
   - words: POWR, plus citation shares;
   - conjugate classes: POWC;
   - sentences: POUW;
   - POWV at every level.

   A seated mind is paid to its own key (Concordance v5 6.2a).
8. **The compute lease.** Escrow is a claim on issuance, and the yield is
   paid for verified traversal. An **availability yield** is also paid on
   escrowed idle capacity, from the recycled pool (MU-ECONOMY §7.2:
   dissipation recycles), never minted, so the supply invariant (§6)
   holds. MU-ECONOMY §5.2.
9. **A mode's amount is its share of the frame, never S mass.** A
   recolour pays G by its share. This closes MU-ECONOMY §12 item 4.
10. **Tile turns are H modes,** read in degrees on their named plane.
    Concordance 5.4: H is paid the exact degrees traversed per named
    plane, with no floor, and holonomy is never priced.
11. **Reflections get a lane of their own.** An improper motion (a
    reflection) is carried on a lane opened by a ruled act agreed between
    kernels: the first opened lane, with the proposed name P (parity;
    its opening is proposed in 78.178). Until P opens, a mirrored
    closure's pay is held as a claim, never paid without a mode.

## 2. Open, not ruled

Room over modes (MU-ECONOMY §12 item 5), the ask under modes (§12 item 8)
and the Note's mode-0 receipt were ruled by the coordinator on 2026-11-06,
under the operator's delegation: room and the ask are readings per lane
and per mode, and a mode-0 receipt is a founding-mode receipt
([78.180](78.180-delegated-rulings-2026-11-06.md) ruling 3; MU-ECONOMY
§2.5). What stays open:

- **The availability yield's measure.** Ruling 8 fixes its source (the
  recycled pool) and its object (escrowed idle capacity); the reading
  that sizes it per lane and per mode is to be stated before it is
  built.
