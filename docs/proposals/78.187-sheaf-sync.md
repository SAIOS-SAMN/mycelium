# 78.187 — LAW-SHEAF-SYNC v1: the language is synchronised by descent along its charts

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the sheaf sync (`transducer::sheaf`, `transducer::stalk`); the digest and fetch acts (`ACT_DIGESTS` 0x10, `ACT_FETCH`; `anastome::dialogue`); the court's answer (`judge/syllabus.rs`); every asker: peer hyphas (`exchange.rs`, `judge/fold.rs`, the server's boot) and minds (mycelium-client, `src/client/sdk/mind/learn.rs`, `src/client/sdk/session.rs`).

**Status:** proposed 2026-11-20 for #349, under Concordance §0 AMENDING, for the maintainer's approval. It is taken through [MU-RELEASE §3a](../MU-RELEASE.md#3a-how-a-hypha-standard-becomes-a-kernel-standard) as a network clause, 4.6 (MU-CONCORDANCE §13, draft 6). Not signed, not in force.

## 1. The defect, read live

Asked with any voice but the court's own, the digest act answers with one
`StratumDigest` (`inv`, `count`, `root`) for every stratum of the whole
language, unpaged (`judge/syllabus.rs`, `sheaf::digests`). On hypha a on
2026-11-19 that reply was 1.3–2.1 MB against the wire budget
(`BODY_BUDGET`, 768 KiB), and every one was refused whole (#385 names the
act in the refusal). As a result:
- b and c read a at h ≈ 4,000 while a stands at h 13,524, so the mesh is split around a;
- minds cannot learn a's language and read "no clock carried".

The reply grows with the language, so any budget is outgrown. A larger
budget postpones the refusal and does not remove it. The fetch has the same
shape on its request side: `sheaf::have` sends every address the asker
holds in a stratum, so a wide stratum outgrows the request.

## 2. The law

The stalk tree is already a trie in the language's own coordinates. A
node is a chart; its children are the charts it refines, each by one
`ChartAxis` (`stalk/stratum.rs`); `Invariant::peel` is the step back up
(A21: a stratum loses one whole coordinate). The sync descends that tree.
It descends no tree of bytes.

- **A node's reading.** A node at chart `c` reads as
  `(count(c), root(c))`. `count(c)` is the members of every stratum at or
  below `c`. `root(c)` is the locator over the root of `c`'s own members
  (`stratum_root`, as today) followed by each child's `(axis, root)` in
  axis order. A root only locates (MU-ANTI-FLATTENING 13): it says where
  two holders differ and admits nothing. Every word is still admitted by
  reconstruction (Concordance 4.4).
- **The request** names a chart: `[ACT_DIGESTS][voice 32][chart][resume]`.
  The chart is written out axis by axis. `resume` is the child axis to
  start the page from, or none. The 33-byte request
  `[ACT_DIGESTS][voice 32]` keeps the act as already signed (§5).
- **The reply** is the node's own `(count, root)`, then, for each child
  from `resume` in axis order, `[axis][count][root]`, filled to the wire
  budget. Where children remain, it ends with the next child's axis as the
  resume. The cover digest (`sign_cover_digest`) follows as today. Every
  reply fits the budget by construction.
- **Descent.** The asker compares each child with its own node at the same
  chart. Where the roots locate each other, the subtree is glued and
  nothing more is asked. Where they do not, or the child is absent here,
  it descends. A node whose own-members root differs is a discordant
  stratum, fetched as before. The exchanges number about the discordant
  strata times the depth, plus one per page. Charts that agree cost
  nothing past their parent's page.
- **The fetch inside a stratum.** In place of the full have-list, the
  asker sends the roots of the stratum's address ranges, split at the
  midpoint of the address order and descended the same way to ranges
  small enough to list. An address only locates (MU-HOSTING §5a.10), so a
  range root is lawful. `serve` keeps filling its reply to the budget.
- **The cost to the server.** Each node's `(count, root)` is held in the
  stalks and updated along the path of a word as it lands. A request
  reads one node and its children, and is charged one stroke per child
  read (`Budget`, 78.153). Nothing walks the whole language per request.

**The signature is unchanged.** `sign_cover_digest` signs the cover roots,
the ruled root and the room (`cover_signed_prefix`), not the strata list.
Before step 4 it must be read whether the cover's roots bind the stalk
tree's root. If they do not, `root(∅)` joins the signed prefix, and that
change is part of this clause.

## 3. Fixtures (MU-RELEASE §3a step 3)

Charts are written as count axes (`Invariant::counts`); `w₁…` are word
addresses. Tree A holds strata `(1): {w₁}`, `(1,2): {w₂, w₃}`,
`(1,3): {w₄}` and `(2): {w₅}`. Each fixture's outputs are exact decoded
values. Roots are compared only by whether they locate each other, never
by their bytes (Concordance 1.4, 9.1).

1. **The root page.** The request for `∅` on A reads `count 5`, and its
   children are `[(1): count 4, (2): count 1]` in axis order, with no
   resume.
2. **The signed request.** The 33-byte request on A reads the four
   strata, flat and in chart order, `(1)`, `(1,2)`, `(1,3)`, `(2)`, each
   with its count and its own-members root. Under a budget that holds
   two of them, it reads `(1)` and `(1,2)` only: the reply is filled to
   the budget and never refused whole.
3. **Glued.** A against a copy of A: the root roots locate each other.
   There is one exchange, and no stratum is discordant.
4. **One word apart.** B is A with `w₆` added to `(1,3)`. B descending A
   asks `∅`, then `(1)`, then reads `(1,3)` discordant. It never asks
   `(2)` or `(1,2)`. Exactly one stratum is fetched, and B's roots change
   only at `(1,3)`, `(1)` and `∅`.
5. **A stratum absent.** C is A without `(2)`. C descending A reads `(2)`
   as discordant from the root page alone.
6. **Pages.** A node with children on axes `1..=k`, under a budget that
   holds `m` children per page, answers in `⌈k/m⌉` pages. Each page's
   resume is the first child it did not send. The pages together are
   every child once, in axis order.
7. **Ranges in a stratum.** Two holders of one stratum whose members
   differ by the single address `w` reach `{w}` as the lacking set by
   range descent, without either sending its whole member list.

## 4. What it replaces

- The flat `encode_digests_reply` over `sheaf::digests` as the sync. It
  is kept only as the answer to the 33-byte request, filled to the
  budget (§5).
- The full have-list in the fetch.
- The court's own-voice special case (`ruled_digests_for`) stays: it reads
  the ruled syllabus, which the same tree serves.

## 5. Transition (Concordance §0, CLAUSE BY CLAUSE)

A kernel that has not signed 4.6 is read under the act as signed. Its
33-byte request is answered in the reply shape it already reads, the flat
strata in chart order, filled to the budget (fixture 2). While the
language fits one reply, that is the whole sync. Past it, that kernel
syncs only the strata that fit. The reply is partial, never refused, and
the hypha logs it as held for 4.6. Hyphas sign with the release that
builds it. Minds sign as their kernel's readers descend
(mycelium-client). Netstratum and xylarium sign as their kernels re-pin.
The server answers both forms, so the two sides land in either order.

## 6. The live reading (the pass condition)

On hyphas a, b and c with the build:
- no reply is refused past the budget for act `10`;
- b and c read a at a's own height, so the split is closed;
- both minds carry a clock, with `grounded > 0` and `answered > 0`.
