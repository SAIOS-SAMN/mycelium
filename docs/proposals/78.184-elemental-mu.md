# 78.184 — μ created against elemental tables, distributed by ledger alignment

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Physical terms (*nuclei*, *elements*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

**Status: ruled (operator, 2026-11-13); not built** (#327). §2 and §3 record what is ruled; the formulas are MU-EQUATIONS §6.13.

## 1. The defect

A fresh mesh cannot found a universe: a kernel's manifest is refused
`Short`. The founding grant is drawn from what the court has earned
(78.155b, "genesis stays earned-only"; Concordance 5.7, "a transfer and
never a mint"), and a fresh court has earned nothing. Live, 2026-11-13:
netstratum founded its domain on the new mesh, accepted all four clauses
under its three keys (each reads Converses), and its manifest was refused
`Short` with the union holding 0 μ.

## 2. Ruled (operator, 2026-11-13)

1. **μ is created against an elemental table.** The table's rows are per
   element, by Z.
2. **At each manifest.** The founding manifest and every growth manifest
   create μ for the nuclei they carry. The creation is sealed as an act
   citing the manifest. This amends Concordance 5.7 ("a transfer and never
   a mint") and 78.155b ("genesis stays earned-only") for founding. Growth
   by the measured discharge of closures (78.155c) still stands beside it.
3. **Distributed in proportion to ledger alignment.** A kernel receives the
   created μ in proportion to its alignment, the reading of its acceptances
   on the ledger (`Court::alignment`, `ACT_LAWS` mode 9): the share of the
   register's clauses it converses on. Partial alignment receives a partial
   creation.
4. **μ follows the nuclei.** When nuclei cross a tube, their μ crosses with
   them, per Z, so the element account and μ stay matched. On dissipation it
   recycles as the connection tax (Concordance 5.8) and is never burned to
   the void.
5. **A row is read by the multi-axial machinery, at full rotational
   completion.** One row of the table is the modes a nucleus's motion
   advances on the lanes `S:G:T:H`, which are a four-dimensional rotor space
   (MU-RECURSION 52.5 item 7), read over one completed rotation: the motion
   applied to its order `ℓ` (`Rotor::order`), which closes one turn on its
   plane (`K = 1`). The modes are read by the machinery every other motion's
   modes are read by (`cga::motion_modes`), so the row is a lane vector of
   modes (MU-ECONOMY §1), never four numbers.

## 3. The nucleus's motion (ruled 2026-11-13)

The operator: "Z needs to be coupled to Q intrinsically, algebraically, and
geometrically." A nucleus is one coupled body, the nucleon dipole
(LAW-COUPLING v2, 78.181), read from the universe's stated `(Z, Q)`:
- **intrinsically:** its rest point, where the push balances the phase,
  `p ⊕ R = 0`, so `R = −p` (xylarium's E018: `Q*/Z = D(R)`);
- **algebraically:** the push `p = (Z − Q)/(Z + Q)`, a rate in `(−1, 1)`
  composed by the flow law;
- **geometrically:** the dilation `D(p) = Z/Q`, a boost on S, and the turn
  of its plane by `R`, on H.

Its motion is `D(p)` composed with the turn by `R`, and its row is that
motion's modes over one full rotational completion (`K = 1`). Each
universe's table is its own, read from its stated basket.

*Superseded reading (kept for the record):* §3 as first written offered a
row "by Z alone" (the dipole at the even split) or "from the stated basket".
The ruling takes the basket's `(Z, Q)` as one coupled dipole, which is
neither a table by Z alone nor two independent counts.

## 4. The build, once §3 is ruled

- The table: a row per Z, derived once per row (MU-STANDARDS 2.12) and
  never a stored table of values.
- Creation at the manifest: the nuclei per Z times their rows, sealed as an
  act citing the manifest, replacing `GrantRefusal::Short` for founding.
- Distribution by alignment: the domain's sitting reserve receives the
  creation scaled by its alignment share.
- μ carried with nuclei across the tube, and the connection tax on
  dissipation.

**Live check.** On a fresh mesh, netstratum's manifest is granted μ in
proportion to its alignment, the union holds it, and the element account and
μ read matched across a crossing.
