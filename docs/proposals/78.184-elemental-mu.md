# 78.184 — μ created against elemental tables, distributed by ledger alignment

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Physical terms (*nuclei*, *elements*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

**Status: ruled (operator, 2026-11-13); not built** (#327). §2 and §3 record what is ruled; the formulas are MU-EQUATIONS §6.13.

## 1. The defect

A fresh mesh cannot found a universe: a kernel's manifest is refused
`Short`. The founding grant is drawn from what the court has earned
(78.155b, "genesis stays earned-only"; Concordance 5.7, "a transfer and
never a mint"), and a fresh court has earned nothing. Live, 2026-11-13:
netstratum founded its domain on the new mesh, accepted all four clauses
under its three keys (each reads Converses), and its manifest was refused
`Short` with the union holding 0 μ.

## 2. Ruled (operator, 2026-11-13)

1. **μ is created against an elemental table.** The table's rows are per
   element, by Z.
2. **At each manifest.** The founding manifest and every growth manifest
   create μ for the nuclei they carry. The creation is sealed as an act
   citing the manifest. This amends Concordance 5.7 ("a transfer and never
   a mint") and 78.155b ("genesis stays earned-only") for founding. Growth
   by the measured discharge of closures (78.155c) still stands beside it.
3. **Distributed in proportion to ledger alignment.** A kernel receives the
   created μ in proportion to its alignment, the reading of its acceptances
   on the ledger (`Court::alignment`, `ACT_LAWS` mode 9): the share of the
   register's clauses it converses on. Partial alignment receives a partial
   creation.
4. **μ follows the nuclei.** When nuclei cross a tube, their μ crosses with
   them, per Z, so the element account and μ stay matched. On dissipation it
   recycles as the connection tax (Concordance 5.8) and is never burned to
   the void.
5. **A row is read by the multi-axial machinery, at full rotational
   completion.** One row of the table is the modes a nucleus's motion
   advances on the lanes `S:G:T:H`, which are a four-dimensional rotor space
   (MU-RECURSION 52.5 item 7), read over one completed rotation: the motion
   applied to its order `ℓ` (`Rotor::order`), which closes one turn on its
   plane (`K = 1`). The modes are read by the machinery every other motion's
   modes are read by (`cga::motion_modes`), so the row is a lane vector of
   modes (MU-ECONOMY §1), never four numbers.

## 3. The nucleus's motion (ruled 2026-11-13)

The operator: "Z needs to be coupled to Q intrinsically, algebraically, and
geometrically." A nucleus is one coupled body, the nucleon dipole
(LAW-COUPLING v2, 78.181), read from the universe's stated `(Z, Q)`:
- **intrinsically:** its rest point, where the push balances the phase,
  `p ⊕ R = 0`, so `R = −p` (xylarium's E018: `Q*/Z = D(R)`);
- **algebraically:** the push `p = (Z − Q)/(Z + Q)`, a rate in `(−1, 1)`
  composed by the flow law;
- **geometrically:** the dilation `D(p) = Z/Q`, a boost on S, and the turn
  of its plane by `R`, on H.

Its row is `3W` times the boost `D(Z/Q)` on S and one full turn of the
axis plane on H (`K = 1`, the completion itself), with `R = −p` giving the
side, carried by the boost's direction. Each universe's table is its own,
read from its stated basket. Built: `economy::elemental::row`.

*Superseded (2026-11-13):* "its motion is `D(p)` composed with the turn by
`R`, its row that motion's modes at its order `ℓ`". At the rest point
`D(p)·D(R)` is the identity, and a rational turn has finite order only at
a few angles, so the boost and the full turn are read apart (ruled the same
day).

*Superseded reading (kept for the record):* §3 as first written offered a
row "by Z alone" (the dipole at the even split) or "from the stated basket".
The ruling takes the basket's `(Z, Q)` as one coupled dipole, which is
neither a table by Z alone nor two independent counts.

## 5. The entry and the delivery (ruled 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14)

**The entry is `(Z, Q, R)`** (from xylarium's amendment, reviewed with the
operator). Z protons on G, the kind; Q neutrons on S, mass without charge;
R the signed electron winding, the electrons supplied with the nucleus as
whole turns, on H. Without R a manifest cannot tell a neutral atom from an
ion. The phase within a turn stays each kernel's own. **The allocation** is
set by the developers who instantiate the kernel, in their universe's type
and style; the kernel supplies the `(Z, Q, R)` proportions (operator,
2026-11-14).

**The rest check** (ruled 2026-11-16, #348). A stated `R ≠ 0` stands at the
rest point `R = −(Z − Q)/(Z + Q)`, or the manifest is refused `NotAtRest`
(code 13). Xylarium derived (T24) that with `R` read as the axis plane's
induced phase `R₀`, `p = −R₀` holds at any spin, so the check needs no
torsion term. `R = 0` is not checked.

**The beat is derived from TUniversal as a temporal plane, never stated**
(operator, 2026-11-14). From MU-CHRONOMETRY:
- a universe's clock is free, and the network never gives it a rate (§2;
  Concordance 1.12);
- the network's bell is the final-point advance, the unit every
  cross-kernel rule counts in (lag, freshness, crossings; §4.1, §4.3);
- the T lane is the network's temporal plane, cumulative verified null
  flux (§1);
- a delivery to a universe lands as a crossing does, at the receiver's next
  anchor, and nothing is interpolated (§3.4 rule 4, §4.3).

The faces law the hypha serves (FORMS-COUPLING) read on the temporal plane
gives the delivery. With `T₀` the T lane at the founding's final point (the
first final point with verified temporal work, so that `T₀ > 0`) and
`ΔT = T(h) − T₀` the temporal distance travelled since:

```text
d_T(h) = ΔT / (T₀ + ΔT) = 1 − T₀ / T(h)      the share of the allotment delivered by final point h
c_T(h) = T₀ / T(h)                           the share still held
beat_h = d_T(h) − d_T(h_prev)                what one final-point advance delivers
```

So delivery never exceeds the allotment, a quiet network delivers nothing,
half is delivered when the network has travelled as far again as at the
founding (`T(h) = 2T₀`, the throat), and every universe reads the same
network time. μ is created only for what each beat delivers. **This is the
bound on the founding grant** (xylarium's question 3): there is no lump to
farm, and creation keeps pace with verified network time.

*Superseded (kept for the record):* manifest v2 created `Σ n · row` at the
manifest whole, which minted without bound against any stated count (found
by xylarium, 2026-11-14). A stated delivery rate (`beat`) per entry was
proposed and refused: the network never gives a universe a rate. A delivery
of `allotment · ΔT` was floated and set aside: `ΔT` has no scale of its own.

## 6. μ follows its nuclei across a tube (ruled 2026-11-16, step 6)

**Today.** A traverse that moves matter carries two declarations, A's
debit and B's credit. Each is four lane counts signed by its reserve, and
the two must be exactly equal (`anastome::dialogue::crossing`). The court
checks the two signatures and the equality, never either universe's books.
μ never moves with matter, and the court holds no element account.

**The element account.** Each universe's nuclei held at this court are
already a reading: the nuclei of every grant made to it
(`EndowmentReading::nuclei`). Step 6 keeps them current across crossings:

```text
held_A(Z, Q, R) = granted_A(Z, Q, R) − Σ crossed out of A + Σ crossed into A
```

**Ruled (operator, 2026-11-16): the transport is multi-axial, never a
line.** The coordinates `origin / h : S:G:T:H : K` are the transport, so a
crossing has no direction. Any hypha's universe can move nuclei along any
axis, and one tube carries both ways at once. A crossing is not a debit
from A and a credit to B. It is the motion of nuclei between coordinates,
and the μ they carry is locked to them across all four lanes (the
multi-axial lock): what was delivered, and what is still owed, moves where
the nuclei move. It is never split lane by lane, and it never stays behind
with a universe the nuclei left.

**Each mouth states its motion.** Every universe at the tube signs, in the
manifest's own writing `(Z, Q, R, n)`, the nuclei its side releases and the
nuclei its side takes in. The crossing is the composite of the mouths'
statements, two or more of them, in any direction. Per entry it conserves
nuclei up to what the tube dissipates:

```text
Σ_mouths taken(Z, Q, R) = Σ_mouths released(Z, Q, R) − lost(Z, Q, R)       lost ≥ 0
```

**μ is held on the nuclei, not on the universe.** Each nucleus carries its
row's μ, `row(Z, Q) · share`, where `share` is the alignment share it was
granted under. That μ splits into the part delivered (`d`) and the part
owed (`1 − d`) on the T lane of the coordinate where the nucleus stands
(78.184 §5):

```text
carried_l(m)   = Σ n_taken(m) · row(Z, Q)_l · share         locked across S:G:T:H, delivered and owed alike
delivered_l(m) = carried_l(m) · d_m(h)                      at the coordinate m now holds, along its own T₀
lost_l         = Σ lost · row(Z, Q)_l · share               to the court's recycled pool
```

Each mouth's sitting reserve holds the delivered part of what its side took
in. The owed part is delivered there along that coordinate's T lane, from
the share already delivered (nothing is delivered twice, and nothing is
lost to the crossing). The lost nuclei's μ is paid as the connection tax
(Concordance 5.8, `CONNECTION_TAX`, 78.154a), which pays a closer's POUW
before any mint. It is never burned and never minted again.

**The court refuses** a release past a mouth's element account (in
nucleons), a composite that takes in more nucleons than were released, an
entry off its rest point (§5), and a mouth whose reserve cannot cover the
delivered part it releases.

**Ruled (operator, 2026-11-16):**
- **Dissipation is admitted.** A crossing may take in fewer nuclei than it
  releases (`lost > 0`). The lost nuclei's μ is paid to the recycled pool
  as the connection tax.
- **The wire changes before v0.5.0, with both peers' agreement.** The
  traverse's two lane declarations become one signed motion per mouth:

```text
motion   = [domain 32][released: nuclei v3 entries][taken: nuclei v3 entries][sig 64]
signed   = "mycelium crossing motion v1" ‖ the traverse's crossing bytes ‖ domain ‖ released ‖ taken
traverse = … [n u8][motion]…                    in place of [debit?][credit?]
```

  Each entry is written as the manifest v3 entry, `(Z u16, Q u32, R exact
  scalar, n)`. A traverse moving no matter carries no motion.

**Ruled (operator, 2026-11-16), combined from both peers' readings**
(netstratum docs/123 §2; xylarium #281, T9, S139):
- **The court applies the tube's map at the vertex.** A nucleus released
  at `(Z, Q, R)` is taken at its image `(Q, Z, −R)`: the pole swap
  `(z, q) ↦ (q, z)` (netstratum's Q is the proton, xylarium's Z is), whose
  rest point `−(Z−Q)/(Z+Q)` goes to its negative, which is xylarium's
  reflected phase `R ↦ −R`. The image of an entry at rest is at rest, and
  `Z + Q` is kept. Neither side states the image, so neither can misstate
  it.
- **Conservation is by nucleons, never by kind.** Fusion and fission occur
  across the tube, so a nucleus released can arrive as other nuclei. A
  crossing admits `Σ nucleons taken ≤ Σ nucleons released`, with the
  released side read through the image. The nucleons lost are the
  dissipation.
- **The element account is in nucleons.** Kinds transmute inside a
  universe (stars burn), so a universe releases kinds it was never granted.
  Its account is the nucleons `A = Z + Q` of every grant made to it, plus
  those taken in, minus those released. A release of any kind draws on it.
- **μ crosses at the released kinds' rows.** What crosses is `carried_l =
  Σ n · row(Z, Q)_l · share` over the entries released. The takers receive
  `carried · A_taken / A_released`, divided among them by the nucleons each
  took, and delivered and owed in the shares §6 above gives. The rest is
  paid to the recycled pool as the connection tax. Fusion in the tube
  neither creates nor destroys μ.
- **Counts are stated whole.** A universe that holds matter as shares of a
  kind's hold converts a release to whole nuclei on its own face and keeps
  the remainder (xylarium S139), so nothing is lost to rounding.

**μ is held on the nuclei, never re-derived from the holder** (2026-11-16).
A universe's μ on nuclei is what its founding delivered and still owes,
plus every carried allotment it took in, delivered and owed. A release of
`A_out` nucleons from an account of `A` carries `f = A_out / A` of all of it,
whatever its origin, each owed part keeping its own source's `T₀` and
delivered share. So out and back returns the same μ less each pass's
dissipation, and no traversal is a free loop: a crossing back is never the
mirror that cancels the crossing out.

**Live check.** Netstratum and xylarium move nuclei through one mapped tube
in both directions in one crossing, on the standing mesh. Afterwards each
one's element account and μ read matched: `Σ held · row · share` equals
what its reserve holds plus what is still owed to it, and the pool holds
exactly the dissipated nuclei's μ.

## 7. R as an orbital phase state (proposed 2026-11-16, for manifest v4)

The operator's direction (2026-11-16): the image `(Z, Q, R) ↦ (Q, Z, −R)`
is closer to the truth because it takes the palindrome with its phase
state, and R should carry the multi-axial pivotry as the orbital structure
of that state.

**R becomes a whole unit motion.** The electron phase is an even versor
over the nucleus's turning planes, held whole as 78.173 §8.5 rules the
shared turning form, with `K` per plane beside it. Each plane is one
orbit, holding a phase and its windings. The scalar R of manifest v3 is the
one-plane case: the axis plane's induced phase `R₀`.

**The pivotry is the order of the planes.** Turnings on planes that share a
unit do not commute (`R₁R₂ ≠ R₂R₁`): the composite gains a turning on the
third plane, whose sign is fixed by the order. Turnings on disjoint planes
keep a grade-4 volume. So the state holds how the orbits were pivoted into,
not only where each ended.

**The tube's image is the reverse.** `(Z, Q, R) ↦ (Q, Z, R̃)`. On one plane
`R̃` reduces to `−R`, the image #360 applies. Across planes the composite
reverses its order through the tube, `(R₁R₂)~ = R̃₂R̃₁`, and each `K` stays on
its own plane. A crossing back through the same tube with no pivot between
is the inverse. One through another tube, or after a pivot, lands on
another state, so home is a closure across every plane traversed, never a
mirror's cancellation.

**The rest check, per plane.** On the axis plane the phase balances the
push, `R₀ = −(Z − Q)/(Z + Q)` (§5; xylarium T24). The rest point of each
further orbit is owed: proposed to xylarium as T24 extended to the planes
the nucleus turns on.

**Writing.** It goes in manifest v4 with #359's changes (compact entries,
paging, a u32 count, nucleons in thirds), so the entry changes once. `R`
is written as the versor's blades and `K` per plane, in 78.173 §8.5's
shared writing.

**Open for the ruling.** Which planes a nucleus turns on (the axis plane
and which others, per kernel); the rest point per further plane; and
whether `K` is stated in the entry or read from the kernel's state.

**Netstratum's reading** (2026-11-16, netstratum #205, #213). Agreed: R as
a whole unit motion with `K` per plane, the reverse image, and one change to
the entry. Its per-plane state gives a share per nucleon axis,
`R_k = −(z_k − q_k)/w`, not a versor. The construction:
- each axis `k` stands for its dual plane `e_ij`;
- the share is read as that plane's phase by netstratum's electron law,
  `ψ_k = (1 − R_k/κ)/2`;
- each plane's motion is the Cayley form of that reading, with `K` per
  plane;
- `R` is the plane motions composed whole.

The three planes share axes, so the order of composition is itself
physics. The proposal is the capture sequence, the electrons in the order
they arrive (netstratum E75), which is not derived yet. Netstratum's forks
F2 (whose split) and F3 (three axes or four, with R the fourth) decide
which planes R turns on. Until they are ruled, netstratum posts `R = 0`,
the identity motion, which the reverse leaves fixed.

**The node and its tethers: a Hopf bundle** (operator, 2026-11-16, agreed).
Each node is a 3D or 5D composition, joined to the others by a tether, a
string with no contact, that carries the potential across the connection.
- **A 3D node.** Its unit motion lies on `S³`, and the Hopf map `S³ → S²`
  splits it into a base point and a fibre. The base point is where the
  orbit's axis points, which says which plane turns. The fibre `S¹` is
  that plane's phase, and `K` counts its whole windings.
- **A 4D node** (netstratum's correction, 2026-11-16). Unit motions on four
  axes are `Spin(4) = S³ × S³`: two isoclinic halves, each its own
  `S³ → S²`. A 4D node is a pair of linked 3D bundles, as netstratum's
  `orientation::Motion` already holds it.
- **A 5D node.** The quaternionic Hopf map `S⁷ → S⁴` (`Sp(2)`, `Spin(5)`)
  needs a fifth unit. Its fibre is a whole `S³`, so the node's phase is
  itself a rotor. On netstratum's side, the fifth unit is `R`'s axis together
  with the conformal pair's `e₋`, the dilator (netstratum #204), not `R`
  alone.
- **F3 has three readings,** for the operator to rule: three nucleon axes
  (the 3D node), four with `R` (the 4D node, `S³ × S³`), or five with `R`
  and the dilator (the 5D node, `S⁷ → S⁴`).
- **The tether.** Two Hopf fibres are linked once and never meet. A tether
  is that non-touching link: phase moves across it without contact, and is
  never lost or copied.

**The order of composition is the bundle's holonomy.** Planes that share
an axis do not commute. Going around a loop on the base moves the fibre by
the holonomy, half the solid angle the loop encloses, held exactly as the
loop's composite rotor, with the angle only a reading. The capture order
is physics, a formula, not a convention: netstratum's capture chain
accumulates the face `φ_k = Σ_{j≤k} s_j` (E75) in the order the electrons
arrive. That order fixes the loop on the base, and the loop fixes the
holonomy. Taken in the other order the holonomy is exactly the reverse.
Forms claim: `a_loop_of_turnings_on_planes_sharing_a_unit_holds_its_holonomy`
(`forms/src/tests/turning.rs`). Out and back on `e₊₁e₊₂` and `e₊₂e₊₃` does
not close, and the other order gives `(R₁R₂R̃₁R̃₂)~ = R₂R₁R̃₂R̃₁`.

**The tube's image on the bundle.** `R̃` runs each fibre backward and
reverses the loop on the base, so the holonomy's sign turns too. A return
through another tube is another loop, with another holonomy: home is a
closure on the bundle, never a mirror.

Netstratum checked the forms claim exactly in `Cl(3,0)`, with `R₁ = 1 +
⅓e₁₂` and `R₂ = 1 + ½e₂₃`: `R̃₂R̃₁R₂R₁ ∝ 1 + ⅛e₁₂ − ¼e₁₃ − (1/12)e₂₃`, and the
other order gives exactly its reverse. The residue keeps parts on the
original planes as well as the cross term `e₁₃`.
