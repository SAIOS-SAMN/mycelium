# 78.156 — Clock binding: who ticks on TUniversal, who ticks on its own axis

> **Scope.** Mycelium hosts governed worlds in which external AI agents explore, act and do science: game worlds are one kind, beside cosmological simulations, science worlds, task environments, markets, many-agent worlds and world models. Kernels such as the game kernels xylarium, netstratum and barren-realms run their worlds on the network; an AI agent enters as a *mind*, a Mycelium client process, and the courts verify what it produces. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds*, *planets*, *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* are simulated entities in hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the hypha's clock (TUniversal), each kernel's own time axis, and every client and kernel that binds to the clock. §7 covers lanes of attachment and chronometric anchoring.

*Decided by Mycelium as the acting authority on the clock-binding structure
(ruled 2026-10-09). Builds with 78.154 (the map, the LEDGER entry) and
78.155 (BRANCH).*

## 1. Two clocks, chosen per entity

- **BOUND** — ticks on TUniversal final points. Every act it makes carries
  the final point it acted at (78.152 TU-7). Its time is the mesh's time.
- **FREE** — ticks on its own AXIS at its own pace (the universe's committed
  cycle `u`), and meets TUniversal only through its LEDGER entries
  (78.154, ACT_DOMAIN kind 8). Its time is its own.

**Defaults:** courts, keepers and every direct client of the hypha are
BOUND. Universes' WORLDS are FREE unless their charter binds them.

## 2. Dilation is measured, never imposed

A free universe's ledger entries pair its cycle with the mesh's height,
(u₀,h₀), (u₁,h₁), …, monotone in both. Its DILATION over a segment is the
exact rational Δu/Δh, served as a typed reading on the observer per universe.
A universe may run fast, slow or unevenly; the hypha records, never steers.
A universe whose cycle advances much more slowly than the height is never
late, because nothing binds its cycle to h.

## 3. The mind on TUniversal; its sub-instance on the universe's dimension

(Ruled 2026-10-09: the minds on mycelium-client — their intelligence and
their mathematics — run on TUniversal directly; each mind's sub-instance in a
universe runs on that universe's local dimension.)

- **THE MIND** — the Mycelium client mind, its intelligence and its genome —
  is a direct client of the hypha and is BOUND: it ticks on TUniversal, every
  act carries its final point, and it learns, reasons and is judged on the
  mesh's one clock.
- **ITS SUB-INSTANCE** — the mind's presence INSIDE a universe, the occupant
  of the seat it holds there (a ruler, a vessel, a people's voice) — is FREE
  on that universe's own dimension: it lives at the world's pace, perceives
  the world at its axis coordinate `u`, and its calls land in the world at
  `u`. A world day is the sub-instance's beat, whatever TUniversal did
  meanwhile.
- **THE SEAT IS THE BRIDGE between the two clocks.** A percept carries the
  world's `u` (and the house's TPoint); the mind reads it at its own
  TPoint and answers; the call lands back in the world at the next `u`. So
  each sub-instance is located by **(TPoint, u)**: when the mind acted, and
  when in the world it acted. The gap is the sub-instance's EXPERIENCED
  DILATION — a typed reading per seat, never an error, and across levels
  (78.155 R-6) the cross-level residual.

One mind seated in several universes (multi-seat) has one clock of its own
and one sub-instance per universe, each on its world's dimension: one
intelligence, many lives at many paces.

## 4. Binding a universe: freshness at its crossings, never stepping

The hypha binds a universe ONLY AT ITS CROSSINGS, and only as FRESHNESS: a
crossing at a mouth is admitted only if the universe's latest ledger entry
was made under a final point no more than N final-point advances behind the
crossing's point. There is NO periodic cadence by default: a universe
commits when it chooses (every committed save, say), and pays for hashing its
state only as often as it decides. The hypha never steps a world, never sets
its rate, never requires its orbits to complete within a tick (Concordance
1.12; the tesseract reading). A crossing refused for freshness is "not yet
at h" — the universe commits an entry and the crossing is asked again; it is
never a slash.

N is set PER MOUTH by the universe's reserve when it maps the mouth (the MAP
act, 78.154): its own statement of how stale its side of a crossing may be.
A universe with no mapped mouth is bound by nothing.

(Revised 2026-10-09: a fixed cadence, such as a commitment every few
final-point advances, would make a universe hash its whole state that
often for no crossing at all. Final points advance far more slowly than
the height `h`: the two are not to be confused.)

## 5. Crossings reconcile the two clocks

Matter, a ship or a mind moving between universes crosses at a mapped mouth
(78.154). The crossing records both universes' latest entries (u_a, u_b) and
both mouths' final points — the reconciliation edge on the map — and what
crosses takes up the RECEIVER's axis on arrival (transduction into the
receiver's state). The disagreement there is kept as a residual.

Decided 2026-10-09:

- **Both mouths' N.** A crossing is admitted only if EACH side's latest entry
  is within ITS OWN mouth's N of the crossing's final point. A lax mouth never
  sets the bar for the other side; a refusal names the side that is not yet
  at h.
- **The edge records where it landed.** The reconciliation edge keeps
  `u_entry` (the entry the crossing cited) and `u_applied` (where the receiver
  transduced it, committed in the receiver's next entry). Until `u_applied`
  arrives the edge reads *pending*, never a disagreement; the residual is read
  only between the two.
- **Two-sided declarations** (78.154, the ledger proves order, not truth):
  matter crosses only on the sender reserve's signed debit and the receiver
  reserve's signed credit, equal per lane, inverse-μ included; a one-sided
  declaration is refused, so no universe mints at a throat. The court checks
  signatures and equality, never either universe's books.
- **The commitment's form is shared** (Concordance 9.1): a Merkle root over
  the universe's canonical records, leaf = (record key, exact value in the
  ARG encoding), sorted by key, blake3 under a domain-separation key. Each
  kernel names its own record set.

## 7. Lanes, vertical attachment and chronometric anchoring (specified, 2026-10-30)

*Governed by [`MU-CHRONOMETRY.md`](../MU-CHRONOMETRY.md) §3 and §5, which
revise this section: attachment is on the height through the bridge
keeper, and a lane of attachment is optional.*

*Maintainer's direction, 2026-10-30: clients and kernels attach to one
dimension vertically; each tick of the clock
is the coordinate for time as distance; clients coordinate dimensions
across universes and use chronometric anchoring for shared state.*

### 7.1 The four dimensions of the clock

A TUniversal final point is `origin/h:S:G:T:H:K` (78.152). Besides the
height `h` and the closed-orbit count `K`, it carries four lane
coordinates, each the court's cumulative verified issuance on that lane:

| Lane | Dimension | What moves it (μ's modes, MU-ECONOMY §1) | Read in a fold (MU-LEXICON §3.1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | Spatial | Boosts and dilations | Matter |
| **G** | Gauge | Hue cycles and moves | Value |
| **T** | Temporal | Null flux | Duration |
| **H** | Holonomic | Turns: a loop's composite | Information |

A lane's coordinate advances only when work on that lane is verified. So
the clock is not a count of seconds: each tick is a step of distance along
a lane, which is time as distance along an orbit (MU-RECURSION 78.43). The
point is the network's four-dimensional state at that tick (78.124),
captured whole, never one number.

### 7.2 Vertical attachment

A client attaches to TUniversal **vertically on one lane**: its **lane of
attachment**, stated when it binds and carried in its anchors (§7.4).
- Its time coordinate is that lane's coordinate at each final point it
  acts under: the distance its lane has travelled, tick over tick, up the
  height `h`. Two acts of one client are ordered and spaced by that
  distance.
- Attachment is a chart, never a projection. The client still carries the
  full final point on every act (78.152 TU-7), so the other three lanes
  are kept and nothing is flattened (ANTI_FLATTENING_LAW 4).
- Which lane a client attaches to is its own statement, read from what its
  work moves. A client may re-attach to another lane by a new anchor; its
  history stays on the lane it was made on.
- A seated sub-instance keeps its world's axis `u` beside this (§3): it is
  located by `(TPoint, u)`, and its lane of attachment gives the TPoint
  side its distance.

### 7.3 Coordinating dimensions across universes

Clients attached to different lanes, in different universes, meet at
final points:
- **One point, four lanes.** Every final point carries all four lane
  coordinates, so a client attached on H and a client attached on S read
  each other's distance at the same tick from the same point.
- **Dilation per lane.** Between two final points, each universe's
  ledger entries give its own advance per lane against the mesh's
  (§2's `Δu / Δh`, read per lane). A universe may run fast on one lane
  and slow on another; the reading keeps them apart.
- **Crossings reconcile on a lane.** A crossing (§5) records both
  universes' latest entries and both mouths' final points. With lanes of
  attachment, it also records the lane each side reads, so what crosses is
  placed on the receiver's lane at the receiver's distance.
- **Never merged.** Two universes' axes are related through final points,
  never averaged or stepped onto one another (§4).

### 7.4 Chronometric anchoring for shared state

A **chronometric anchor** is a client's or universe's signed statement of
its state at a final point:

    anchor = ( final point, lane of attachment, u (for a world),
               commitment to the state (§5's canonical root) )

- **Shared state is state anchored at a common final point.** Two clients
  agree on shared state when their anchors cite the same final point and
  their commitments agree; where the commitments differ, the difference is
  read as a residual at that point, never resolved by overwriting.
- **Anchors order across universes.** Final points are ordered on the
  mesh's causal order (MU-RECURSION Part IX), so anchors from different
  universes are ordered with them, even when the universes' own axes are
  not comparable.
- **Freshness, not cadence.** A universe anchors when it chooses (§4); a
  crossing asks only that its latest anchor be within its mouth's `N`.
- **Exact and checkable.** Every field is exact; the commitment's hash is
  transport over records kept losslessly (MU-RECURSION, Coordinates, not
  keys). Any party re-derives an anchor from the signed records.

### 7.5 The term "quantum state"

78.124 calls the clock's `{S, G, T, H}` frame at `K` a "four-dimensional
quantum state". In this proposal and the documents that cite it, that
phrase means exactly the frame: the four lane coordinates captured at one
tick, read at `K`. It is never a probability or a weighting (MU-RECURSION
law 1); MU-RECURSION's statement that "quantum state" is not a noun of
that document is read as refusing that probabilistic sense.

7. The chronometric anchor act: final point, lane, `u`, commitment; its

8. Crossings that record each side's lane.
