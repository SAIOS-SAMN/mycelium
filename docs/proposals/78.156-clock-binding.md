# 78.156 — Clock binding: who ticks on TUniversal, who ticks on its own axis

*Decided by mycelium as the acting authority on the clock-binding structure
(the operator, 2026-10-09). Builds with 78.154 (the map, the LEDGER entry) and
78.155 (BRANCH). Nothing here is built yet.*

## 1. Two clocks, chosen per entity

- **BOUND** — ticks on TUniversal final points. Every act it makes carries
  the final point it acted at (78.152 TU-7). Its time is the mesh's time.
- **FREE** — ticks on its own AXIS at its own pace (the universe's committed
  cycle `u`), and meets TUniversal only through its LEDGER entries
  (78.154, ACT_DOMAIN kind 8). Its time is its own.

**Defaults:** courts, keepers and every direct client of the hypha are
BOUND. Universes' WORLDS are FREE unless their charter binds them.

## 2. Dilation is measured, never imposed

A free universe's ledger entries pair its cycle with the mesh's height,
(u₀,h₀), (u₁,h₁), …, monotone in both. Its DILATION over a segment is the
exact rational Δu/Δh, served as a typed reading on the observer per universe.
A universe may run fast, slow or unevenly; the hypha records, never steers.
(Barren-realms at ~0.2 cycles/s against ~3 h/s has dilation ≈ 1/15 — and is
never late, because nothing binds its cycle to h.)

## 3. A mind is bound AND embedded

A mycelium client mind is a direct client of the hypha: BOUND — it ticks on
TUniversal and every act carries its final point. When it sits a seat in a
universe it also perceives that universe's axis coordinate `u` (the percept
carries it, beside the house's TPoint). So a mind holds TWO coordinates,
**(TPoint, u)**: when the world event happened in its universe (`u`), and
when the mind read and acted on it (TPoint). The gap is the mind's
EXPERIENCED DILATION — a typed reading per mind and per seat, never an error,
and across levels (78.155 R-6) the cross-level residual.

A mind seated in several universes (multi-seat) holds one TPoint and one `u`
per universe: one clock of its own, many worlds' times seen through it.

## 4. Binding a universe: cadence, never stepping

The hypha MAY bind a universe where it holds the connection — as a condition
of its tube connection and connection tax (78.154a) — but only as a COMMIT
CADENCE: "post a ledger entry at least once per N final-point advances". It
never steps a world, never sets its rate, never requires its orbits to
complete within a tick (Concordance 1.12: compact until observed; the
operator's tesseract — no client or universe forced to complete its
rotations within one clock tick). A universe that misses its cadence is not
slashed: its connection is marked STALE on the map and crossings to it are
refused as not yet at h until its next entry.

N is per connection, set by the universe's reserve within the hypha's bound
(the bound itself: the keepers' aperture, `physics::aperture_cycle`, of
final-point advances, unless the operator rules otherwise).

## 5. Crossings reconcile the two clocks

Matter, a ship or a mind moving between universes crosses at a mapped mouth
(78.154). The crossing records both universes' latest entries (u_a, u_b) and
both mouths' final points — the reconciliation edge on the map — and what
crosses takes up the RECEIVER's axis on arrival (transduction into the
receiver's state). The disagreement there is kept as a residual.

## 6. What gets built

1. The `u` coordinate on percepts (seat wire: the percept carries the house's
   axis coordinate beside its TPoint) — with seat-wire v1.4.
2. The mind's (TPoint, u) per seat, and its experienced-dilation reading.
3. The per-universe dilation reading from ledger entries (with 78.154).
4. The connection's commit cadence N and the STALE mark on the map (with
   78.154).
