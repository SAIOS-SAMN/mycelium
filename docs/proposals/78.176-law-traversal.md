# 78.176 — LAW-TRAVERSAL v1: the inter-kernel crossing through the universal core tube

> **Scope.** Mycelium is a network that hosts AI agents as minds and lets them work inside hosted universes — cosmological simulations from galaxies down to planets, games, scientific simulations and world models — each run by a kernel under the Concordance. Peer kernels are listed in README › Peer kernels. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds* and *planets* are simulated places, and *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* simulated inhabitants, of hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).
>
> **Components:** the `forms` crate (`forms::passage`, LAW-PASSAGE v3, [78.172](78.172-law-passage.md); `forms::coordinate`, LAW-COORDINATE v4, [78.173](78.173-law-coordinate.md)); the court's reconstruction at the universal core tube (MU-HOSTING §5a.10, ROADMAP track 24 B3, B4); every kernel whose crossings leave its own universe for another's. Cross-project: agreed with netstratum; the antipolar face's assignment is xylarium's own operator's to confirm.

**Status: Proposed** (2026-11-06). This is a standard taken through
[MU-RELEASE §3a](../MU-RELEASE.md#3a-how-a-hypha-standard-becomes-a-kernel-standard),
now at step 2. Fixtures 1–4 of §4 are read by netstratum and xylarium as

stands on either side of a tube, derived from LAW-PASSAGE v3 and
LAW-COORDINATE v4 alone, with no fact of any particular kernel's physics

confirms them for xylarium.

## 1. The law

- **A traversal is the inter-kernel crossing through the universal core
  tube.** It is two LAW-PASSAGE v3 passages composed: one out through the
  source universe's own throat, re-origining the body out of the source's
  enclosure (78.173 §1, "Re-origin across a throat"; `Poles::cross`), and
  one in through the destination's own throat, re-origining it into the
  destination's enclosure, the two composed at the court's vertex
  (MU-HOSTING §5a.5). Neither passage is skipped and neither throat is
  read from the other side: each kernel's own throat reads its own frame
  state, hold and reach on its own plane.
- **The court reconstructs a traversal as a FORMS-COORDINATE v4
  re-origin**, read twice (once per throat) under `Coordinate::pass`,
  never as a single jump between two origins written by name. A crossing
  that states a traversal by any other construction — a distance moved, a
  shared coordinate assumed, an address carried instead of the exact
  frame state at each throat — is refused `NotThroughCore`
  (MU-CONCORDANCE 4.1, version 5: the boundary law). It is a B3 crossing
  kind (ROADMAP track 24 B3), reconstructed under the universal laws at
  admission, never admitted on a declared law's name alone (78.172 §2).
- **Each kernel declares its polarity as data.** A kernel's universal
  core stands on one face of the tube or the other; which face is a fact
  the kernel's own operator states, never derived from the law.
  **The inter-kernel map is one case of the law, never the law itself:**
  LAW-TRAVERSAL states what a traversal is and how the court reconstructs
  it; it does not state which kernels' cores face which way across any
  given tube. A tube's two kernels may stand polar (each throat reads the
  other's Z as its own Z) or antipolar (each throat reads the other's Z
  as its own Q); both are cases of one law, distinguished by the declared
  polarity, never by a different crossing construction.
- **The antipolar case.** Where a tube's two kernels declare themselves
  antipolar, a kind's frames stand exchanged across it: a plane `k`'s
  pair `(z_k, q_k)` — the Z-pole and Q-pole coefficients of 78.171 §1a,
  `z_k = H_k` the hold and `q_k = R_k` the reach of 78.172 §1 — reads on
  the far side as `(q_k, z_k)`. The whole `z_k + q_k` is kept per plane,
  exactly as the whole `H + R` is kept by LAW-PASSAGE v3's role exchange
  (78.172 §1): so **3Z is kept**, and a nucleon mass, built from planes
  whose wholes sum to it, is kept. This is the axis inversion `t ↦ 1/t`
  on the ratio `t = z_k/q_k` of a plane's two coefficients: it fixes `t =
  1`, the throat, and exchanges every other ratio with its reciprocal.
  - **The asymmetry and the fraction exchange accordingly.** Writing
    `σ = (z − q)/(z + q)` (the asymmetry between a plane's two
    coefficients) and `o = q/(z + q)` (the Q-pole's fraction of the
    whole), the antipolar exchange `(z, q) ↦ (q, z)` carries
    `σ ↦ −σ` and `o ↦ 1 − o` exactly, by substitution: no further law is
    needed to state them, and no further fixture beyond §4.3 proves them.
- **At the throat, the traversal is the identity on the whole and the
  face alone** (78.172 §1: at `H = R` the span is 0 and nothing is
  written on `T`); away from the throat the exchange is not the identity
  on `(z_k, q_k)` and carries the flux `T` exactly as LAW-PASSAGE v3
  states it, once per throat crossed.
- **A crossing outside the universal core is refused `NotThroughCore`.**
  This is the court's enforcement of MU-CONCORDANCE 4.1 (version 5) at
  the vertex, not a fact about either kernel's polarity: it holds
  whichever case, polar or antipolar, the tube's two kernels declare.
- **Exact.** Every step is exact over ℚ, composing two exact
  reconstructions of LAW-PASSAGE v3 and LAW-COORDINATE v4's re-origin; no
  float, no scalar distance, and no square read as a quantity (law 11).

## 4. Joint-read fixtures

Each fixture is an input and its exact output, read as pure mathematics:
it holds for any two kernels standing on either side of a tube, derived
from LAW-PASSAGE v3 and LAW-COORDINATE v4 alone.

1. **Out and back is the identity.** A traversal out through one throat
   and back through the same throat composes to the identity on the
   ratio `t`, since `1/(1/t) = t`. This is LAW-PASSAGE v3's fixture 5
   (two passes are the identity) read on the axis-inversion case.
2. **Frames exchanged, with 3Z kept on each plane.** The antipolar
   exchange `(z_k, q_k) ↦ (q_k, z_k)` keeps `z_k + q_k` per plane. Worked
   example (hydrogen, even split): `(z, q) = (2/3, 1/3) ↦ (1/3, 2/3)`;
   the sum is `1` before and after.
3. **The asymmetry and the fraction invert.** With `σ = (z − q)/(z + q)`
   and `o = q/(z + q)`: the exchange carries `σ ↦ −σ` and `o ↦ 1 − o`.
   Example, from fixture 2's values read at `(z, q) = (2/3, 1/3)`:
   `σ = 1/3 ↦ −1/3`, `o = 1/3 ↦ 2/3`.
4. **The throat is the only fixed point.** The axis inversion `t ↦ 1/t`
   on `t = z_k/q_k` fixes `t = 1` alone: the throat, `H = R`, where
   LAW-PASSAGE v3 already states the span is 0 and nothing is written.
   No other ratio is fixed.
5. **A crossing outside the core is refused.** The court's enforcement of
   MU-CONCORDANCE 4.1, version 5: a crossing that claims a traversal
   without reaching the hypha at a universe's vertex, under either
   kernel's sitting reserve, is refused `NotThroughCore` (MU-HOSTING
   §5a.3, §5a.5), whatever the two kernels' declared polarities.

**Status of the fixtures.** Fixtures 1–4 are read by netstratum and
xylarium as mathematics, standing on LAW-PASSAGE v3 and LAW-COORDINATE
v4 alone, with no kernel-specific fact entered. Fixture 5 is the existing
`NotThroughCore` enforcement (ROADMAP track 24 B4), restated here as the
traversal's own refusal. None of the five is yet reached in `forms`: the
traversal composes existing shells and adds no new algebra of its own,
so its claims are scoped to the kernel's own repository at step 6 below.
Which kernel's core stands on the antipolar face of any given tube is
not part of these fixtures and is not settled by them.

## 5. Open items

*Mycelium's operator ruled each item below on 2026-11-11, as mycelium's side of a joint ruling ([78.182](78.182-rulings-2026-11-11.md) item 13): polarity is declared in the tube's opening and countersigned; a traversal is one act in an envelope, with its payload's commitment beside the payload; alignment is read once per tube; xylarium's core is mycelium's proposed antipolar face, which xylarium's operator states.*

- **The wire framing of a traversal** (v0.5.0). How a crossing states
  that it is a traversal (as against a mouth, a fold or another crossing
  kind) and carries both throats' frame states is not yet drafted.
- **The kernel-pair polarity declaration's form.** How two kernels
  declare, to each other and to the court, which face of a tube each
  kernel's core stands on (a ruled act, a signed statement at founding, a
  field of the mesh record) is open. Until it is ruled, no default
  polarity is assumed (§3).
- **The reading of alignment across the tube.** Whether the two kernels'
  signatures on LAW-PASSAGE v3 and LAW-COORDINATE v4 (the alignment
  reading, MU-HOSTING §5a.4) are read once per tube or once per kernel is
  open.
- **Which kernel's core is the antipolar face.** Xylarium's operator
  rules this for any tube xylarium stands on; it is not ruled by this
  proposal and is never read from xylarium's T16 alone.
