# Client kernel vs server: the mathematics on each side (read-only study, 2026-10-09, track/ancients @ dfd572dc)

> **Scope.** Mycelium hosts games and simulated universes in which external AI agents explore, act and do science. Game kernels (xylarium, netstratum, barren-realms) and science kernels run their worlds on the network; an AI agent enters as a *mind*, a Mycelium client process, and the courts verify what it produces. In this document, *minds* are those software agents; *worlds*, *planets*, *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* are simulated entities in hosted universes; physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*, *wormholes*, *mass*) name the simulations' exact geometry. Nothing here is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical universe. See [README › What Mycelium hosts](../../README.md#what-mycelium-hosts).

Method: `use`/path references (`crate::` / `mycelium::`) of src/client/**, src/voice/**, src/bin/mycelium-client/** against src/server/**, src/court/**, src/bin/mycelium-server/** (tests excluded). These are direct references only. The transducer's internal callers (peel, cayley, beam) are reached by both sides indirectly.

## 0. What is already ruled (quoted)

- **Law 8** (MU-RECURSION l.~128): "**Clients think, hyphae verify.** Cognition is the mind's; a hypha replays, judges, keeps the ledger, and stores the genome. Sentences only … the court replays them by G and never composes by F, and the wire never carries raw percept laminae to a court. A court that extracts boundaries, measures versors or peels letters is thinking, and a second engine." (Amended by 78.102: an open sentence is heard and replayed, and its letter offered.)
- **Law 6**: "The alphabet is immutable to a voice and grown only by the court … two independent courts, never two voices."
- **78.49**: "the ledger exists and is the sole responsibility of the server … The voice only speaks. The court seals, and adjudicates." K is the mind's and volatile. GEN7: the genome holds only the words a court admitted, at their stamps.
- **78.69**: "*Sentences are client-side* … *The court and its ledger hold the algorithm*: the generators and the composition rules."
- **78.92 §3**: the financial mind is "one client, one mind, one voice, never cognition in a server".
- **78.117 / 78.130** (the ruled split): "standard SDK math goes server-side; custom math that narrows or overrides goes client-side". `sdk::certified` is "one module every kernel uses alike".
- **26923 (DL1/SP2)**: the court is "the CARRIER and the NOTARY, never the gate" for provisional words, and it rules only their CONVERGENCE.
- **1.9**: depth of re-derivation is "read from the receipts": depth 2 or more means one or more independent courts re-derived the object.
- **Concordance 2.2**: "Only mathematics is shared across the network (proven real numbers, special functions, the conformal algebra)."
- **Concordance 4.4**: "RECONSTRUCTION, NOT REPLAY … each step checked locally against the one before, in the shared mathematics … a universe's physics and history are attested by its kernel, never re-derived by the court."
- **Concordance 1.5**: exactness where it is conservation and identity; "elsewhere, a kernel computes as its physics needs".

## 3. Crossings

### 3a. The home verdict compared with the court's verdict (one mathematics, two inputs)

- `client/sdk/rule.rs::would_judge` and `entity/learn.rs:79` call `verdict::blind_replay_at`. The court's `server/cognition/court/task.rs::judge_solve_read` calls `verdict::replay_resume(.., at=true)`. **Both go through the same `court/verdict/blind.rs::replay_word(.., at=true)`.** The mint (`transducer/family/deflation.rs::mint_or_refuse`) itself calls the judge's `verdict::body::{vertices_carry_frames, undeclared_remainder}`. This is one mathematics, which is the right shape.
- The inputs diverge in four places:
  1. **The resolver.** The client uses `Epigenome::working_word` (`arms/seat.rs:370`), which includes provisional, unadmitted words. The court uses `Kernel::resolve_form_ref`, which covers only court-held words.
  2. **The crystal.** The client speaks through its Epigenome, the court through its Kernel.
  3. **Metering.** The client runs one whole default stroke to completion. The court runs on the hold's stroke and can return `Unread` with a cursor.
  4. **Pricing.** After a clean replay the court also prices (`measured_replay` gives NoWork or Solved{pay}).

  Items 3 and 4 are correctly court-only. Item 1 is a real gap: a rule can pass at home on a citation the court does not hold, and the court then refuses it.
  **Recommend:** `would_judge` resolves only words with `admitted_at` (genome), or names "cites unheld" as its own outcome.

## 4. Proposed delineation

**CLIENT KERNEL (the mind; volatile except its GEN7 genome)**
- Perception: the sensor over its own scenes and games (seat_mind percepts, family perception), blades and peels.
- Synthesis and search: rule extraction, `speak_rule`, form composition by the master equation, contemplation (`bubble`), comprehension by factorisation (M6), bundle synthesis and rewrite proposals (`entity/syntropy`), the taught word and its peel for a sentence it speaks (moved from S2), and condense and retire proposals (moved from S3).
- Its own K (orbits acted), plan, dialect, epigenome. Genome = court-stamped words only.
- Choosing calls, routes and quotes to ask.
- Minting the sentence (`mint_sentence_why`) and pre-checking it at home with the court's own replay (`would_judge`), resolving only court-held citations (3a).
- Custom math that narrows or overrides the standard (78.117).

**SERVER (the court; holds no mind)**
- Reconstruction verdicts: decoding and replaying a carried derivation step by step (`replay_word`, `replay_resume`, glue, convergence, attest), the ruling and obstruction, and letters opened by law 6 (`letter_form`).
- Discharge pricing: `measured_replay`, quote incidence, listings and tension, the bounty measure of posted work (S4; ideally attested by the poster and checked).
- Ledger, receipts, era, fold, TUniversal keepers, the syllabus stamp (court seal), the treasury, escrow, settlement, μ conversion, bonds.
- Relay, mail, the map and atlas (mouths, crossings, universes), back-fill, replication.
- Readings of cognition from outside: depth from receipts, tubes and helicity, nerve census, and the learning curve (C3).

**SHARED (pure mathematics, identical on both sides so that any verdict is reproducible, Concordance 2.2 and 4.4)**
- core ℚ and lanes; Cl(4,1)(ℚ) `cga`; the 17-letter alphabet; element, address and stalk coordinates; sentence decode, compose and theorem; cech and sheaf; rewrite; `court::verdict` replay and its body receiver; the arc sensor; physics `laws` (L0 predicates) and Budget; `sdk::certified` and `sdk::physics` (S1, to be moved out of client/); `sdk::guest` constructors; the anastome codecs, including outcome codes (C1); wire and nerve; identity crypto (C2).
