### A ground's vortex collapses by a relation (track 26 S6 stage 5, #32)
- **The relations** (ruled 2026-11-06 on #32 (3)), read per candidate at
  any rank with no threshold (`transduce::collapse`): `Stands`, a body
  stands on the candidate on every train input; `PaintedOn`, every pair's
  residue changes only the candidate's sites.
- **The collapse.** A candidate collapses the vortex where one of the two
  holds for it on every pair and for no other candidate
  (`Collapse::Collapsed`); otherwise the candidates stay superposed.
  `Ground::of` reads the collapsed ground; `Ground::collapse` gives each
  candidate's readings. A closing rule never collapses the ground.
- Claims: `ground_vortex_collapses_where_bodies_stand_on_one_candidate`,
  `ground_vortex_collapses_where_residue_paints_one_candidate`,
  `closing_rule_never_collapses_the_ground`.
- The perceived ground of a family whose vortex collapses changes (the
  census and the oracle's golden reading move); no task address changes,
  since identity reads no ground.
