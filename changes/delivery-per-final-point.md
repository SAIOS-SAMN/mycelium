### A founding is delivered per final point along TUniversal's temporal plane (78.184 §5, #327 step 5)

A manifest's creation is now the grant's allotment, minted over time instead of whole at the manifest.
- **The read:** each final-point advance reads TUniversal's T lane.
- **The start:** the first point with `T > 0` fixes `T₀`.
- **The beat:** each grant then has `d_T = 1 − T₀/T(h)` of its allotment by point `h`, and each advance mints its beat to the domain's sitting reserve (`Court::deliver_at`).
- **The bound:** delivery never exceeds the allotment, half arrives at `T = 2T₀`, and a quiet network delivers nothing.

This bounds the founding grant: since #331 it had minted whole at the manifest, without limit against any stated count. The held grant carries its allotment, `T₀` and the share delivered, so the layout changes and the mesh is founded again.
