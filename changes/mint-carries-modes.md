### A mint carries its proof's modes (MU-ECONOMY §2.5; #169 step 3b)

A closure's discharge now holds the modes its motion moved (`Mu::carried_by`): each lane's ruled share is set on that lane's modes in proportion, exactly. A lane the motion does not reach stays at its founding mode.

The paths a discharge travels keep those modes:
- `Mu::capped` keeps the holding's own modes, scaling a lane past the cap.
- A mint past the recycled pool keeps the rest of the mint's modes.
- A claim's release pays the discharge capped by the escrow.
- POWV's remainder is drawn from the discharge.

The amounts are unchanged lane by lane.
