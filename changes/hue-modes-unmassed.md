### A recolour's discharge holds its hue modes (78.174 §1 item 9; #169)

A closure's hue-map modes were read only where a pair carried mass. A recolour moves no site, so it carries none, and it was paid on G with no mode: the live runs reported `modes no modes`.

Hue modes are now read on every closed pair, at the pair's G share, the share of the enclosure recoloured. A motion's modes are still read at the share its carried mass covers.

Claim: `recolour_discharge_holds_its_hue_modes`, which fails on the earlier reading.
