### Proposed: the body on the sample's manifold, with no unit cube and no two-coordinate site (78.190)

Proposal 78.190, for #420 and #421 (Plan 3, #429). It derives from 78.179 §1, MU-ANTI-FLATTENING 12 and the no-lattice rule:
- **A site** is a point of the sample's manifold: turns and windings on circle phases, places on intervals, at the sample's own rank.
- **A body** is a connected region of the manifold, with the seam glued on circles, held by its boundary.
- **Extent** is read in the manifold's own periods, never as the unit cube's `1/12`.
- **Identity** is by position on the manifold.

There are five stages, each with its claims and its live reading. S3–S5 are a recut and need the operator's approval of the ledger layout and the wire.

**Network contract:** none in this change, which adds documents only.
