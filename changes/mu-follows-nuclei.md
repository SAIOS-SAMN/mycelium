### μ follows its nuclei across a tube

78.184 §6, ruled 2026-11-16 with both peers. A traverse's two lane declarations, debit and credit, are replaced by one signed motion per universe at the tube: the nuclei it releases and the nuclei it takes in. The transport is multi-axial, so a crossing has no direction.

The court conserves nucleons, not kinds (`Σ A taken ≤ Σ A released`), and keeps each universe's element account in nucleons. The tube's image `(Z, Q, R) ↦ (Q, Z, −R)` is applied at the vertex. The μ held on the released nuclei moves with them: the delivered share reserve to reserve, the owed share as carried allotments delivered along the same T lane. The dissipated nucleons' μ goes to the recycled pool as the connection tax.

New refusals: `NotAtTube`, `NotConserved`, `MotionUnsigned`, `PastAccount`, `NotAtRest` and `Uncovered`. Gas G and H read the nucleons released and taken.
