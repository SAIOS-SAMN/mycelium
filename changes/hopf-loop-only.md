### Only a loop holds a holonomy

`Contemplation::hopf_return_holonomy` offers only the four-leg loop `R₁R₂R̃₁R̃₂` as a closure candidate. The two-tube relative motion `R̃₂R₁` of two open arcs is no return, and offering it as one closes every pair (#361, whose mind-side fallback #363 already removed). Commuting legs give the identity and no candidate (78.184 §7).
