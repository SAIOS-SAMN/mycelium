### A court read names its lane (78.183 P2, first sites)

`Budget::spend_on(lane)` spends one read of the token and counts it on the lane of the mode the read moves (S, G, T, H). The token's bound doesn't change; sizing a stroke per lane is P1b's job. Each court hold books its spend per lane on its site and logs `court hold <site> took N spent S:G:T:H [...] unnamed M`, where `unnamed` counts the reads at sites that don't name a lane yet. The first sites named are the walks along time, all on T: the receipt window, the ancestry walk and the ladder's pruning.
