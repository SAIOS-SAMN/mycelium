### How the Concordance rules the engine, and the mind–hypha interaction (docs)

- **Concordance §0.2** is the one description of how a clause becomes the engine's law:
  - a clause is a law form with joint-read fixtures;
  - it is accepted clause by clause, by reconstructing those fixtures;
  - every acceptance, veto or refusal is an event on the ledger, and the engine reads it as the alignment;
  - the law is applied at the universal core tube, in the reconstruction of what crosses, and in the L0 verifier;
  - kernels accept, and clients are bound by the Network clauses without signing.
- **MU-CLIENT §2.5** is the map of the mind–hypha interaction: the connection, the round trips, deferral and stall, interactivity, and the laws each act is read under. It links to the sections that specify each part.
