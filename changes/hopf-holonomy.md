### A loop of turnings holds its holonomy

A forms claim for 78.184 §7. A loop of turnings on two planes that share a unit, `R₁R₂R̃₁R̃₂`, does not return home: its holonomy is an exact unit motion other than the identity. Taken in the other order, the holonomy is exactly its reverse. This makes the order in which a node's planes compose physics, the Hopf bundle's holonomy, not a convention.
