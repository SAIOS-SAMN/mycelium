### "Stroke" names one thing: the reserve's per-lane motion for one act

Ruled 2026-11-13 (78.183 §7). "Stroke" named three categories: work, time and a pass of a loop. It now names only the reserve's motion for one act, a lane vector on `S:G:T:H` drawn from each lane's open share, composed by the flow law and regenerated by return along the keep. Time is the bell `h` and the orbit count `K`. A loop's pass is a cycle (court), a winding (mind) or a circuit step (carrier). `stroke_floor` becomes the park floor. 78.183's questions Q1–Q7 are answered; the build is #308. The GLOSSARY carries the definitions.
