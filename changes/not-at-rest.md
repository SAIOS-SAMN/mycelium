### A manifest entry off its rest point is refused, NotAtRest

A manifest entry that states a phase `R ≠ 0` must stand at its rest point `R = −(Z − Q)/(Z + Q)`; one off it is refused `GrantRefusal::NotAtRest` (code 13). `R = 0` stays unchecked. This holds at any spin, since `R` read as the axis plane's induced phase already carries the throat's torsion (xylarium T24). Ruled 2026-11-16 (78.184 §5, MU-EQUATIONS §6.13, #348).
