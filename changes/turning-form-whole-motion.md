### The shared turning form is the whole unit motion

78.173 §8.5, ruled 2026-11-15 for every kernel: the shared form of a turning is the whole unit motion `R`, with `K` per plane beside it. The Cayley generator is a reading where it exists, and a boost or turning split off a motion is read as a projective (unnormalised) versor. A rational unit motion's polar factors are rational only up to scale (netstratum docs/138 §5), so a turning held by its generator cannot hold the Thomas–Wigner turning of two rational boosts. `forms::turning` already works this way, so no code changes.
