### LAW-COUPLING v2 drafted: the nucleon dipole, its antipole, and the electrons' offset (78.181, #239)

This draft restates F1 and F2 of LAW-COUPLING v1 on the three operator rulings of 2026-11-11 (mycelium #239, netstratum docs/125 §4.1, xylarium #183):
- **Poles:** the poles are the nucleons'. A body's 3·protons and 3·neutrons are each read from the body, never one as the remainder of the other.
- **Axis plane:** the split stands on the axis plane of the join `S¹¹ = S¹_axis * S⁹_iso`.
- **Orientation and inversion:** each kernel's orientation is declared, and its antipole applied. The only inversion between kernels is `t ↦ 1/t`, with `t = protons/neutrons` never inverted at the seam.
- **Electrons:** R, the electrons' phase, is the axis plane's phase. The electrons offset the poles by a count `h`; the push is `π = p·(1 − h)/w`, and its antipole is `1 − π`.

It also proposes fixtures, notes where it does not yet meet #183 (the five isoclinic planes, and Xylarium's Q-face bench), and records what it replaces. Draft: not ruled, not signable.
