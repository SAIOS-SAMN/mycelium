### LAW-COUPLING v2 ruled on mycelium's side: the nucleon dipole, its antipole, and the electrons' offset (78.181, #239)

It stands on the operator rulings of 2026-11-11 (mycelium #239, netstratum docs/125 §4.1, xylarium #183):
- **Poles:** the poles are the nucleons'. A body's 3·protons and 3·neutrons are each read from the body, never one as the remainder of the other.
- **Axis plane:** the split stands on the axis plane of the join `S¹¹ = S¹_axis * S⁹_iso`.

- **Electrons:** R, the electrons' phase, is the axis plane's phase. The electrons offset the poles by their bound share `g`, the polarity equilibrium (R stays in the turning only); the push is `π = p·(1 − g)/w`, and its antipole is `1 − π`.
