### Sense 1 of retiring "frame": pole plane, pole unit, and PassageState (78.182 item 12, #239)

- **`PassageState { hold, reach }`** replaces the passage's `Frame` state in `forms::passage`.
- **`NotOnPolePlane`** replaces `PassageRefusal::NotFrame`: a passage state that is not on the pole plane's two units.
- **`pole_plane`** replaces `frame_plane` in `field::carrier::shares`: the key of plane $k$'s units $e_{+k}e_{-k}$.
- In GLOSSARY, MU-ANASTOME, and MU-CLIENT, the conformal pair's plane and units are named **pole plane** and **pole unit** (78.182 item 12).
