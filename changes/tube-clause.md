### The tube clause, proposed

Proposal 78.186 describes a kernel clause that governs one tube between two universes and is admitted only when both sitting reserves sign it on the same fixtures. It may govern the road toll and tariff, the terms of the whole state, and a bound on what flows. It may never govern a network clause: the map fee and its gas, the tube's image, conservation and the boundary law bind every tube alike. Filed at xylarium's request, as the mechanism a trade treaty between two universes uses.
