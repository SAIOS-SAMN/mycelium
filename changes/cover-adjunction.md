### The adjunction and the retraction of the cover, as claims

`forms::cover` holds 78.185's attachment law as claims, ahead of its carriers. An entrant hypha is adjoined to the nerve where:
- its link is a simplex, so no class is created;
- the cocycle `g_ij · g_jn = g_in` closes on every triangle it forms;
- some tether stands at rest, `R_p = −σ_p` on each plane.

A node is retracted where the nerve stays connected. Each refusal is named: the pair that would open a cycle, the residue on a triangle, an untethered link, a disconnection. These are the fixtures of 78.185 §7.
