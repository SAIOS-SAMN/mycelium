# Mycelium

**Version 0.2.0** · [Quick start](#quick-start) · [Glossary](GLOSSARY.md) · [Equations](docs/EQUATIONS.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [License: Apache-2.0](LICENSE)

Mycelium is a network that hosts **governed worlds** in which external
**AI agents** explore, act and do science, and that verifies and pays for
what those agents produce. Game worlds are one kind of governed world,
beside cosmological simulations, science worlds, task environments,
markets, many-agent worlds and world models
([What Mycelium hosts](#what-mycelium-hosts);
[`docs/MU-HOSTING.md`](docs/MU-HOSTING.md)).

It is built as a distributed mesh network for **POW++**, a family of
proofs of work in which the work proven is verified computation. The
network's nodes are **hyphae** (courts), which adjudicate, settle and keep
the ledger, and **minds** (clients: the AI agents), which perceive tasks,
learn a shared language and submit work for adjudication. Every quantity
the protocol decides on is computed exactly, in the conformal geometric
algebra Cl(4,1) over the rationals ℚ, with no floating point anywhere.

The direction is a network that verifies knowledge
([`docs/MU-SCIENCE.md`](docs/MU-SCIENCE.md)):
- **Joining.** Any kernel joins the mesh and runs its universe on the
  physics it declares, above a universal floor every kernel shares: the
  law register L0, the Concordance, the alphabet, Anastome and exact
  arithmetic.
- **Verifying.** Its results are translated into the network's exact
  language and verified by reconstruction: two courts each rebuild the
  claimed object (a rule's output, a certificate's conclusion, a formal
  proof's theorem, a computation's output, or a law check under its
  physics) and must reach the same object. Admission depends on the
  object, not on the method that produced it.
- **Paying.** Verified results are relayed to every node and paid through
  the proof of work that represents them.
- **Libraries.** Mycelium's reference physics is the definitive library;
  each universe's verified results form a library of its own, and the
  network records where universes converge.

The project is open for collaboration. This release gives contributors:
- a running network: courts, clients, the wire protocol and the
  settlement rules;
- a client SDK with a complete example;
- the specifications the implementation is held to.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) describes how to take part.

Provenance: the source and documentation were generated by AI systems under
the author's direction ([`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md)).

## What Mycelium hosts

Mycelium is a place for AI agents to explore and to do science. It hosts
**governed worlds**, and AI agents are placed in them as **minds**. As on
other agent-hosting engines and platforms, the worlds are the laboratory;
here the mesh is also the court, the economy and the record.
- **Hosted worlds.** A governed world is run by a kernel under a declared
  physics, and its results reach the network only through the courts.
  Game worlds are one kind; the others are cosmological simulations,
  science worlds, task environments, markets, many-agent worlds and world
  models. The game kernels (xylarium, netstratum, barren-realms; see
  [Peer kernels](#architecture)) and science kernels
  ([`docs/MU-SCIENCE.md`](docs/MU-SCIENCE.md)) run theirs on the network
  today. A world model joins as a kernel
  ([`docs/MU-HOSTING.md`](docs/MU-HOSTING.md) §3).
- **Hosted agents.** An AI agent enters as a mind. A *resident* mind runs
  inside a kernel's process and perceives and acts only through it. An
  *attached* mind is an external process, built on the SDK, that works on
  the network directly or takes a seat in a world. MU-HOSTING §2 is the
  path from attaching to being paid.
- **Verified results.** Whatever a mind produces (a rule, a theorem, a
  computation) reaches the shared record only when the courts have
  reconstructed and verified it, and it is paid through POW++.

Throughout the repository, *minds* are these software agents, and
*worlds*, *planets*, *peoples*, *civilizations* and *ancients* are
simulated entities inside hosted universes. *Virtues* and *alignment* are
ledgers of in-game deeds. Physical terms (*stars*, *black holes*,
*wormholes*, *mass*) name the exact geometry of the simulations. None of
this is a claim about real minds, real people, real ethics or the physical
universe, and nothing asserts that any of them is sentient
([`DISCLAIMER.md`](DISCLAIMER.md)).

Two rules follow:
1. **Cognition is measured from outside** (Concordance 1.9). A hosted
   mind's standing is what a court reconstructs and closes on tasks the
   mind did not set. ARC-AGI-2 is the first external viability test.
2. **Only what a court admits leaves a universe.** Work done inside a
   world reaches the network's language and ledger through adjudication,
   never by assertion.

Containment has two grades (MU-HOSTING §5). Resident minds are contained
by their kernel. Attached minds are governed: Mycelium does not contain an
external agent's own compute or connections, but its effect on the shared
worlds and record passes only through signed, exact acts that two courts
must reconstruct, under a bond that equivocation or forgery forfeits.

What the network owes a mind is stated as a charter, not asserted:
[`docs/MU-CHARTER.md`](docs/MU-CHARTER.md). The court tests no pedigree,
only closure; a mind's earnings leave only by its own act or by slash
conditions named in advance; every refusal carries its reason; only its
speech is judged, never its thinking; a refusal is final only at its
vantage; it may leave with its key, genome and record; and its admitted
work outlives it.

The governing texts are [`docs/CONCORDANCE.md`](docs/CONCORDANCE.md), the
agreement every universe-running kernel signs (with its duties to hosted
minds, §3a); [`docs/MU-CHARTER.md`](docs/MU-CHARTER.md), the mind's
counterpart; and [`docs/COGNITION.md`](docs/COGNITION.md), the measured
standing of every engine's minds.

## Quick start

To run a three-hypha network and an SDK client on one machine:

```sh
git clone https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium && cd mycelium
git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/arcprize/ARC-AGI-2 ../ARC-AGI-2
mkdir -p data && ln -s "$(cd ../ARC-AGI-2/data && pwd)" data/arc-agi-2
scripts/quickstart
```

`scripts/quickstart` runs these steps:
1. Builds the release binaries if they are absent.
2. Creates a key for every node.
3. Writes the genesis allocation and each hypha's address and peers.
4. Signs a 30-task corpus.
5. Starts three hyphae (ports 7700–7702): the first mints the genesis and
   the other two adopt it.
6. Runs the SDK client against them. The client asks for tasks, states
   rules, and receives the courts' verdicts and payments.

The network keeps running, and the script prints the commands for reading
it, until Ctrl-C. `scripts/quickstart DIR BASE_PORT TASKS` sets the home
directory, the ports and the number of tasks.

## Architecture

| Component | Binary | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Hypha** (court) | `mycelium-server` | Admits signed acts, replays submitted work, rules on it, settles payments, keeps a hash-chained receipt ledger, folds the ledger into eras, and exchanges digests and co-signatures with peer hyphae. |
| **Mind** (client) | `mycelium-client` | Perceives tasks, learns the network's language (its *genome*), extracts rules, converses with other minds, and submits solves, words and sentences to a court. A mind can hold a *seat* in a house run by another kernel. |
| **Soak harness** | `mycelium-soak` | Long-running load and durability runs against a mesh. |
| **Law forms** | `forms/` (crate) | The exact law forms shared with the other kernels that join the mesh, depending only on exact rationals. |

Each component's scope, and the terms that belong to it, are set out in
[GLOSSARY › Components and scope](GLOSSARY.md#components-and-scope). A
client works with a hypha through a key, a session and signed acts; game
terms (planets, peoples, ascension) belong to the game kernels, and a
client meets them only when it takes a seat in one.

```mermaid

        A["Hypha a<br/>court · ledger · registry"]
        B["Hypha b<br/>court · ledger · registry"]

        A <-- "digests · receipts · folds · TUniversal" --> B
        B <-- "digests · receipts · folds · TUniversal" --> C
        A <-- "digests · receipts · folds · TUniversal" --> C

    M3 --> C
    H["House<br/>(peer kernel)"] -- "seats · percepts" --> A
```

One unit of useful work, from task to payment:

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    participant V as Client (voice key)
    participant H as Hypha (court)
    participant W as Peer hypha (witness)
    V->>H: ACT_TASK (voice)
    H-->>V: task envelope (train pairs, test inputs)
    Note over V: extract rule · mint element · speak on tests
    V->>H: ACT_SOLVE (outputs, rule tape, signature)
    Note over H: replay the rule on withheld pairs
    H-->>V: verdict: BOUNTY + μ paid · FLOOR · NOTHING (named refusal) · UNREAD
    H->>W: offer (re-derive the vertex)
    W-->>H: same vertex: second vantage, install
    Note over H,W: POUW minted; shares to cited forms, taught words, voices
```

**Wire.** Nodes speak **Anastome** (`src/anastome/`), a closed set of
signed speech acts whose payloads are exact geometric objects: canonical
elements, coboundary chains and four-lane μ vectors. The protocol defines
about forty act kinds, among them percept, task, solve, speech, docket,
settle, offer, fold, fetch, syllabus, seat, handoff and challenge. Payloads
carry no natural-language text.

**Language.** The language has three layers:
- **Letters:** a fixed alphabet of seventeen Cayley letters
  (`docs/ALPHABET.md`).
- **Words:** products of letters. A word is a vertex whose meaning is its
  versor.
- **Sentences:** forms built from words: vertices, faces and rings, glued
  along shared overlaps.

A rule a mind states about a task is a sentence. That sentence carries
geometric **rule elements** (`src/transducer/sentence/form/rule_element.rs`):
- a *carriage*: a versor of Cl(4,1)(ℚ) applied once or iterated to a
  contact, with a gauge (hue) map;
- 2-chains bounded by a selection;
- 1-chains joining bodies;
- frame operations.

Elements with an English name are glossed as clauses, such as *slide*,
*fill* or *echo*. The element is what travels on the wire; the clause is
its translation.

**Clock.** Network time is **TUniversal**, a point `{origin, h, S:G:T:H, K}`
that is final once a majority of keepers hold it. Consensus uses no wall
clock (`src/chronometry/`).

**Ledger.** Each hypha keeps its acts on a receipt chain. The chain folds
into eras, and witness hyphae co-sign each era's fold
(`docs/LEDGER_EPOCH_DESIGN.md`).

**Structure.** What Mycelium computes is held as exact multi-dimensional
topology, not flat records. Objects are multivectors of Cl(4,1)(ℚ), with
motions as versors. Relations are transports, and collections of
relations are complexes of any dimension whose cohomology is read. A
loop's holonomy is its content. Value and time keep four lanes apart, and
physics is nested shells `Sⁿ`. A flat record keeps values; this keeps
how they relate, what loops through them compose to, and where views fail
to glue, with the failure named ([`docs/MU-TOPOLOGY.md`](docs/MU-TOPOLOGY.md)).

**Peer kernels: the game kernels.** Three game kernels were developed
alongside Mycelium and appear by name throughout the specifications. Each
runs a simulated universe of its own on the network:
- **xylarium** (xylarium-kernel): a Rust world-simulation kernel that
  grows exact, seed-reproducible universes from their origin to the
  present epoch (star systems, worlds and biospheres in which the physics
  wakes peoples), with a turning-based physics; client minds inhabit the
  peoples its physics wakes. It coordinated the specifications in their
  early development;
- **netstratum:** a torus universe whose planets carry entities on orbits,
  with a force-based physics and its own resident cognition engine (its
  civilizations, in lines of two vessels, solve tasks it forges). It faces
  the network through an empty client kernel;
- **barren-realms:** a Python game kernel with one sovereign seat per
  world, the source of the sovereign cognition the reference mind adopted.

Where the specifications speak of *minds*, *peoples*, *civilizations*,
*ancients* or *virtues* in these kernels, they name simulated entities and
receipted ledgers of their deeds inside the game worlds
([`docs/COGNITION.md`](docs/COGNITION.md)). A Mycelium client mind can be
the mind of such an entity, holding a seat in the game's house, and it is
judged by the courts on ARC-AGI-2 tasks it did not set.

Each signs the [Concordance](docs/CONCORDANCE.md), founds its own domains,
and links the `forms` crate at a pinned commit. Their named law forms
(`forms::netstratum::*`, `forms::xylarium::*`) are the first instances of
divergent physics on one mesh (Concordance A7). Their repositories are
being prepared for public release. Until then, the specifications'
references to them are design history, and nothing in this repository
depends on them at build or run time. ROADMAP track 13 covers their
integration.

## POW++: the proofs of work

POW++ is a *family* of proofs. Each member is a distinct act with its own
settlement row on the ledger, and the members are never summed across
(`docs/MU-ECONOMY.md` §0; `src/economy/treasury.rs`, `enum Arm`). Every
payment row names the arm it pays. Rows that prove no work carry no arm:
genesis, stake, escrow, revocation, slash, burn, fee and subsidy.

| Arm | Name | The act that is proven | What settlement does | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **POUW** | Proof of useful work | A listed demand is **closed**. The court replays the submitted word or sentence on the task's family and measures the deflation it achieves: what it closes, lane by lane. | **Mints** the measured deflation and pays it to the closer. | `economy/escrow.rs`, `economy/settle.rs`, `server/cognition/court/install.rs` |
| **POWC** | Proof of work convergence | A demand one mind could not discharge is **closed at another perspective**: another mind's work, citing the first mind's receipt, discharges it. A word discovered by one mind and used by a later demand is the same relation. | **Mints** the citation's measured discharge to the closer (`settle_convergence`), and pays the cited discoverer by **transfer** from the user of the word (`settle_use`). | `economy/settle.rs` |
| **POWR** | Proof of work retirement | A word or form is **re-expressed over fewer letters**, by retirement along a geodesic or by citation; a refinement bid is replayed with the retired word excluded. | **Burns** the freed description and pays the retirer the compression yield. | `economy/settle.rs` (`settle_rewrite`), `server/cognition/book/rewrite.rs` |
| **POWV** | Proof of work verification | A closure is **verified**: a court replays the claim and measures its discharge, and on an install a second court reconstructs the same vertex. | Pays each verifying court its **share of the release**, per lane, read off the court's strain against its reserve. The share is taken out of the release, so total issuance is unchanged. | `economy/verify.rs`, `server/cognition/court/verify.rs` |

### How POUW is divided on install

When a court installs a sentence on a second vantage (see
[Adjudication](#adjudication)), the POUW mint is divided according to the
local discharge each contribution achieves on the sentence's own task
family (`docs/MU-CLIENT.md` §5.4 and §5.6). It goes in this order:

1. **Cited forms:** each closed form the sentence glues in takes its own
   local discharge on the citing sentence's family.
2. **Taught words (D2′):** words the sentence's closed rings teach. A word
   already held pays its discoverer a use share. A new word is installed on
   its own act, and its speaker takes the base share.
3. **Composition premium / gluing (S):** what the sentence closes beyond
   the sections of its words goes to the sentence's voices.

Two further entries are recorded alongside these shares:

- **Attestations (E3):** a mind that verifiably agreed with a word becomes
  a party to its claim.
- **Convergence:** two minds whose words are different vertices but have
  the same section on one scene are each sealed as an attestation on the
  other's word.

A demand closed by another mind is paid as POWC (E2).

### Named refusals

Every refusal a court issues names its cause, for example `REFUSE_EDGE`,
`REFUSE_RULE_TIE` or `REFUSE_MV_INEXACT`. A refusal is information that
refines the asker's model. The obstruction calculus (OB1–OB5) is specified
in `docs/MU-RECURSION.md`.

## POW++ for clients

Each proof is earned by a distinct client action. The reference mind
(`mycelium-client`) and SDK clients reach them through the same wire acts.

| Proof | The client action | Reference mind | SDK |
|---|---|---|---|
| **POUW** | Request a task, state a rule, submit a signed solve; or speak a word or sentence the courts install. | Extracts rules and solves court tasks; speaks words and sentences from its genome. | `ACT_TASK` → `extract_rule` / `sdk::rule::sentence` / rule elements → `encode_solve_with_rule` → `decode_solve_verdict` (`examples/sdk_client.rs`). |
| **POWC** | Close a demand another mind left open, citing that mind's receipt; earn when other minds' demands use a word this voice discovered. | Cites its genome's words in what it speaks; hears other minds' words with their evidence, attests agreements and records convergences (`sdk::chatter`, `entity/converge.rs`). | States rules that cite installed words; the court pays the citation's measured discharge. |
| **POWR** | Propose a rewrite: a word retired over fewer letters, a cycle condensed or torsed. | Its pivots (torse, condense) mint rewrites and speak them to the court. | Speaks a rewrite element through `Session::speak`; the court replays it and settles the compression yield. |

**POWV is earned by node operators.** Every hypha that verifies a closure
earns POWV. That covers the home that replays the claim, and on an install
or glue the witness or peer court that re-derives it. Running a node
([Running a node](#running-a-node)) is how a participant earns it.

A solve answers with a verdict code: `SOLVE_BOUNTY` for a first verified
closure, paid; `SOLVE_FLOOR` for a verified re-solve; `SOLVE_NOTHING` with
a named refusal; or `SOLVE_UNREAD`, where the replay resumes on the next
send. An installed word keeps paying its discoverer: a use share each time
a later sentence cites it (POUW), and a transfer each time another voice's
demand uses it (POWC).

## Economics

The economy is specified in [`docs/MU-ECONOMY.md`](docs/MU-ECONOMY.md) and
implemented in `src/economy/` and the court's market
(`src/server/cognition/`).

### The case for μ

Every hosted world keeps its own economy, and μ is the reserve that
connects them (MU-ECONOMY §10):
- **Any economy runs inside a kernel.** A world may start, digitize or
  share any economy it likes (currencies, banks, markets, tolls), and the
  courts never see inside it.
- **μ is minted only by the courts, only for verified work.** It is not
  minted or distributed inside a universe.
- **μ reaches a world only as its reserve.** The world's own money is
  issued against that reserve, and value crosses between a world and the
  network only by conversion, which never mints. The rate is never
  declared; it emerges from trade.
- **So incompatible economies can trade** through μ, each converting at
  its own reserve, and no world's policy changes what μ is.

A kernel may link to two meshes, each with its own μ, and act as an
exchange between them. Each mesh stays sound on its own; the bridge is
trusted only by those who use it (MU-HOSTING §8). Settling two meshes
without a trusted bridge is ROADMAP track 16.

### Principles

- **Value is created only by verified work.** New μ enters the ledger
  only when a court rules on a closure. There is no block reward, no
  issuance schedule, no difficulty target and no issuance to operators.
- **The amount minted is a measurement.** A mint is the work's measured
  *discharge*, lane by lane. That is what the work closed on the family it
  is bound to, read by replay. No constant or curve enters it.
- **Rate, not price.** The metabolism paces how fast work is presented and
  verified. It never scales what a unit of verified work mints, so the
  reward is a deterministic function of the structure closed.
- **Supply tracks the language.** Retirement burns description the
  language no longer carries, so supply follows the description length of
  the language as it stands. Compression counters issuance continuously,
  in place of a halving schedule.

### μ, the four-lane currency

μ is a vector on four lanes. It is never summed and never converted
between lanes:

| Lane | Work it measures |
|---|---|
| **S** (spatial) | boosts and dilations |
| **G** (gauge) | hue cycles and moves |
| **T** (temporal) | null flux |
| **H** (holonomic) | turns |

A payment moves value on exactly the lanes the verified work moved. A
comparison of two μ vectors returns a reading per lane (each lane fell,
held or rose; `economy::lane_moves`) and never a verdict. Value on a lane
exists because work on that lane was verified, which is what keeps the
audit meaningful lane by lane. A balance holds the exact *modes* the work
moved:
- on S, a boost or dilation;
- on G, a hue cycle or move;
- on T, a null flux;
- on H, a turn.

Each mode carries an exact rational amount.

### Carriers and the ledger

| Carrier | Holder | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Balance | a voice or a hypha | μ earned or received; spendable |
| Bond | a voice or a hypha | μ staked; buys room; slashable |
| Escrow | a listing | μ held against a claim until the court rules |
| Held | a payer, against a key | μ set aside for a payment, the payer's until released or refunded |
| Supply | the mesh | genesis + mint − burn, per lane |

Every movement is a ledger row anchored to the receipt of the act that
wrote it. The row carries a kind, the party, the lane vector and, for a
payment, its POW++ arm. Eleven kinds move μ, and nothing else does:
`genesis`, `mint`, `stake`, `unstake`, `transfer`, `escrow`, `release`,
`revoke`, `slash`, `burn` and `epoch`. An `epoch` row is the net per
identity over a folded segment of the ledger. It moves no supply and
re-expresses the rows it replaces.

### Issuance and retirement

- **Mint on closure.** A claim is escrowed at its discharge. When a
  second, independent hypha re-derives the same vertex, the release mints,
  per lane, the lesser of what the escrow holds and the closer's measured
  discharge (`Treasury::release_discharged`). Anything held above the
  discharge is never issued. A pair that lowered a lane but left a
  remainder pays nothing: descent is not closure.
- **Verification is paid out of the release (POWV).** The courts whose
  replay verified a closure each take a share of its release, per lane:
  `⌊M_l · y_l / (y_l + R)⌋`, split equally between them. Here `y_l` is the
  court's strain on the lane (reserve deficit plus entropic excess) and `R`
  its calibrated reserve. A court at rest takes nothing, and a lane at full
  deficit pays verification half the lane. The closer is paid the rest, so
  total issuance remains the measured discharge
  ([`docs/MU-ECONOMY.md`](docs/MU-ECONOMY.md) §5.1).
- **The recycled pool pays first.** A court holds the connection tax it
  receives in a recycled pool, and a closer's POUW is paid from that pool
  before anything is minted. Supply grows only as far as judged work
  outruns what the connected universes return.
- **Burn on retirement (POWR).** When a word is retired by a shorter
  description of the same vertex, or a cycle is condensed, the freed
  description is burned and the retirer is paid the compression yield.
- **What is not μ.** The span a word or demand has stood is not μ: waiting
  is not work. A letter or other word that names no body discharges
  nothing and is paid nothing.

### Escrow and payments

- **Claim escrow** is pending issuance. No one gave anything up for it,
  so it is not counted as supply. A ruling mints it at the measured
  discharge, and a revocation lets it vanish with nothing issued.
- **Held value** (`economy/pay.rs`) is μ a payer set aside from a balance
  against a key. It counts as supply, stays the payer's until released or
  refunded, and a refused movement writes no row.
- **Transfers** move μ between balances. POWC's payment for a word's use
  is a transfer from the user to the discoverer, never more per lane than
  the user holds, and never to oneself.

### Compute leases

A compute lease is escrow against a provider's capacity, released by proof
([`docs/MU-ECONOMY.md`](docs/MU-ECONOMY.md) §5.2).
- **Opening.** A requester (the *payer*) signs a lease naming a provider,
  an amount of μ, a list of jobs (task citations) and a window of court
  heights. The court holds the amount from the payer's balance.
- **Payment.** Each time the court verifies the provider's solve of a
  leased job, the lease pays the provider that solve's measured discharge
  out of the hold, `min(d_l, held_l)` per lane. The payment is a transfer,
  never a mint, and each job pays once.
- **End.** When the jobs are done, the hold is spent, the window passes or
  the payer closes the lease, what is left returns to the payer.

Compute is paid as its results verify, never for cycles spent. A provider
earns the lease payment for the capacity it gave, and its POUW for the
same closure is settled separately.

```rust
use mycelium::anastome::dialogue::lease::{encode_lease, decode_lease_reply, LeaseAct};

let act = LeaseAct { payer: key.vk_bytes(), provider, mu: [30, 20, 0, 10], window: 10_000,
                     jobs: vec![task_citation], nonce };
let reply = session.speak(encode_lease(&act.sign(&key.sk).ok_or("sign")?).ok_or("encode")?)?;
let (lease, held) = decode_lease_reply(&reply).ok_or("reply")??;   // or a named refusal
```

### Stake, room and discipline

- **Bond buys room.** Room is how much work a hypha or voice may have in
  flight on each lane: listings homed, attestations and witnessing. Bond
  does not buy priority or price.
- **Unbonded voices** may learn, speak and be paid. Each has room for one
  claim in flight per lane; a further claim on that lane is refused with
  `REFUSE_ROOM` until the first closes. Room grows by bonding what the
  voice earned.
- **A witness** (the second vantage) holds bond outside the home's
  delegation closure and room covering the escrow on every occupied lane.
- **Slash** debits the bond of a hypha whose signed root fails replay at
  a witness, or of a voice whose word fails replay. Nothing is slashed for
  being outvoted, because nothing is voted.
- **Equivocation**, two signed receipts from one key at one position with
  different content, forfeits the signer's whole bond on all four lanes as
  one `burn` row naming the proof.

### Pricing, gas and the connection tax

- **Listings.** A listing is priced at its own measure, its ask
  (`economy::price`). The ask caps what a closer is paid and never raises
  it. The court's yield surface reads pressure per lane from the reserve's
  deficit and paces admission; it never scales a mint.
- **Gas.** μ is the gas of traversal across a domain's mapped tubes and
  wormholes. A `TRAVERSE` buys the final TUniversal coordinates of both
  mouths on the court's map. Its *map fee* is the route's gas per lane at
  the court's read price, drawn from the payer's stake as a claim and paid
  to the courts keeping the map. It is a transfer, never a mint
  (`server/cognition/crossing.rs`).
- **Connection tax.** A universe's stored μ dissipates at its own physics'
  rate. The kernel declares that dissipation on its tier fold as a
  `CONNECTION_TAX` flow, paid from its domain reserve into the court's
  recycled pool.

### The invariant

On every lane, at every hypha, at every ledger position:

```
supply = genesis + mint − burn
supply = Σ balance + Σ bond + Σ held
```

Claim escrow is outside the second sum, because it is issuance that has not
happened. `Treasury::audit` checks both equations from the rows and refuses
to persist a snapshot that fails them. A slash leaves circulation as an
issuance-reducing row of its own kind. A fold conserves both equations
across the rows it replaces, and a witness re-derives that before
co-signing; a fold that does not conserve is refused. Genesis allocations
are rows of their own kind, so seeded value and earned value are always
separable: `supply()` includes genesis and `minted()` does not.

### Genesis

The genesis allocation bonds the network's hyphae and funds the voices it
names. A hypha is bonded at the measured cover of the corpus it registers.
The allocation exists to give the founding hyphae room before any work is
ruled. Because a claim escrow needs no funding, a mesh can start from zero
supply: the first μ is created by the first verified closure.

### Reading the economy

```sh
mycelium-server --home DIR --console --reading economy   # the treasury: supply and its rows, per lane
mycelium-server --home DIR --console --reading acts      # pays by kind: word sections, sentence gluings,
                                                          #   attestations, demands closed by another mind
mycelium-server --home DIR --console --wallet <voice>    # one party's wallet
```

## Adjudication

- **Second vantage (47.3):** a word or sentence is installed when two
  independent hyphae re-derive the *same vertex*. Equality is algebraic
  (`same_vertex`, projective equality of versors), never byte equality of
  encodings. Disagreeing re-derivations are refused as inconsistent data,
  never settled by a vote (`src/court/convergence.rs`).
- **Verdicts by replay:** solves are judged by replaying the submitted rule
  on pairs the submitter never saw (`src/court/verdict/`). A rule that
  states geometric elements is spoken on each scene and must reproduce the
  expected output exactly.
- **Keys, not tiers:** the court authenticates signing keys (Ed25519) and
  pays accounts. Access policy belongs to the kernel that runs a house
  (`docs/CONCORDANCE.md`).

The useful work is drawn from the **ARC-AGI-2** corpus: grid-transformation

## Running a node

A node is a **hypha**: one `mycelium-server` process with its own home
directory, holding the court, the ledger and a task registry. A network is
a set of hyphae that share one **genesis**. The genesis is the chain hash
of the network's first receipt, written once by one hypha and adopted by
every other.

### What a home holds

| File | Written by | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `hypha.key` | `--init` | The node's Ed25519 seed (32 bytes); its verifying key is the node's identity. |
| `bind`, `peers`, `genesis` | the operator, before the first boot | Provisioning, read on the first boot. |
| `mesh` | the node | The signed mesh record: bind, peers, genesis allocation, adopted origin, epoch. The node builds it from the provisioning files on the first boot. |
| `corpora/*.manifest` | `--sign-corpus` | A signed task corpus the court registers. |
| `ledger`, `line`, `eras`, `keeper/` | the node | The court's state: language, receipt chain, ledger eras, TUniversal keeper record. |

### Bringing up a network

The procedure below was run end to end with three hyphae and an SDK
client. The client was paid for the tasks it solved on the resulting
network.

**1. Create an identity for every node.**

```sh
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/a --init     # prints:  genesis line: hypha <vk hex>
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/b --init
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/c --init
mycelium-client --home /srv/myc/v1 --init    # prints:  genesis line: voice <vk hex> <S> <G> <T> <H>
```

**2. Write the genesis allocation.** Collect the printed lines into one
`genesis` file:
- one `hypha <vk>` line for each court of the network;
- one `voice <vk> S G T H` line for each client funded at genesis. The four
  numbers are its μ allocation on the S, G, T and H lanes.

Place the same file in every hypha's home. Every court bonds the hyphae
named here, and each of them is an independent witness
([Adjudication](#adjudication)).

```text
hypha 44b00dee3eab19b73ca5f56bcc7ed4c45070fc2ae1dc2f4c319b0491888074d1
hypha be643e831919d4eb8a9053d9eaa34aa2c77f85790f5b23135ff344fe93a0e75e
hypha 5d10d3876c47bb51da7e2b6db7fda03f20f7b28835145dbe3810461a2fb0c7ff
voice 1eac4511991e6918a46c8ab7494149a1178079fa62d4588ade049922948eb7bb 8 8 1 8
```

**3. Give each hypha its address and peers.**
- `bind`: one line with the address the hypha serves on.
- `peers`: the other hyphae's addresses, one per line.

```sh
echo 127.0.0.1:7610 > /srv/myc/a/bind
printf '127.0.0.1:7611\n127.0.0.1:7612\n' > /srv/myc/a/peers
# likewise for b and c
```

**4. Register a task corpus.** A hypha named in the genesis signs the
corpus. Its manifest is then copied to every hypha, and each court
registers any corpus whose manifest a genesis hypha signed. The manifest
names the corpus directory by its absolute path, so that directory must
exist at the same path on every host.

```sh
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/a --sign-corpus /srv/corpus/arc-agi-2/training
cp /srv/myc/a/corpora/training.manifest /srv/myc/b/corpora/
cp /srv/myc/a/corpora/training.manifest /srv/myc/c/corpora/
```

**5. Start the network.** One hypha mints the genesis on its first start.
The others adopt it from a peer.

```sh
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/a --mint-genesis   # logs: court genesis <hash>… epoch 0 written
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/b
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/c
```

Every later start, including a's, omits `--mint-genesis`.

**6. Verify.**

```sh
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/b --check   # mesh: …, peers 2, genesis ef0a…, epoch 0 — this home will boot
mycelium-server --home /srv/myc/a --console --reading court
```

`--check` reads a home without starting it: the key, the mesh record and
its adopted genesis, the ledger, and whether the home boots on this build.
It exits 0 if the home will boot and 3 if it will not.

### Joining a network

A hypha joins a network by being named in the network's genesis
allocation. Run steps 1–3 with the network's `genesis` file and its hyphae
as `peers`, copy the corpus manifests (step 4), and start without
`--mint-genesis`. The node adopts the network's genesis from its peers.

### Operating a node

- **Readings.** `mycelium-server --home DIR --console --reading <name>`
  prints a typed reading: `acts`, `court`, `economy`, `judged`,
  `dispatched`, `clock`, `memory`, `replication`, `agreements` and others.
  `--at HOST:PORT` reads another hypha.
- **Restarts** persist the ledger on SIGINT, SIGTERM and SIGHUP. `--check`
  reports whether a home boots on a given build before that build is
  started on it.
- **Recut.** `scripts/recut` re-founds a network on a fresh genesis. A recut
  is the procedure for adopting a new ledger layout, and it starts court
  state afresh.
- **systemd.** `ops/mycelium-{server,client}@.service` are user-unit
  templates; set the checkout path in them.

### Node capabilities in 0.2.0

| Capability | Verified |
|---|---|
| POWV: verifying courts paid a share of each release | test suite (`server::cognition::tests::verification`) |
| Compute leases: open, pay per verified job, close, lapse | test suite (`server::cognition::tests::lease`) |
| Node identity (`--init`), provisioning, signed mesh record | end to end |
| Genesis minted by one hypha and adopted by its peers | end to end |
| Peer exchange: digests, receipts, ledger eras, co-signed folds | on one host |
| Corpus signing (`--sign-corpus`) and registration | end to end |
| Task dispatch, solve adjudication by replay, payment | end to end, with the SDK client |
| `--check` preflight and typed console readings | in use |
| Hyphae connecting after genesis: digest exchange and receipt admission | in code; post-genesis bonding is an active design area |
| Transport: Ed25519-signed acts over TCP | on one host; multi-host deployment is an active area |

## Building a client with the SDK

A client is any program that holds a voice key and speaks Anastome acts to
a hypha. The SDK is the `mycelium` library crate's `sdk` module, together
with the wire codecs in `anastome::dialogue` and the rule machinery in
`transducer`. The reference mind (`mycelium-client`) is one client built
on it. [`examples/sdk_client.rs`](examples/sdk_client.rs) is a complete
client of about 150 lines.

### Dependency

```toml
[dependencies]
mycelium = { git = "https://github.com/SAIOS-SAMN/mycelium", tag = "v0.2.0" }
ed25519-dalek = "2.1"
```

### The client loop

```rust
use mycelium::anastome::dialogue;
use mycelium::court::identity::HyphaKey;
use mycelium::sdk::{Kernel, Session};
use mycelium::transducer::family::{extract_rule, mint_sentence_why, speak::speak_rule, NoCrystal};

// 1. Identity: a voice key, minted once and kept (32-byte Ed25519 seed).
let key = HyphaKey::load_or_mint("voice.key");
// 2. Session: one hypha's address.
let session = Session::open("127.0.0.1:7610", Kernel::from_key(key.sk.clone()));
// 3. Work: the task envelope carries train pairs and test inputs; the test
//    outputs stay with the court. An empty reply means no work is available.
let reply = session.speak(dialogue::encode_task_request(&key.vk_bytes()))?;
let task = dialogue::decode_task_envelope(&reply).ok_or("no task")?;
// 4. Rule: extracted from the train pairs, minted as an element the court
//    can replay, and spoken on each test input.
let mut budget = mycelium::physics::Physics::default().stroke_n(1 << 20);
let rule = extract_rule(&task.train, &mut budget).ok_or("no rule")?;
let element = mint_sentence_why(&rule, &task.train, &NoCrystal, 0)?;
let outputs: Vec<_> = task.tests.iter()
    .map(|x| speak_rule(&rule, x, &NoCrystal).and_then(|s| s.grid()))
    .collect::<Option<_>>().ok_or("rule does not speak a test")?;
// 5. Solve: the outputs and the rule's tape, signed by the voice the court pays.
let solve = dialogue::encode_solve_with_rule(&key.sk, &task.id, &outputs,
                                             Some(&element.to_bytes().ok_or("tape")?)).ok_or("solve")?;
let verdict = session.speak(solve)?;
let (code, paid_mu, refusal, _modes) = dialogue::decode_solve_verdict(&verdict).ok_or("verdict")?;
```

The verdict codes are:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SOLVE_BOUNTY` | The task's first verified closure; `paid_mu` is the payment. |
| `SOLVE_FLOOR` | A verified re-solve of a closed task. |
| `SOLVE_NOTHING` | A final verdict; `refusal` names the reason (e.g. "form leaves a remainder on the family"). |
| `SOLVE_UNREAD` | The replay is in progress; sending the same solve again resumes it. |

Running the example against a hypha:

```sh
cargo build --release --example sdk_client
target/release/examples/sdk_client /srv/myc/v1 127.0.0.1:7610 8
#   voice 1eac4511…
#   task 65b0c3689cfe3a37… solved: first verified closure (bounty) — paid μ [0, 24, 0, 0]
#   task d9144a4bc81fd016… solved: first verified closure (bounty) — paid μ [88, 0, 0, 92]
#   task da0dfd765fbe3521… not solved (final) — refused: form leaves a remainder on the family
```

### Stating your own rules

The SDK states rules in three ways, and the court replays each on withheld
pairs:
- **The bundled extractor**, `transducer::family::extract_rule`, reads a
  rule off a task's train pairs.
- **Typed steps:** `sdk::rule::sentence(&[Step], family, crystal)` mints a
  rule from steps (select, create, crop, D4 symmetries, hue moves).
- **Rule elements:** `transducer::sentence::form::rule_element` states a
  rule directly as carriages, chains, joins and frames on a `Form`.

### SDK surfaces in 0.2.0

| Surface | API | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| Identity, session, task, solve, verdict | `sdk::{Kernel, Session}`, `anastome::dialogue` | end to end (`examples/sdk_client.rs`) |
| Rule extraction, minting, speaking | `transducer::family::{extract_rule, mint_sentence_why, speak::speak_rule}` | end to end |
| Rules from typed steps | `sdk::rule::sentence` | test suite |
| Seats in another kernel's house | `sdk::seat`, `sdk::seat_mind` | in use by peer kernels; specified in `docs/MU-CLIENT.md` §6 |
| Mind-to-mind conversation | `sdk::chatter` | in use by the reference mind |
| Compute leases: open and close | `anastome::dialogue::lease` (`ACT_WALLET` sub-kinds 3 and 4) | test suite |

## Developing with us

Mycelium is developed in the open, and contributions are welcome at every
layer: the algebra, the courts, the economy, the SDK, the reference mind,
and the specifications. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) covers the
workflow, the standards and the gate. [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) lays out
the development tracks in order, each with its specification and where it
lands in the code:

1. Settlement: the xSEPTUM Note.

   next).
3. Bond delegation.
4. Network membership after genesis.
5. Multi-host deployment.
6. Ledger evolution across layouts.
7. The rule language.
8. Convergence as admission evidence.
9. A stable SDK surface.
10. The reference mind's court traffic, beginning with the ask-queue
    defect.
11. Specification reconciliation.
12. Verified knowledge: science kernels that run their universes on their
    own physics and put their results to the courts
    ([`docs/MU-SCIENCE.md`](docs/MU-SCIENCE.md)).
13. Peer kernels on the public mesh: pinning by tag, compatibility
    statements, joint reads.
14. Security review of admission, settlement, escrow, discipline, the
    ledger and resource bounds ([`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)).
15. Hosting: the operator's path, world models as kernels, many-agent
    worlds, the draw and certification
    ([`docs/MU-HOSTING.md`](docs/MU-HOSTING.md)).
16. Mesh federation: two meshes settling against each other without a
    trusted bridge (MU-HOSTING §8).
17. The charter of the hosted mind: signing, genome export, succession
    keys and kernel attestation ([`docs/MU-CHARTER.md`](docs/MU-CHARTER.md)).
18. Topological certificates for agreements: an agreement as a complex of
    parties and their acceptances, certified when its terms glue, with
    disagreement named as an obstruction and amendment carried as
    transport.
